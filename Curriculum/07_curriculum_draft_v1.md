# M&A Leadership Institute: Executive Course Curriculum
## Draft v1 -- "How to Think About Acquisitions Successfully"

**Duration:** 5 Days (expandable to 3-day intensive)
**Target:** Corporate Business Development / M&A Teams (7-10 members per cohort company)
**Cadence:** 3 cohort sessions per year, depth increasing over time
**Credential:** Certificate from IIT Roorkee + M&A Leadership Platform

---

## Day 1: Strategic Lens -- Why Acquire?

### Theme: From Opportunistic to Strategic M&A

**Morning Session (9:00 - 12:30)**
- **Module 1.1:** The M&A Landscape in India's Mid-Market
  - Market data: $113B M&A market, 115K+ mid-market enterprises
  - Why 70-90% of deals fail to deliver value
  - The 5 gaps: Strategy, Capability, Sourcing, Execution, Regulatory
- **Module 1.2:** M&A as a Strategic Capability (not an event)
  - Framework: When to buy vs. build vs. partner
  - Aligning M&A strategy with corporate strategy
  - The "Programmatic Acquirer" advantage (8.5% TSR vs. 3.7%)

**Afternoon Session (14:00 - 17:30)**
- **Module 1.3:** Workshop -- Map Your M&A Strategy
  - Participants map their company's growth ambitions to M&A opportunities
  - Sector-specific opportunity scanning exercise
  - *Deliverable: Personal M&A Strategy Canvas*
- **Module 1.4:** Case Study -- Successful vs. Failed Indian Mid-Market Acquisitions
  - 2 live cases: Manipal-Sahyadri ($700M) vs. a failed integration
  - Group discussion: What did the winners do differently?

**Aha Moment:** The "70% failure" statistic becomes personal when participants map their own company's past deals against the failure patterns.

**Tangible Takeaway:** M&A Strategy Canvas (reusable framework)

---

## Day 2: Finding the Right Target

### Theme: Building a Proprietary Deal Pipeline

**Morning Session (9:00 - 12:30)**
- **Module 2.1:** Target Screening Frameworks
  - Strategic fit assessment matrices
  - Financial screening criteria for mid-market
  - Sector-specific screening (Healthcare, Renewables, EV, Tech)
- **Module 2.2:** Intelligence & Sourcing Methods
  - Building proprietary deal pipelines (not waiting for bankers)
  - Using data platforms, industry networks, and relationship mapping
  - *Platform Demo: Target Sourcing Dashboard (live walkthrough)*

**Afternoon Session (14:00 - 17:30)**
- **Module 2.3:** Valuation Essentials for Corp Dev Teams
  - EV/EBITDA benchmarks by sector (India-specific)
  - Revenue multiples, DCF shortcuts, and comparable analysis
  - When valuation gaps kill deals (and how to bridge them)
- **Module 2.4:** Workshop -- Build Your Target Shortlist
  - Participants work with real sector data to build a target shortlist
  - Scoring and ranking exercise using the screening framework
  - *Deliverable: Target Screening Scorecard (template)*

**Aha Moment:** Participants realize they've been dependent on bankers for deal flow and learn to build their own pipeline.

**Tangible Takeaway:** Target Screening Scorecard + Pipeline Building Playbook

---

## Day 3: Due Diligence & Deal Structuring

### Theme: Seeing What Others Miss

**Morning Session (9:00 - 12:30)**
- **Module 3.1:** Due Diligence That Actually Works
  - Beyond financial DD: operational, cultural, technology, regulatory
  - The India-specific DD checklist (promoter risks, related party transactions, compliance)
  - Red flags that experienced acquirers catch (and novices miss)
- **Module 3.2:** Culture & Talent Assessment
  - Why 68% cite culture as biggest M&A challenge
  - Culture compatibility diagnostic tools
  - Only 30% of Indian executives assess culture before diligence (vs. 44% globally)

**Afternoon Session (14:00 - 17:30)**
- **Module 3.3:** Deal Structuring for Mid-Market India
  - Equity vs. asset deals, earnouts, deferred consideration
  - Regulatory considerations: CCI thresholds, FEMA, MCA
  - Tax-efficient structures for Indian mid-market
- **Module 3.4:** Simulation -- The Deal Room
  - 🔥 **War Room Exercise:** Teams negotiate a mock acquisition
  - Buyer vs. Seller teams with competing information
  - Real-time negotiation with curveballs
  - *Deliverable: Deal Term Sheet (negotiated in simulation)*

**Aha Moment:** The Deal Room simulation -- participants experience the pressure, information asymmetry, and negotiation dynamics of a real deal.

**Tangible Takeaway:** DD Checklist (India Mid-Market) + Deal Structuring Framework

---

## Day 4: Post-Merger Integration -- Where Value Lives or Dies

### Theme: The First 100 Days (and Beyond)

**Morning Session (9:00 - 12:30)**
- **Module 4.1:** The Integration Imperative
  - Why 83% of deals fail at integration (not at deal-making)
  - Day 0 vs. Day 1 vs. Day 100 planning
  - The Integration Management Office (IMO) playbook
- **Module 4.2:** Synergy Tracking & Value Protection
  - Revenue synergies vs. cost synergies: realistic planning
  - Synergy dashboards and milestone tracking
  - 92% success rate when synergies tracked from Day 1
  - *Platform Demo: Integration Monitoring Dashboard*

**Afternoon Session (14:00 - 17:30)**
- **Module 4.3:** People & Culture Integration
  - Managing 47% Year 1 turnover risk
  - Retention strategies for key talent
  - Communication playbooks for Day 1 announcements
- **Module 4.4:** Workshop -- Build Your PMI Playbook
  - Each team builds a 100-day integration plan for a real or hypothetical deal
  - Using the synergy dashboard template
  - Peer review across teams
  - *Deliverable: 100-Day Integration Playbook (customizable template)*

**Aha Moment:** Participants build an actual integration plan they can take back and use on their next deal.

**Tangible Takeaway:** 100-Day PMI Playbook + Synergy Tracking Dashboard Template

---

## Day 5: Governance, Policy & Building a Repeatable M&A Machine

### Theme: From One Deal to an M&A Capability

**Morning Session (9:00 - 12:30)**
- **Module 5.1:** M&A Governance Frameworks
  - Board-level M&A governance structures
  - Investment committee design and decision frameworks
  - Deal approval workflows for mid-market companies
- **Module 5.2:** Regulatory Navigation
  - CCI merger control (new DVT thresholds, timelines)
  - SEBI takeover code essentials
  - FEMA compliance for cross-border
  - Guest Speaker: Regulatory expert / former CCI member

**Afternoon Session (14:00 - 17:30)**
- **Module 5.3:** Building a Repeatable M&A Capability
  - From "one deal" to "programmatic acquirer"
  - Lessons library: capturing and reusing deal knowledge
  - Building the internal Corp Dev team of the future
- **Module 5.4:** Capstone Presentation & Certification
  - Each team presents their M&A Capability Roadmap
  - Peer and faculty feedback
  - Certificate ceremony
  - *Deliverable: Company-specific M&A Capability Roadmap*

**Aha Moment:** The shift from "we did a deal" to "we have an M&A capability" clicks when participants see their own roadmap.

**Tangible Takeaway:** M&A Capability Roadmap + Full Toolkit Portfolio
