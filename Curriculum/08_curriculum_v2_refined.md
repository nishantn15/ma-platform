## Day 1: The Strategic Lens
### "Why Acquire? From Opportunistic to Strategic M&A"

**Faculty:** 1 Academic (Strategy/Finance) + 1 Practitioner (Former Corp Dev Head)

| Time | Session | Method |
|------|---------|--------|
| 09:00-10:30 | **The M&A Inflection Point** -- India's mid-market landscape, $113B market, why 70-90% fail, the 5 gaps | Lecture + data walk |
| 10:45-12:30 | **Case: The Programmatic Acquirer** -- How Danaher/Aditya Birla built repeatable M&A machines vs. one-off acquirers (8.5% vs 3.7% TSR) | HBS-style case discussion |
| 14:00-15:30 | **Framework: M&A Strategy Canvas** -- Map corporate strategy → growth gaps → M&A opportunities → target criteria | Workshop (individual + group) |
| 15:45-17:30 | **The Week-Long Deal Simulation: BRIEFING** -- Teams assigned to a simulated Indian mid-market acquisition. Buyer/Seller roles. Background packs distributed. | Stanford-style simulation kickoff |

**Aha Moment:** When participants map their own company's growth gaps and realize M&A is not a "nice to have" but a strategic imperative -- and they've been doing it wrong.

**Toolkit #1:** M&A Strategy Canvas (reusable framework with scoring rubric)

## Day 2: Finding & Valuing the Right Target
### "Building Proprietary Deal Flow -- Not Waiting for Bankers"

**Faculty:** 1 Academic (Valuation/Finance) + 1 Practitioner (Investment Banker / PE Partner)

| Time | Session | Method |
|------|---------|--------|
| 09:00-10:30 | **Target Screening Frameworks** -- Strategic fit matrices, financial screens, sector-specific criteria (Healthcare/Renewables/EV/Tech). How KKR/Carlyle source in India's mid-market. | Lecture + framework |
| 10:45-12:30 | **Case: The Hidden Target** -- Real Indian mid-market case where target was found through non-traditional sourcing (network, platform intelligence vs. banker) | Case discussion + group exercise |
| 14:00-15:00 | **Valuation Essentials** -- EV/EBITDA benchmarks by Indian sector, DCF shortcuts, comparable analysis, when valuation gaps kill deals. Not a finance course -- a decision framework. | Lecture + worked examples |
| 15:15-16:15 | **Platform Demo: Target Sourcing Dashboard** -- Live walkthrough of the platform's target intelligence module. How daily monitoring, weekly refreshes, and Top Targets Dashboard work. | Live demo + hands-on |
| 16:30-17:30 | **Deal Simulation: TARGET SCREENING** -- Teams apply Day 2 frameworks to screen and shortlist targets for their simulated deal. Present rationale. | Simulation checkpoint #1 |

**Aha Moment:** When participants realize they've been 100% dependent on bankers for deal flow -- and learn they can build their own proprietary pipeline.

**Toolkit #2:** Target Screening Scorecard (weighted scoring template)
**Toolkit #3:** Pipeline Building Playbook (sourcing channels + tracking system)

## Day 3: Due Diligence & The Deal Room
### "Seeing What Others Miss -- Then Negotiating Like You Mean It"

**Faculty:** 1 Academic (Law/Regulatory) + 1 Practitioner (M&A Lawyer / DD Expert)
**Guest Speaker:** Regulatory expert (former CCI / SEBI practitioner)

| Time | Session | Method |
|------|---------|--------|
| 09:00-10:30 | **Due Diligence That Actually Works** -- Beyond financial DD. Operational, cultural, technology, regulatory. India-specific: promoter risks, related party transactions, compliance gaps. Red flags experienced acquirers catch. | Lecture + checklist walkthrough |
| 10:45-12:00 | **Culture & Talent: The Silent Deal Killer** -- 68% cite culture as #1 challenge. Only 30% of Indian execs assess culture pre-diligence (vs 44% global). Culture compatibility diagnostic tools. | Workshop + diagnostic tool |
| 12:00-12:30 | **Regulatory Navigation** -- CCI DVT thresholds (INR 2,000 Cr), SEBI takeover code, FEMA cross-border, MCA compliance. What mid-market teams must know. | Guest speaker / Q&A |
| 14:00-15:30 | **Deal Structuring for Indian Mid-Market** -- Equity vs. asset deals, earnouts, deferred consideration, tax-efficient structures. Promoter-led deal dynamics unique to India. | Lecture + worked examples |
| 15:45-17:30 | **THE DEAL ROOM (War Room Simulation)** -- Teams negotiate their simulated acquisition. Buyer vs. Seller with competing information. Real-time curveballs: regulatory surprise, talent flight risk, hidden liability discovery. Negotiation under pressure. | Harvard Negotiation Practicum style |

**Aha Moment:** The Deal Room. Participants experience the pressure, information asymmetry, and negotiation dynamics of a real deal. Curveballs force real-time decision-making. This is the session they'll remember.

**Toolkit #4:** DD Checklist -- India Mid-Market (comprehensive, sector-customizable)
**Toolkit #5:** Deal Structuring Framework (decision tree for Indian mid-market)

## Day 4: Post-Merger Integration -- Where Value Lives or Dies
### "The First 100 Days: From Closing Champagne to Integration Reality"

**Faculty:** 1 Academic (Operations/OB) + 1 Practitioner (PMI Specialist / Former Integration Leader)
**Guest Speaker:** CXO who led a successful (and a failed) integration

| Time | Session | Method |
|------|---------|--------|
| 09:00-10:00 | **The Integration Imperative** -- 83% fail at integration, not deal-making. Day 0 vs Day 1 vs Day 100. The Integration Management Office (IMO) playbook. What winners do: 92% success when synergies tracked from Day 1. | Lecture + data |
| 10:15-11:30 | **Case: Two Integrations** -- Guest CXO shares two real stories: one that created massive value, one that destroyed it. Same company, different approaches. Q&A. | Guest speaker + discussion |
| 11:45-12:30 | **Platform Demo: Integration Monitoring Dashboard** -- Synergy tracking, milestone monitoring, risk heat maps, stakeholder communication tools. How the platform provides real-time visibility. | Live demo + hands-on |
| 14:00-15:30 | **People & Culture Integration** -- Managing 47% Year 1 turnover risk. Retention strategies. Day 1 communication playbooks. How to keep the lights on while changing everything. | Workshop + templates |
| 15:45-17:30 | **BUILD YOUR PMI PLAYBOOK** -- Each team builds a 100-day integration plan for their simulated deal. Synergy targets, milestone map, risk register, communication plan. Peer review across teams. | Chicago Booth playbook-building exercise |

**Aha Moment:** When participants hear the CXO guest share "we lost Rs.50 Cr of value because we didn't have this playbook" -- and they're building theirs right now.

**Toolkit #6:** 100-Day PMI Playbook (customizable template with timelines)
**Toolkit #7:** Synergy Tracking Dashboard (spreadsheet + platform integration)

## Credentialing Architecture

### Tier 1: Certificate of Completion
- **Requirement:** Complete 5-day intensive
- **Issued by:** M&A Leadership Institute (IIT Roorkee co-branded)
- **Value:** Participation credential (comparable to IIM cert)

### Tier 2: Certified M&A Strategy & Integration Professional (CMASIP)
- **Requirements:**
  1. Complete 5-day intensive
  2. Participate in all 3 coaching sessions (12-month engagement)
  3. Submit company-specific M&A Capability Roadmap (assessed)
  4. Document at least 1 deal involvement during the year
- **Issued by:** M&A Leadership Institute + IIT Roorkee
- **Value:** Practitioner credential with real-deal requirement (modeled on CM&AA/CMAS approach -- no Indian equivalent exists)

### Tier 3: M&A Leadership Fellow (Future)
- Multi-year engagement, advanced modules, published thought leadership
- Aspirational tier for Year 2+

## Faculty & Guest Speaker Model

### Core Faculty (Per Session)
- **1 Academic:** IIT Roorkee / IIM faculty (Finance, Strategy, or Law)
- **1 Practitioner:** Former Corp Dev Head, PE Partner, M&A Lawyer, or Investment Banker
- This is the IIMB proven model (Dr. Ashok Thampy + Mr. Pratap Giri S)

### Guest Speakers (3-4 Per Program)
- **Day 3:** Regulatory expert (former CCI/SEBI practitioner)
- **Day 4:** CXO who led a real integration (success + failure stories)
- **Day 5:** CXO Panel (2-3 leaders from different industries)
- Columbia model: 6-7 named guests per cohort adds massive credibility

### Advisory Board (For Curriculum Governance)
- 3-5 senior M&A practitioners from different industries
- Review curriculum annually
- Provide case studies from their deals (anonymized)
