## 1. Architectural Pivot

> **From:** Selling a 5-day course to individuals.
> **To:** Onboarding companies as accounts. All 3 layers unlock together.

The institute develops M&A capability **holistically across the firm** — strategy, execution, and ground-level implementation must move in sync for acquisitions to create value.

## 2. The Three Layers

| Layer | Cohort | Duration | Audience | Core promise |
|-------|--------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **L1** | Enterprise M&A Leaders | 2.5 days | CXOs, promoters, board members, group heads, corp dev heads, CFO/CHRO/CSO, PE operating partners | Strategic judgment, governance discipline, failure recognition, stakeholder conviction |
| **L2** | M&A Execution Leaders | 5 days (Day 5 half) | BU heads, VPs, functional leaders (strategy, finance, HR, IT, ops, sales, transformation), integration leaders | Translate deal thesis into Day 1 readiness, 100-day execution, workstreams, synergy tracking, governance |
| **L3** | Implementation Champions / Budding M&A Leaders | 5 days (Day 5 half) | Senior managers, managers, analysts, workstream leads, high-potential talent, integration champions | M&A fluency, role clarity, escalation discipline, communication confidence, ground-level integration leadership |

## 3. L1 — Enterprise M&A Leaders (2.5-Day Closed-Door Forum)

**Status:** TK has thought this through. Captured here verbatim from cohort architecture PDF.

### Positioning
A premium, discussion-led leadership forum for people who **approve, sponsor, govern, and communicate** acquisitions. Should not feel like a compressed M&A course. Should feel like a **boardroom-quality peer forum**.

### Design principles
- Fewer sessions, more dialogue
- Curated failure cases
- Boardroom dilemmas
- Peer debate
- Practical leadership artifacts

### Social architecture
- Networking lunch — Day 1 & Day 2
- Curated dinner — Day 1
- Informal peer time — Day 2 evening
- Departure-friendly lunch — Day 3

### Signature feature
**M&A Failure Case Discussion** — inspired by the spirit of *Billion Dollar Lessons*, but tailored to acquisition failures, value leakage, cultural mismatch, synergy overclaiming, and integration governance breakdowns.

### Day-by-day

#### Day 1 — Strategic Judgment & Failure Recognition
- Opening provocation: *Why good companies make bad acquisitions*
- Session 1: The M&A leadership problem
- Session 2: Failure case studies in M&A
- Session 3: Deal thesis and boardroom judgment
- Evening: curated networking dinner

**Outputs:** M&A Failure Pattern Map · Board-Level Deal Approval Checklist

#### Day 2 — Governance, Integration Oversight, Stakeholder Conviction
- Session 4: Executive integration governance
- Session 5: Stakeholder storytelling in M&A
- Session 6: Building repeatable M&A capability
- Late afternoon: capstone briefing + team prep

**Outputs:** Executive Integration Governance Model · M&A Leadership Narrative Template · Enterprise M&A Capability Roadmap

#### Day 3 (Half) — Capstone & Closure
- Capstone: **M&A Leadership War Room**
- Plenary: key takeaways + leadership commitments
- Photos, certificates, closing lunch, departures

**Capstone recommendations:** approve / reject / approve-with-conditions verdict on a live case + top risks + integration governance + stakeholder narrative + first 100-day leadership priorities.

## 4. L2 — M&A Execution Leaders (5-Day Applied Intensive) — DRAFT

**Status:** TK note — *needs relook*. PDF draft below; refined proposal in companion doc `11_curriculum_v3_L2_L3_refined.md`.

### Positioning
Flagship practical program for leaders who must translate acquisition intent into operating results. Structured, **template-driven, simulation-heavy**.

### Day-by-day (PDF draft)

| Day | Core focus | Outputs |
|-----|------------|---------|
| 1 | Deal Thesis → Integration Thesis: execution failure patterns, deal logic, value creation thesis, integration priorities, case discussion | Deal Thesis Canvas · Integration Thesis Statement |
| 2 | Day 1 Readiness & 100-Day Planning: announcement/close readiness, employee/customer continuity, reporting, compliance, communication cascade | Day 1 Readiness Checklist · 100-Day Integration Plan |
| 3 | Workstreams, Governance, Decision Rights: IMO design, functional tracks, escalation rules, cross-functional dependencies | Workstream Charter · Decision Rights Matrix · Dependency Map · Governance Cadence |
| 4 | Synergy Realization, Risk, Stakeholder Mgmt: cost/revenue synergies, baselines, value leakage, talent/culture risks, communication | Synergy Tracker · Value Leakage Risk Register · Stakeholder Communication Map |
| 5 (½) | Capstone Integration Blueprint: team presentations + feedback. Certification, photos, lunch | Complete Integration Blueprint covering thesis, Day 1, 100-day plan, workstreams, synergies, risks, communications |

### Experience design
Mornings = concepts & cases. Afternoons = applied labs + templates. End with **boardroom-style integration blueprint presentation**, not an exam.

## 6. Commercial Launch Logic

1. **L2 Execution Leaders** = launch flagship. Closest to organizational pain. Easiest for companies to justify spend.
2. **L1 Enterprise Leaders** = premium invite-only anchor. Drives platform partner relationships, CXO trust, thought leadership.
3. **L3 Implementation Champions** = scale lever. Company-wide integration readiness, open enrolment, certification pipelines.
4. **Corporate Package** = L1 + L2 + L3 sold as **"M&A Capability Building Stack"** — end-to-end. This is the canonical offering once a company is onboarded.

---

## 7. Account Model

- **Account = a company.** Not an individual seat, not a cohort booking.
- **Onboarding = signing the company to the Capability Building Stack.**
- **All 3 layers unlock** for the account on onboarding.
- Each layer runs as its own scheduled cohort; the account sends the right people to each.
- Renewals, expansions, and roadmap reviews are at the **account level**.

---

## 8. Pedagogical Threads Across All Three Layers

| Thread | L1 | L2 | L3 |
|--------|----|----|----|
| Failure cases | Strategic / boardroom failure | Execution failure patterns | Ground-level failure scenarios |
| Templates | Governance models, narrative templates | Workstream/decision-rights/synergy templates | Trackers, checklists, scripts |
| Simulation | Leadership War Room (capstone) | Integration Blueprint (capstone) | Champion Simulation (capstone) |
| Output mode | Boardroom recommendation | Boardroom-style blueprint | Action plan + role clarity |

Common architecture: every layer ends with a **capstone**, not an exam. Every layer produces **artifacts** the participant can take back to their firm.
