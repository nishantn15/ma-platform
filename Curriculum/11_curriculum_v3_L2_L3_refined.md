## L2 — M&A Execution Leaders (Refined)

### What changed vs. PDF draft

| Area | PDF draft | Refined |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Pedagogy mix | Concepts mornings, labs afternoons | Same shape, but **every day ends with a sim/exercise**, not just templates |
| Day 1 sim | Case discussion only | **Deal Thesis Defense** — each team defends their thesis to a faculty "investment committee" |
| Day 3 sim | Workstream charter exercise | **IMO Sprint Room** — teams stand up an Integration Management Office in 4 hours with real conflict injects |
| Day 4 sim | Synergy tracker exercise | **Synergy Pressure Test** — faculty injects value-leakage curveballs mid-tracking |
| Capstone | Blueprint presentation | Blueprint to a **CXO panel + Board Observer** (guest exec roles), Q&A includes hostile questions |
| Faculty model | Implied single-faculty | **Practitioner + Academic pair per day**, plus 1 guest CXO across Day 4 / Day 5 |
| Output portfolio | 4–5 templates | **9-artifact Integration Blueprint** packaged as a take-home binder |

### Positioning
Flagship 5-day practical program for leaders who **own integration outcomes**. Heavy on templates, heavy on simulation. Participants leave with a customised Integration Blueprint for their next acquisition — not just frameworks they "could use later".

### Audience
BU heads, VPs, functional leaders (strategy, finance, HR, IT, ops, sales, transformation), corporate development executives, integration leaders. Typical experience: 10–18 years.

### Cohort size
20–24 participants per cohort. Teams of 4–6.

### Day-by-Day (Refined)

#### Day 1 — Deal Thesis to Integration Thesis
**Morning:** Concept
- Why deals fail at execution (data, not anecdotes)
- The deal thesis → integration thesis bridge
- Case: a real Indian mid-market integration that lost value in Year 1

**Afternoon:** Applied lab
- Deal Thesis Canvas — teams complete for an assigned target
- Integration Thesis Statement — what changes Day 1, what changes in 100 days, what changes in Year 1

**Sim:** **Deal Thesis Defense (90 min)** — each team presents to a faculty "investment committee". Faculty role-plays skeptical CFO, sponsor, and outside director.

**Outputs:** Deal Thesis Canvas · Integration Thesis Statement

**Aha moment:** Teams discover their thesis is operationally vague — they cannot defend the synergy math under pressure.

---

#### Day 2 — Day 1 Readiness & 100-Day Plan
**Morning:** Concept
- The Day -90 / Day -30 / Day 0 / Day 1 readiness curve
- Continuity vectors: employee, customer, supplier, IT, finance, legal, brand
- Communication cascade architecture

**Afternoon:** Applied lab
- Day 1 Readiness Checklist (sector-customised — Healthcare / Renewables / EV / Tech / Mfg)
- 100-Day Integration Plan with milestone map + RACI

**Sim:** **Announcement Day Tabletop (60 min)** — at 8 AM Day 1, a regulator query / customer leak / key talent resignation hits. Teams triage in real time.

**Outputs:** Day 1 Readiness Checklist · 100-Day Integration Plan · Cascade Communication Map

**Aha moment:** Day 1 risk is mostly comms, not legal or financial. Most teams under-invest in cascade design.

---

#### Day 3 — Workstreams, Governance, Decision Rights
**Morning:** Concept
- IMO design — full-time vs. matrixed
- Decision rights: who decides, who recommends, who informs
- Cross-functional dependency mapping

**Afternoon:** Applied lab
- Workstream Charter (per functional track)
- Decision Rights Matrix
- Dependency Map
- Governance Cadence calendar

**Sim:** **IMO Sprint Room (3 hours)** — teams stand up a full IMO with charters and decision rights. Faculty injects 3 conflicts: HR demands authority over retention, IT escalates a data integration risk, Sales wants exception on customer comms.

**Outputs:** Workstream Charter · Decision Rights Matrix · Dependency Map · Governance Cadence

**Aha moment:** Most "execution problems" are decision-rights problems in disguise. The IMO is a decision-rights instrument, not a project plan.

---

#### Day 4 — Synergy Realization, Risk, Stakeholder Management
**Morning:** Concept
- Cost vs. revenue synergies — base rates, leak rates, optimism bias
- Talent & culture risk — quantifying the unquantifiable
- Stakeholder narrative — investors, regulators, employees, customers

**Afternoon:** Applied lab
- Synergy Tracker — baselined, with leakage triggers
- Value Leakage Risk Register
- Stakeholder Communication Map
- Talent retention model

**Sim:** **Synergy Pressure Test (2 hours)** — faculty injects mid-quarter curveballs: a key salesperson resigns taking 30% revenue; a regulator opens an antitrust inquiry; a synergy assumption proves 40% optimistic. Teams replan.

**Guest:** A CXO who led one successful and one failed integration. Open Q&A.

**Outputs:** Synergy Tracker · Value Leakage Risk Register · Stakeholder Communication Map · Talent Retention Model

**Aha moment:** Synergy realization is mostly a defensive game — protecting baseline value matters more than chasing upside.

---

#### Day 5 (Half) — Capstone Blueprint
**Morning:**
- Teams finalise their **Complete Integration Blueprint** binder: Deal Thesis Canvas + Day 1 Readiness + 100-Day Plan + Workstreams + Synergy Tracker + Risk Register + Stakeholder Map + Comms + Talent Retention
- Boardroom-style presentations to a **CXO + Board Observer panel** (guest execs)
- Faculty + peer scoring
- Certification ceremony
- Closing lunch

**Outputs:** Complete Integration Blueprint (9-artifact bound deliverable)

### Faculty Model (L2)
- **Lead academic** — Strategy / Finance professor
- **Lead practitioner** — Former Corp Dev head / Integration leader
- **Guest CXO** — Day 4 panel + Day 5 capstone panel
- **Sim facilitator** — 1 trained facilitator per 6 participants for sim days

### Toolkit / Artifact Portfolio
9 take-home artifacts → bound as **Integration Blueprint Binder** with company brand cover.

### Optional Year-Long Engagement
Same as v2 logic — 3 coaching touchpoints after the 5-day intensive (Month 4, Month 8, Month 12) on the account's live deals.

## L3 — Implementation Champions / Budding M&A Leaders (Refined)

### What changed vs. PDF draft

| Area | PDF draft | Refined |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Audience clarity | Generic "managers / analysts / champions" | **Explicit personas**: 1) workstream members, 2) sales/marketing integrators (per TK Apr 28), 3) high-potential talent, 4) field-level integration leads |
| Day 1 framing | M&A foundations lecture | **Why M&A matters TO YOUR ROLE** — role-specific framing per persona |
| Day 2 framing | Day 1 basics generically | **The role-shift moment** — what changes for me Monday Day 1, with persona-specific drills |
| Day 4 framing | Communication scripts | **Rumour-mill simulation** — handle confusion, change resistance, founder-team tension live |
| Day 5 capstone | Single scenario | **Multi-scenario carousel** — each team cycles through 4 scenarios in 30-min rotations |
| Output focus | Trackers + checklists | Same + **a personal "Day 1 Monday Action Plan"** they take back |

### Positioning
A practical, role-aware program for the people who actually deliver integration on the ground — including the often-overlooked **sales and marketing integrators** who own customer continuity post-deal.

### Audience
Senior managers, managers, analysts, workstream leads, sales/marketing integrators, high-potential talent, change champions. Typical experience: 4–10 years.

### Cohort size
24–30 per cohort. Teams of 5–6, ideally cross-functional.

### Day-by-Day (Refined)

#### Day 1 — M&A Foundations Through Your Lens
**Morning:** Concept
- Why companies acquire — strategic logic in 60 minutes
- Deal lifecycle map — what happens at each stage and who's involved
- Why deals fail — common pattern map (kept punchy, not academic)

**Afternoon:** Role-specific tracks (parallel sessions)
- *Functional staff track* — what your function does in integration
- *Sales/marketing integrator track* — customer continuity, GTM unification, brand decisions
- *Analyst track* — the data and tracking that integration runs on

**Output:** M&A Lifecycle Map · M&A Vocabulary Guide · Role-Specific Lens Card

**Aha moment:** "I thought integration was someone else's job — it's mine, and here's exactly where."

---

#### Day 2 — Day 1 & the Role-Shift Moment
**Morning:** Concept
- The 0 → 1 → 30 day curve from your seat
- Continuity vectors and where each role plugs in
- Reporting changes, role-clarity, identity (badges, email, systems)

**Afternoon:** Persona drills
- Workstream member: what does your task tracker look like Day 1?
- Sales integrator: handling customer questions, joint pitching, account assignments
- Marketing integrator: brand decisions, comms freeze, joint announcements
- Analyst: data pipelines, reporting consolidation, KPI alignment

**Output:** Day 1 Task Checklist · Personal Role Clarity Map · Persona Day-1 Drill Sheet

**Aha moment:** Most confusion in Week 1 is informational, not strategic. Clear role clarity prevents 70% of escalations.

---

#### Day 3 — Workstream Execution & Issue Escalation
**Morning:** Concept
- Task tracking, dependency management
- The escalation discipline: when to escalate, to whom, how
- The "issue log" as a discipline, not a tool

**Afternoon:** Applied lab
- Workstream Task Tracker (populated for your function)
- Issue Escalation Log
- Dependency Map (mini, your function only)

**Sim:** **Escalation Game (90 min)** — 4 issues flow into a team-shared issue log. Teams practice categorising (own / escalate / wait) and writing escalation memos.

**Output:** Workstream Task Tracker · Issue Escalation Log · Dependency Map

**Aha moment:** Not escalating is a bigger sin than over-escalating. Most ground-level disasters come from issues that sat too long.

---

#### Day 4 — Communication, Change, Local Leadership
**Morning:** Concept
- Managing uncertainty under your own
- Rumour mechanics and how to defuse them
- Change resistance — surface vs. underlying
- Talking to: peers, customers, vendors, your team

**Afternoon:** Role-play labs
- Customer conversation script — sales integrator
- Internal team comms script — manager
- Vendor renegotiation cue — analyst
- Local risk surfacing — all

**Sim:** **Rumour-Mill Simulation (2 hours)** — actors role-play disgruntled colleague, anxious customer, confused report. Participants navigate live.

**Output:** Communication Script · Change Adoption Checklist · Local Risk Register

**Aha moment:** Saying "I don't know yet, here's what I'll do to find out" is the single most-valuable phrase in integration comms.

---

#### Day 5 (Half) — Integration Champion Simulation
**Format:** Carousel — 4 scenarios, teams rotate every 30 min.

| Scenario | Trigger |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A top-3 customer threatens to leave citing "change of ownership" anxiety |
| 2 | IT cutover slips 2 weeks; finance reporting at risk for quarter-end |
| 3 | Two managers from acquirer and acquired both think they own the same team |
| 4 | Founder of acquired company is undermining the new joint strategy in town-halls |

Closing plenary: each participant submits their **Day 1 Monday Action Plan** — what they will do their first Monday back at the firm.

**Outputs:** Integration Champion Action Plan · Day 1 Monday Action Plan · Certificate · closing lunch

### Faculty Model (L3)
- **Lead practitioner** — Former integration manager or HR business partner with M&A experience
- **Sales integration specialist** — for the Day 1 + Day 4 sales/marketing tracks
- **Role-play actors** — 2–3 for the rumour-mill sim
- **Sim facilitators** — 1 per 8 participants on sim days

### Toolkit / Artifact Portfolio
7 take-home artifacts → bound as **Integration Champion Field Kit**.
