## CRITICAL findings

### #1 — §1 demotes the master brand line
v1 says category idea is the top, "everything else expresses it." But TK's source treats master brand line and category idea as co-equal. Master brand line is the **repeatable signature**; category idea is the **positioning sentence**.

**Fix:** Rewrite §1 with three stacked-equal lines:
- Master brand line (the signature, used everywhere)
- Category idea (what we are)
- Platform promise (the journey, used as headline)

### #11 — A copywriter can't extract 3 viable headlines confidently
§13 says "Hero replaces with master brand line + platform promise" but doesn't show the worked example.

**Fix:** Add hero pattern callout:
```
H1: Boardroom judgment. Integration leadership. Enterprise-wide M&A capability.
Sub: Acquire with conviction. Integrate with discipline. Scale into an institution.
Eyebrow: A capability institution for India's next generation of acquirers.
```

### #14 — Missing typography/color/imagery section
A designer asks within 5 minutes. Currently §7 mentions "low-poly faceted gold geometry, navy backdrop" in passing, no hex, no typeface, no scale.

**Fix:** Add §7.5 Visual System (provisional) with current v5 specs marked TBD-pending-Track-B.

### #26 — "Not a SaaS deal-room tool" conflicts with the platform actually being partly SaaS
Track C builds Integration Leadership Room with workstream tracker — that's software. §9 dodge will trip the first dashboard-tagline writer.

**Fix:** Refine §9 from negation to qualification:
> Not "a SaaS deal-room tool" — we are guided M&A capability infrastructure. Software is part of it; the brand isn't.

### #19 — Doc doesn't acknowledge the downstream artifacts that inherit from it
TK's #4 (video) and #5 (website-video sync) chain off #6 (brand). Brand doc never says it.

**Fix:** Add §0 Scope and Downstream Documents calling out video brief + v6 site rewrite explicitly.
