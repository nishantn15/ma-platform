## M&A-Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A Activity and Ownership Structure

**Advanced Enzyme Technologies**  (AETL) has demonstrated a consistent track record of inorganic growth, completing four acquisitions in the last nine years using internal accruals. [[5 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ff279d8921c6823d990cb9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14351&hl=fse14351&page=7)  Recent transactions include the 2023 acquisition of a 50% stake in  **Saiganesh Enzytech Solutions**  for INR 5.99 crore and the acquisition of  **Shri Ganesh Industrial Enzymes**  for INR 0.90 crore. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-254621__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25102&hl=fse25102,fse25526&page=8) [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-da0edb0a350c4f8344f14b2dc0e7952e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse810170&hl=fse810170&page=243)  In March 2024, the company further increased its stake in  **JC Biotech**  to 95.72% for INR 5.61 crore. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-254621__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24465&hl=fse24465,fse24615,fse24631&page=8)  As of March 31, 2026, the company operates 13 subsidiaries globally, including its newest entity,  **Advanced Nutrazyme Private Limited** , incorporated in July 2025. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ff279d8921c6823d990cbb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10854&hl=fse10854&page=4) [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-418773__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20086&hl=fse20086&page=7)  The  **Promoter Group** , led by  **Vasant Rathi** , holds a 43.26% stake. [[11 • Dion Global Solutions] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-254d914e37e45e025463853bd939f690ede36fdf&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse234&hl=fse234&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management explicitly stated in November 2025 that they maintain a "cash warchest" of close to  **INR 600 crore**  and are actively assessing multiple M&A targets. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-27848932-1969806262&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29949&hl=fse29949&page=2)  Strategic priorities for future acquisitions include enhancing front-end marketing capabilities, expanding B2C presence, and acquiring key technologies in the probiotics and enzyme discovery spaces. [[13 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-371107__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24834&hl=fse24834&page=29)  The company remains a zero-debt entity, providing significant balance sheet flexibility for larger transactions. [[14 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ff279d8921c6823d990cb9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14351&hl=fse14351&page=7)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

No formal strategic review or activist activity was reported as of May 2026.  However, the company experienced a leadership change with the resignation of  **Dipak Roda** , Senior VP of Marketing and Business Development, effective April 2026.
