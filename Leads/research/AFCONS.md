## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A and Capital Structure

Afcons Infrastructure recently executed a significant capital event with its  **INR 5,430 crore IPO**  in late 2024, which included an Offer for Sale (OFS) of  **INR 4,180 crore**  by the promoter group and a fresh issue of  **INR 1,250 crore** . [[1 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1ff9d282a2e9c7879dc159&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse16878&hl=fse16878&page=8) [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1ff9d282a2e9c7879dc13f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse54884&hl=fse54884&page=20)  Prior to the IPO, the company consolidated its holdings by acquiring the remaining  **26%**  of  **Afcons Oil & Gas Services**  and the remaining  **0.99%**  of  **Afcons Overseas Singapore**  in FY24. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1fd858fc6ceb69029c725a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse982726&hl=fse982726&page=307)  As of March 2026,  **Goswami Infratech Private Limited**  (Shapoorji Pallonji group) remains the primary promoter with a  **50.2%**  stake. [[4 • ICICI Securities Limited] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICIC-65f7f691601ee9f1c7d74a3be72477e3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9880&hl=fse9880&page=5)

### Subsidiary and JV Network

The company operates through  **12 global subsidiaries**  and approximately  **16 joint ventures** . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69de2f8a0e95a9c34f24ea3c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11754&hl=fse11754&page=24)  Key subsidiaries include  **Hazarat and Company**  and  **Afcons Mauritius Infrastructure** , which acts as a holding vehicle for African and Southeast Asian operations. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1fd858fc6ceb69029c725a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse982959&hl=fse982959&page=307) [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-690241b887123000078290e3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1156730&hl=fse1156730&page=376) [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1ff9d282a2e9c7879dc145&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse65133&hl=fse65133&page=21)  Minority stakes are strategically held in specialized entities like  **Afcons Sener LNG**  (49%) and various international JVs to mitigate risk in new geographies or sectors where the company lacks specific pre-qualification (PQ). ### Growth Signals and Capital Allocation

Afcons maintains an  **"asset-right"**  strategy, prioritizing specialized equipment ownership—valued at  **INR 4,282.55 crore** —over broad diversification. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68646e4814788506e34b88b2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25761&hl=fse25761&page=13) [[12 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547573__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17907&hl=fse17907&page=17)  Post-IPO, capital allocation is focused on  **debt reduction (INR 600 crore)**  and  **working capital (INR 320 crore)**  to support a record  **INR 36,869 crore order book** . [[13 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1ff9d282a2e9c7879dc13f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse54884&hl=fse54884&page=20) [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1fd858fc6ceb69029c725a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse143539&hl=fse143539&page=53)  Analysts note that while inorganic growth remains a signal for entering niche technical segments, near-term priority is on converting a  **INR 4 trillion bid pipeline**  and addressing stretched working capital days, which rose to  **143 days**  in FY26. [[15 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-d7063f8445653cee076b131472bf5415&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1797&hl=fse1797,fse1868&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no formal activist pressure is reported, a  **strategic review**  is evident through the ongoing  **reclassification of 29 promoter group entities**  to the "public" category as of February 2026, aimed at streamlining the group's complex ownership structure. [[16 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69de2f8a0e95a9c34f24ea3c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1253&hl=fse1253&page=1)  Potential exits or stake sales in non-core assets are also influenced by the parent group's need for liquidity related to its  **Tata Sons**  stake exit predicament.
