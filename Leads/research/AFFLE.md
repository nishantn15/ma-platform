## M&A Readiness Profile: Affle 3i Limited

### Recent M&A and Corporate Structure

Affle has successfully integrated a series of strategic acquisitions including  **Appnext**  (2020),  **Jampp**  (2021), and  **YouAppi**  (2023) into its unified CPCU performance business model. Ltd.** , with  **Anuj Khanna Sohum**  serving as the key promoter. ### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

Management has signaled a transition from a three-year hiatus in deal-making to an aggressive inorganic phase targeting "meaningfully sized" transactions in 2026, 2028, and 2030. [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-572582851-1981155596&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse44180&hl=fse44180&page=2)  As of May 2026, the company has narrowed its acquisition pool from 12 candidates to a  **shortlist of 4 targets**  undergoing active due diligence. To support this, the Board approved a preferential issue of 7.4 million warrants to its promoter,  **Affle Holdings** , totaling approximately  **INR 1,100 crore**  at INR 1,487 per share. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-572582851-1999170415&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9860&hl=fse9860&page=1)  This infusion, combined with existing cash reserves of over  **INR 1,300 crore** , creates a warchest of approximately  **INR 2,400 crore**  dedicated to the  **Affle 3i**  vision of 10x decadal growth.
