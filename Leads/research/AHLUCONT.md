## M&A-Readiness Profile: Ahluwalia Contracts (India) Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Ahluwalia Contracts (ACIL) has recorded no material external M&A deals in the last five years, focusing instead on internal consolidation.  The company is merging several 100% wholly-owned subsidiaries—including  **Dipesh Mining** ,  **Jiwanjyoti Traders** ,  **Paramount Dealcomm** , and  **Premsagar Merchants** —into the parent entity to facilitate the development or sale of land parcels held in Kolkata. ### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are no active strategic reviews or activist pressures. While some investors have suggested share buybacks due to the high cash balance, management has instead focused on maintaining liquidity to de-risk against macro uncertainties and execution headwinds.
