## Alivus Life Sciences M&A-Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A and Ownership Structure** In March 2024,  **Nirma Limited**  completed the acquisition of a 75.00% equity stake in the company (then  **Glenmark Life Sciences** ) from  **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd**  for ₹5,650 crore. [[1 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-a90387357039b7a2cea3e0e15a869ddd&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3140&hl=fse3140&page=2)  Following this transition, the company was rebranded as  **Alivus Life Sciences** . [[2 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fdab53b6f53444f2fbbf39&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse100&hl=fse100&page=1)  As of May 2026, the promoter holding stands at 74.90%. [[3 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-20ad8500b4e68a77e99117064d2a3864&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1144&hl=fse1144&page=1)  Research did not surface any active subsidiaries; the entity operates as a standalone business focusing on independent growth. [[4 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-541793__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10344&hl=fse10344&page=15)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** Under previous ownership, M&A was "structurally off the table" as cash was upstreamed to the parent; however, inorganic expansion is now explicitly embedded in the strategy. [[5 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-541793__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11200&hl=fse11200,fse11266&page=15)   **Alivus**  is in an "investment phase," generating a strong free cash flow of ₹259 crore in FY26 and ending the year with a cash warchest of ₹782.40 crore (including short-term investments). [[6 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05e91272ae090d196c0b64&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1211&hl=fse1211,fse1277,fse1365&page=1)

Management is actively evaluating opportunities to acquire inorganic assets to enhance its API platform and complex chemistry capabilities. [[7 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-68b375aed5929715719b3e1774e30bdb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7191&hl=fse7191&page=3) [[8 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-f525c31f94322dee1b740cc8c7733eee&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7077&hl=fse7077&page=3)

**Strategic Review and Growth Catalysts** The company has pivoted from value extraction to value creation, removing legacy constraints on capital. [[9 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-541793__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11099&hl=fse11099&page=15)

**Alivus**  is scaling its  **CDMO**  segment, which it expects to grow from ~7.00% to 12.00-15.00% of revenue within 4-5 years. [[10 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-20ad8500b4e68a77e99117064d2a3864&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7745&hl=fse7745&page=3)

It is also investing ₹600 crore in capex for FY26, including a new R&D center in Taloja for oncology and complex chemistry. [[11 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-68b375aed5929715719b3e1774e30bdb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7431&hl=fse7431&page=3) [[12 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-f525c31f94322dee1b740cc8c7733eee&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6785&hl=fse6785&page=3)  While no activist signals were detected, management has faced investor pressure to increase growth aggression, to which they have responded that they are evaluating "deep plans" for the strong cash position. [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-568548413-1978206077&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse52806&hl=fse52806&page=3) [[14 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-c1b50d7c49a4a69d7eb0e776b65357a4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24783&hl=fse24783,fse24827&page=10)
