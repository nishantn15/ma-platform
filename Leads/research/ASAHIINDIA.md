## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Asahi India Glass (AIS) recently completed a significant internal restructuring, merging four subsidiaries— **GX Glass Sales & Services** ,  **AIS Distribution Services** , and  **AIS Adhesives** —into  **AIS Glass Solutions Limited**  (now  **AIS Consumer Glass Solutions Limited** ), effective July 1, 2025. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-566a74cf0f0c53b9dfeab112e32d809a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9636&hl=fse9636&page=3) [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-546218__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15698&hl=fse15698&page=7)  In terms of external M&A, the company acquired specific architectural glass processing assets from  **Balaji Building Technologies**  in Bengaluru in late 2024 to enhance its local market presence. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-d2e64576288461cba0ca9b44e9483533&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41728&hl=fse41728,fse41893&page=26)  The company is a joint venture between the  **Labroo family**  and  **AGC Inc. (Japan)** , with the promoter group maintaining a steady 51.6% stake as of March 2026. [[8 • ICICI Securities Limited] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICIC-3c9831fa53ee279a8b2c85593a7e010f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6069&hl=fse6069&page=3) [[9 • Money Times (India)] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-9db8c60444f377e82c784d05a1d1bc88bc54354e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse856&hl=fse856&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

AIS demonstrated strong inorganic growth intent by raising  **INR 1,000 crore**  through a  **Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP)**  in September 2025 to fund its expansion plans. [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-566a74cf0f0c53b9dfeab112e32d809a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10774&hl=fse10774&page=3) [[11 • East India Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EAIS-75051489780a43ebb52322e802447ce7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4216&hl=fse4216&page=2)  The board has approved a massive  **INR 2,000 crore**  capex program for greenfield expansions in float glass, coatings, and processing businesses. [[12 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-408769__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1844&hl=fse1844,fse1956&page=1)  A new float glass plant in Soniyana, Rajasthan, recently stabilized and is now fully utilized for in-house automotive requirements.
