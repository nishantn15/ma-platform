## M&A-Readiness Profile: Asian Paints Ltd (NSE: ASIANPAINT)

**Recent M&A Activity and Subsidiary Structure** Asian Paints has focused on consolidating its subsidiary structure and simplifying its corporate footprint. [[1 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1517940&hl=fse1517940&page=281)  On June 27, 2025, the company acquired the remaining 40% stake in  **Obgenix Software Private Limited** , bringing its total ownership to 100%. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-419044__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1534445&hl=fse1534445,fse1534454&page=283)  In March 2026, the company completed the amalgamation of its wholly-owned subsidiary,  **AP Polymers** , into the parent entity. [[3 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse813661&hl=fse813661&page=159)  Additionally, the company's stake in  **Asian White Cement Holdings Limited (AWCHL)**  was diluted from 70% to 60% in January 2026 following the issuance of sweat equity to a joint venture partner. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-419044__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse816330&hl=fse816330&page=159)  The group currently operates 28 subsidiaries and joint ventures, including major international holdings like  **Universal Paints Limited**  and  **Berger Paints Emirates LLC** . [[5 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1511906&hl=fse1511906&page=280)

**Capital Allocation and Growth Signals** As of March 31, 2026, Asian Paints maintains a substantial "cash warchest" totaling  **INR 6,556.46 crore** , composed of cash, bank balances, and liquid mutual fund investments. [[6 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse983021&hl=fse983021,fse983165&page=186)  The company generated strong free cash flows of  **INR 5,566.7 crore**  during the 2025-26 fiscal year. [[7 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse184664&hl=fse184664,fse184681&page=46)  Management continues to signal inorganic intent through its pursuit of "future-ready" businesses, such as home décor and specialty chemicals, while maintaining a steady 60% dividend payout ratio to shareholders. [[8 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse184707&hl=fse184707,fse184711&page=46)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** Research did not surface any current activist investor threats or formal strategic reviews aimed at divestiture.  The company instead reports a stable governance environment with the parent entity contributing  **94.40%**  of the group's total comprehensive income. [[9 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2c0f604ce34e2f3f7df60e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1502981&hl=fse1502981&page=278)  While  **PPG Asian Paints Lanka Private Limited**  is currently under liquidation, this appears to be a routine streamlining of international operations rather than a broader strategic pivot. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-419044__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse816508&hl=fse816508&page=159)
