## M&A-Readiness Profile: Astral Ltd (NSE: ASTRAL)

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Astral has maintained an active inorganic growth trajectory, primarily focused on portfolio diversification and backward integration. [[4 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0db322c3193accecbde5b5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2537&hl=fse2537&page=7)  In June 2026, its subsidiary  **Astral Chemie**  entered definitive agreements to acquire a 60% stake in  **Differentiated and Sustainable Solutions LLP (DSS)**  for INR 39.11 crore to expand into specialty chemicals for electronics and aerospace. [[5 • OFFER] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-578aa5b0b1e4301c803c189c1858df44&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5170&hl=fse5170&page=3)  In 2025, the company consolidated its holdings by acquiring the remaining 20% of  **Astral Chemie**  (formerly Gem Paints) for INR 75 crore in September and the remaining 5% of  **Seal IT Services UK**  for approximately INR 4.8 crore. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-412121__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9747&hl=fse9747,fse9894,fse10049,fse10106&page=4) [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-552360__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse23707&hl=fse23707,fse24032&page=12)  Other significant moves include the 100% acquisition of  **Al-Aziz Plastics**  for INR 33 crore in April 2025 to enter the electrofusion fittings market and an 80% stake in  **Nexelon Chem**  to establish a captive CPVC resin plant. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-552360__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse52408&hl=fse52408,fse52634&page=22) [[9 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-1bdda71fe999f1b00d4400c3b5a3a100&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3985&hl=fse3985&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is promoter-led, with  **Sandeep Pravinbhai Engineer**  serving as Chairman and Managing Director and the promoter group holding a stable 54.22% stake as of March 2026. [[10 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-75292ee7163e66c088acc645801f98f7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse962&hl=fse962&page=1) [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68900043c67d859204b4a949&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse627042&hl=fse627042&page=191)

The subsidiary structure is well-defined across plumbing, adhesives, and paints, with recent efforts focused on making key units wholly owned. [[12 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-552360__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse27495&hl=fse27495&page=13) [[13 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-997934ae874d2730294615a25b1393a3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7025&hl=fse7025&page=3)

### Strategic Signals

Management has explicitly stated that its cash balance—INR 790.30 crore as of March 31, 2026—is earmarked for potential M&A opportunities within its existing four verticals. [[14 • East India Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EAIS-19dcc174bbe85e6752c94aa0ede804cd&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5312&hl=fse5312,fse5358&page=2)

While no formal "strategic review" or activist pressure was reported, the company recently completed a leadership overhaul in its UK operations to improve profitability following a period of underperformance.
