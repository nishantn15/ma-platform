## M&A-Readiness Profile: Adani Total Gas Ltd (ATGL)

### M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Adani Total Gas Ltd operates as a strategic partnership between the  **Adani Group**  and  **TotalEnergies** , with each partner holding a 37.38% and 37.40% stake respectively as of March 31, 2026. [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1249201&hl=fse1249201,fse1249241&page=270)  Recent inorganic activity is characterized by the formation of 100%-owned subsidiaries:  **Adani TotalEnergies E-Mobility Limited**  (ATEL) and  **Adani TotalEnergies Biomass Limited**  (ATBL), which manage 1,765 EV charging stations and 2 biogas plants respectively. [[2 • Sustainability / CSR reports] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1ecfb7493ba6dc5dec7719&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5803&hl=fse5803,fse5868&page=3)  ATGL also maintains a 50% joint venture with  **Indian Oil Corporation**  (IOAGPL), which issued INR 689 crore in Compulsory Convertible Debentures (CCDs) for which ATGL provides backstopping support starting in FY 2026-27. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1007864&hl=fse1007864,fse1007970&page=225)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

ATGL reports a cash and cash equivalents balance of INR 253.05 crore as of March 31, 2026. [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25663&hl=fse25663&page=9)  The company demonstrates clear inorganic intent, stating that "comprehensive due diligence is carried for Mergers and Acquisitions" to manage risks and ensure certainty. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse495540&hl=fse495540&page=142)  Capital management plans are reviewed regularly by the Board, and the company recently invested in  **Smart Meters Technologies Private Limited**  (50% stake) to enhance its infrastructure capabilities. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1006207&hl=fse1006207&page=225)

### Strategic Review and Governance

The company conducts an annual strategy meet with the Board to deliberate on strategic programs and ongoing initiatives. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse684479&hl=fse684479,fse684532,fse684649&page=169)  While no activist investor activity was reported, the company has established a "Business Sustainability Council" and a Corporate Responsibility Committee (100% independent directors) to oversee capital allocation for climate initiatives and sustainable investments. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537090__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse225713&hl=fse225713&page=75)  These structures indicate a high state of readiness for green-energy-focused acquisitions or joint ventures.
