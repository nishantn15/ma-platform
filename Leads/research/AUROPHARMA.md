## M&A-Readiness Profile: Aurobindo Pharma Ltd (NSE: AUROPHARMA)

**Aurobindo Pharma**  is currently in an active inorganic growth phase, characterized by strategic "bolt-on" acquisitions and internal business reorganizations to enhance its specialty segments. [[1 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-1514127-1981898713&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8308&hl=fse8308&page=1)

### Recent M&A Activity and Deal Sizes

In early 2026,  **Aurobindo**  entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of  **Lannett Company LLC**  through its US subsidiary, a move expected to significantly strengthen its US market presence. [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452872__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41962&hl=fse41962&page=13)  On January 1, 2026, the company signed a Business Transfer Agreement to purchase the non-oncology prescription formulations business from  **Khandelwal Laboratories**  for INR 325 crore, comprising INR 300 crore upfront and INR 25 crore in deferred payments. [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452872__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse42312&hl=fse42312,fse42570&page=13)  Other recent deals include the acquisition of  **Diadame Pharma SARL**  in Senegal (January 2026) and the remaining 49% stake in  **GLS Pharma**  for INR 22.50 crore in early 2024 to make it a wholly owned subsidiary. [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452872__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41818&hl=fse41818&page=13) [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68a3e2c8c67d8507d0a60248&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse419163&hl=fse419163&page=105)

### Parent/Promoter and Subsidiary Structure

The company was founded and remains led by promoters  **P. V. Ramprasad Reddy**  and  **K. Nityananda Reddy** , who maintain a 51.8% controlling stake as of March 2026. [[6 • ESG- Other topic-specific reports] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a105397cbd05073d7e3c83c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18329&hl=fse18329&page=9) [[7 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-6653dee4531c25aafd4b06584be0be0b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1155&hl=fse1155&page=1)  The corporate structure is expansive, with over 90 subsidiaries and step-down entities. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-376392__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34431&hl=fse34431&page=11)  Notable subsidiaries include  **Eugia Pharma Specialities**  (injectables) and  **CuraTeQ Biologics** . [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452872__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse64408&hl=fse64408&page=18)  Recent structural shifts involve selling 2% stakes in  **Theranym Biologics**  and  **CuraTeQ**  to leadership as performance incentives. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-b53b551628185c26168f5ffef334d404&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse350180&hl=fse350180&page=96)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

Management maintains a strong net cash position of approximately $317 million (including investments) as of March 31, 2026, even after funding the  **Khandelwal Labs**  acquisition. [[11 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452872__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2590&hl=fse2590&page=6)  Capital allocation is prioritized toward high-growth platforms including  **biologics** ,  **specialty injectables** , and  **biosimilars** . [[12 • Elara Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-331eaf09211ce4368f889dc7d37686d6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2213&hl=fse2213&page=1)  While not desperate for deals, management remains open to targets that fit their strategy at the right price. [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-1514127-1981898713&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse38822&hl=fse38822,fse38937&page=2)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are currently no reported activist signals or formal broad-based strategic reviews.  However, the company is performing internal reorganizations, such as the slump sale of its domestic branded generic formulations business to its wholly owned subsidiary,  **Auropharm Limited** , effective April 1, 2026.
