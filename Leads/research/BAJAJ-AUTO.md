## M&A-Readiness Profile: Bajaj Auto Ltd (NSE: BAJAJ-AUTO)

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Bajaj Auto's most significant recent transaction was the acquisition of a controlling interest in its Austrian partner on  **November 18, 2025** . [[1 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fb87986cda77ed7b21d5d6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse35869&hl=fse35869&page=13)  Through its Netherlands subsidiary,  **BAIHBV** , the company acquired the remaining stake in  **Pierer Bajaj AG**  (now  **Bajaj Auto International Holdings AG** ) for a purchase consideration of  **INR 4,456.04 crore** . [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-538679__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse38281&hl=fse38281,fse38312&page=13)  This transaction effectively increased Bajaj's stake in the listed  **Bajaj Mobility AG**  (formerly  **Pierer Mobility AG** ) to approximately  **74.90%** , providing control over premium brands including  **KTM** ,  **Husqvarna** , and  **GASGAS** . [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-538679__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse84967&hl=fse84967&page=30)

The company maintains a "robust" balance sheet with surplus funds exceeding  **INR 18,000 crore**  as of March 31, 2026, even after allocating  **INR 2,300 crore**  toward strategic bets in subsidiaries during FY26. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fb4f4df9866a105980de51&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4033&hl=fse4033&page=2)  Despite returning  **INR 9,825 crore**  to shareholders via dividends and buybacks in FY26, the cash position remains high, supporting further inorganic growth. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fb4f4df9866a105980de51&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4033&hl=fse4033&page=2)

### Corporate Structure and Strategic Signals

Bajaj Auto operates through a network of wholly owned subsidiaries, including  **Bajaj Auto Credit Ltd (BACL)**  for financing and international sales arms in  **Brazil** ,  **Thailand** , and  **Spain** . [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fb87986cda77ed7b21d5d6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34212&hl=fse34212,fse34382,fse34504&page=13)  The acquisition of  **Bajaj Mobility AG**  signaled a strategic review of its international holdings, moving from a 49.90% associate stake to a 100% holding in the parent entity to better align governance with long-term strategic intent. [[7 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-32669680-1978118777&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25293&hl=fse25293&page=1) [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-32669680-1966533470&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse27982&hl=fse27982&page=2)

Following the acquisition, management immediately commenced a turnaround plan for  **KTM AG** , focusing on liquidity, management restructuring, and cost synergies. [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-32669680-1978118777&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13219&hl=fse13219&page=1)  In February 2026,  **KTM AG**  completed a  **EUR 550 million**  refinancing, allowing it to repay a restructuring loan previously extended by Bajaj's Netherlands subsidiary, further strengthening the group's financial flexibility for future deployment. [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0463b27aa215e135a4f60a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2818&hl=fse2818&page=2)  No activist investor signals were identified.
