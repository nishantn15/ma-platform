## Bajaj Electricals M&A-Readiness Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

**Bajaj Electricals**  has executed several transformative transactions to pivot toward a consumer-centric model. In March 2026, the company completed the acquisition of the  **Morphy Richards**  brand and related intellectual property for  **INR 141.40 crore**  from  **Glen Electric Limited** , moving from a licensing agreement to full ownership in South Asian territories . [[1 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69baa4025bb20aa97f0d853c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5415&hl=fse5415&page=3)  This followed a significant corporate restructuring where the company demerged its  **Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC)**  business into a newly listed entity,  **Bajel Projects Limited** , effective September 1, 2023 . [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-be06c7c6a674d6b1de2936a568498323&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse57270&hl=fse57270&page=25) [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686f70dd71c0870799c7fe13&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse996280&hl=fse996280&page=310)  Additionally, the company simplified its subsidiary structure by merging  **Nirlep Appliances**  (March 2024) and  **Starlite Lighting**  into the parent entity after acquiring controlling stakes . [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-be06c7c6a674d6b1de2936a568498323&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse57351&hl=fse57351&page=25) [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6808441a71c087079b8341fb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse43956&hl=fse43956&page=21)

> As per exchange filings, Bajaj Electricals has announced its entry into the cables business, marking a strategic expansion aimed at broadening its electrical products portfolio and tapping into the strong growth potential of the Indian wires and cables industry. The move follows the company's recent entry into the house wires segment. [[6 • ICICI Direct • 08 Jun 26 • "Opening Bell (June 08)"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-b3141bc2bba1d1bcf4c43a3e4235cbab&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse44380&hl=fse44380,fse44645&page=8)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is part of the  **Bajaj Group** , with ultimate promoter control held through  **Jamnalal Sons Private Limited**  (19.55%) and  **Bajaj Holdings and Investment Limited**  (16.59%) . [[7 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69baa4025bb20aa97f0d853c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15204&hl=fse15204&page=7) [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e049edcca7752e35354dd5c70be54fac&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse260926&hl=fse260926&page=114)  Total promoter holding stood at  **62.70%**  as of March 31, 2026 .
