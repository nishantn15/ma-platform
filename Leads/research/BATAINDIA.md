## M&A Readiness Brief: Bata India Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Structure

Research does not surface any major inorganic acquisitions or divestitures for  **Bata India**  within the last five years; the company primarily operates through its established retail and wholesale networks . [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68d5d43140a55e000765b9a7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse583207&hl=fse583207&page=203)  The corporate structure is led by ultimate promoter  **Bata (BN) B.V.** , which maintains a stable 50.16% stake as of March 2026 . [[2 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-2c77ea9cb62bc65646e873eb7cb710e0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse419&hl=fse419&page=1)  The company has two wholly owned, non-material subsidiaries:  **Bata Properties Limited**  (property letting) and  **Way Finders Brands Limited**  (trading of footwear/apparel) . [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-1f396ea6b441877d2f028b2f8c26d624&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse686796&hl=fse686796&page=267)  Historical joint ventures, such as  **River Bank Developers Private Limited** , appear in older filings but are not flagged as active growth drivers in recent reports . [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-f840298d84f278cdd16f2bcb837f4731&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse218268&hl=fse218268&page=103)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Bata maintains a strong "cash warchest," with bank balances (excluding short-term cash equivalents) totaling INR 487.20 crore as of March 31, 2026 . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-546686__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse76393&hl=fse76393&page=23)  Capital allocation is currently directed toward organic initiatives, including  **Zero Based Merchandising (ZBM)**  rollout to 80% of the network and expanding the franchise footprint to 1,000 stores . [[6 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-2c77ea9cb62bc65646e873eb7cb710e0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3417&hl=fse3417&page=1)  Management maintains a dividend payout policy of at least 40% of Profit After Tax, indicating a preference for returning value to shareholders over aggressive inorganic hoarding . [[7 • ESG- Other topic-specific reports] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a18c59b92c8cc889e37e8c2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3906&hl=fse3906&page=3)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no formal activist pressure is reported,  **Bata India**  is undergoing an intensive internal  **strategic review**  and "transformation plan" to counter stiff competition and weak demand .
