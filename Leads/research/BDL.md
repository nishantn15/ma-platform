### M&A Readiness Profile

**Recent M&A Activity and Structure** Bharat Dynamics (BDL) has no traditional acquisitions or divestitures reported in the last five years. Its "inorganic" activity is limited to strategic minority stakes in two Section 8 joint ventures formed under the Ministry of Defence's Testing Infrastructure Scheme:  **Electronic Warfare (Defence) Testing Foundation**  (10% stake) and  **Advanced Materials (Defence) Testing Foundation**  (20% stake) . It operates no consolidated subsidiaries . [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68b8e3c02e3cd133ec46f6c9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28418&hl=fse28418&page=14)
