### M&A and Strategic Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity** Biocon’s strategy was headlined by the landmark  **$3.3 billion**  acquisition of  **Viatris'**  biosimilars business in November 2022. [[4 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-4538c7d91cf998f491e8615733ae7b6f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3375&hl=fse3375&page=1)  Since then, activity has focused on streamlining: the company divested its non-core Indian branded formulations units to  **Eris Lifesciences**  for INR 1,242 crore in early 2024. [[5 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6903e16b795df60007af3563&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3422&hl=fse3422,fse3600&page=2)

In late 2025 and early 2026, Biocon executed a massive corporate reorganization, spending over  **$1 billion**  to buy out minority investors (including Serum Institute and Mylan) in  **Biocon Biologics (BBL)**  to achieve 100% ownership. [[6 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-bcd64996be1b51a10b9f83e43cf05858&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse946&hl=fse946&page=1) [[7 • PROXY] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fce667b7a0cfbb47ab3792&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse84688&hl=fse84688,fse84873&page=39)

**Corporate Structure and Promoter Holdings** Founder  **Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw**  and promoter entities (Glentec International) maintain a ~45% stake. [[8 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69d77a7d645b011257786b42&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19862&hl=fse19862&page=8)

The group operates through three primary pillars:  **Biocon Biologics**  (wholly owned biosimilars),  **Syngene**  (52.4% owned CRDMO), and  **Biocon Generics** . [[9 • Bernstein Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-BERN-4e97c2cab001f96264fb43304866e5f8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse202962&hl=fse202962&page=78) [[10 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d34118be893756a2c313f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4429&hl=fse4429&page=5)

As of May 2026, the company has consolidated 40 subsidiaries globally to simplify governance. [[11 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-bcd64996be1b51a10b9f83e43cf05858&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse279&hl=fse279&page=1)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** Management has declared the "major investment phase" complete. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-8835628-1995642756&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5045&hl=fse5045&page=1)  Capital allocation is now strictly prioritized toward deleveraging, with a goal to reduce net debt/EBITDA to below  **2.0x** . [[13 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-bcd64996be1b51a10b9f83e43cf05858&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1887&hl=fse1887&page=1)  Current signals suggest an "inorganic pause" while the company focuses on the execution of five key biosimilar launches (e.g., Ustekinumab, Denosumab). [[14 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-bcd64996be1b51a10b9f83e43cf05858&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2068&hl=fse2068&page=1)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** A comprehensive  **strategic review**  concluded with the April 2026 integration of BBL into the parent, unifying leadership and capital allocation. [[15 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-8835628-1977982091&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5124&hl=fse5124&page=1) [[16 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-bcd64996be1b51a10b9f83e43cf05858&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse946&hl=fse946,fse1564&page=1)

While no formal activist campaigns are present, brokers have expressed concerns regarding "below-par sales execution" in the U.S. and the long "absorption curve" for recent capex, placing pressure on management to deliver tangible returns by FY27.
