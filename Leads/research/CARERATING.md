## M&A-Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A and Capital Structure

**CARE Ratings Limited**  operates as a widely held entity with no identifiable ultimate promoter; marquee institutional shareholders include the  **African Development Bank (AfDB)** ,  **MCB Equity Fund** , and  **SBM (NFC) Holdings** [[1 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a04a8bf00164adc0e7f7339&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7399&hl=fse7399&page=14) . In November 2025, the company approved a strategic partial stake sale in its subsidiary,  **CareEdge Global IFSC Limited** , to the  **State Bank of India (SBI)**  and  **NSE IFSC Limited** , with each acquiring up to 9.9% . [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-370238__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3010&hl=fse3010,fse3202&page=2)  As of May 2026, the company noted that legal documentation for this transaction has taken longer than anticipated . [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-545070__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3577&hl=fse3577&page=2)

### Subsidiary and Group Structure

The group has aggressively expanded its non-ratings and international footprint. Key subsidiaries include  **CARE Analytics and Advisory Private Limited**  (100%),  **CARE ESG Ratings Limited**  (100%), and  **CARE Ratings Nepal Limited**  (51%) . [[4 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a04a8bf00164adc0e7f7339&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6206&hl=fse6206&page=13)  Its African operations are managed via  **CARE Ratings (Africa) Private Limited**  (78%), which in turn holds a 100% stake in the newly incorporated  **CARE Ratings South Africa (Pty) Limited** [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-8277ccfb8bc2743e03ecdf377734ef52&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse185307&hl=fse185307&page=62) .

### Growth Signals and Capital Allocation

Management has explicitly signaled an intent to pursue  **inorganic growth**  to explore synergies, particularly to add new markets for the non-ratings business or to acquire new product capabilities . [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-24377649-1942951461&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse51704&hl=fse51704&page=2)  The company maintains a robust "cash warchest," reporting  **INR 172.75 crore**  in standalone cash and cash equivalents as of March 31, 2026, alongside significant fixed deposits and other financial assets . [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-545070__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse48056&hl=fse48056,fse48101&page=18)  While the dividend payout for FY26 totaled INR 22 per share, management remains focused on utilizing the well-capitalized balance sheet for sustained value creation and expansion . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-545070__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse179032&hl=fse179032&page=73)  No significant activist or formal strategic-review signals were identified in recent filings .
