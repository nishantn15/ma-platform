## M&A Readiness Profile: Castrol India Ltd (NSE: CASTROLIND)

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

The defining development for  **Castrol India**  is the December 2025 announcement by ultimate parent  **BP PLC**  to sell a 65% stake in the global  **Castrol**  business to  **Stonepeak**  for an enterprise value of ~$10 billion. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69a34f1b62cc8a000790d085&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse547535&hl=fse547535&page=107)  This transaction, expected to close by late 2026, will see  **BP**  retain a 35% minority interest. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69a34f1b62cc8a000790d085&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse95703&hl=fse95703&page=32)  The promoter,  **Castrol Limited, U.K.**  (a  **BP**  subsidiary), maintains a steady 51% stake.
