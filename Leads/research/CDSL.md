## M&A-Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity and Structure** CDSL has primarily grown through its three key subsidiaries:  **CDSL Ventures Limited (CVL)**  (100% stake),  **Centrico Insurance Repository Limited**  (54.25% stake), and  **Countrywide Commodity Repository Limited (CCRL)**  (52% stake). [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-d58c9634545b3b806203c402faa70e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse489185&hl=fse489185&page=141)  Recent transaction activity is limited; however, the group maintains a 20% associate stake in  **India International Bullion Holding (IFSC) Limited** , a joint venture supporting the bullion exchange ecosystem. [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69849a470397c500078f9282&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19033&hl=fse19033&page=7)  In April 2026,  **BSE Investments Limited** , a minority shareholder in CCRL, merged into  **BSE Technologies Private Limited** . [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a297fd174d26da334cdbfe6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9005&hl=fse9005&page=8)

**Promoter and Subsidiary Details** **BSE Limited**  remains the ultimate promoter, holding a 15% stake as of March 31, 2026. [[6 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537907__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5398&hl=fse5398&page=10)  Major institutional shareholders include  **Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)**  (4.40%) and various mutual funds. [[7 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537907__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5398&hl=fse5398&page=10)  The subsidiary CCRL is co-owned by the  **Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX)**  and  **BSE Technologies** , both holding 24% stakes. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a297fd174d26da334cdbfe6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9005&hl=fse9005&page=8)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** CDSL operates with a zero-debt balance sheet and maintains a substantial cash warchest, with a reported net worth of INR 1,391.28 crore in July 2025. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-d58c9634545b3b806203c402faa70e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse489622&hl=fse489622&page=141)  Management has signaled intent for inorganic growth, with analysts noting that while historical acquisitions have been at reasonable valuations, there is a "minor risk of overpaying" for future targets as they seek to diversify revenue beyond core depository services. [[10 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-51626b452c5d9d6c124f85cee32a508c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19123&hl=fse19123&page=10)  The company prioritizes technology-led innovation, recently implementing  **T+0 settlement**  and expanding into  **Insurance Ancillary services** . [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-d58c9634545b3b806203c402faa70e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse42287&hl=fse42287&page=15) [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1975f2062908fbafeed90f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20695&hl=fse20695&page=14)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** No active strategic reviews or activist presence were identified in recent filings.  The company’s focus remains on maintaining its 80% market share in demat accounts and scaling new revenue streams in KYC and repository services to offset potential slowdowns in IPO and transaction volumes.
