## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

**Cello World**  has recently completed a significant internal consolidation by merging  **Wim Plast Limited** , which became effective on May 27, 2026. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a219028f34584eb15346b6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9924&hl=fse9924&page=5) [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-417994730-2001816618&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10270&hl=fse10270&page=1)  This merger, following a 54.92% stake acquisition in November 2022 for INR 331.14 crore, aims to unify manufacturing verticals and streamline regulatory compliance. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-2e8768c5547290d02e53122568901410&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse781537&hl=fse781537&page=289) [[5 • ESG- Other topic-specific reports] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a10004dd70f32188a7f825b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9447&hl=fse9447&page=6)  In November 2025, the company acquired the  **Cello**  brand rights for writing instruments from  **BIC Cello** , a move expected to scale the segment's revenue to over INR 500 crore in FY27. [[6 • Anand Rathi Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ANRS-859483ea281c425ad46b3079f11abd61&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse21045&hl=fse21045&page=9) [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a219028f34584eb15346b6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse23038&hl=fse23038&page=8)  Historically, the group also acquired  **Unomax Stationery**  via a slump sale for INR 81.13 crore in November 2022. ### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

**Cello World**  is actively signaling inorganic intent, with management stating they are "preserving cash" and "evaluating inorganic opportunities" as of May 2026.
