## Corporate Structure and M&A Activity

**Cera Sanitaryware**  operates as a standalone entity following a strategic restructuring in late 2025. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-536527__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9284&hl=fse9284&page=5)  On September 29, 2025, the company divested its 51% stakes in  **Packcart Packaging LLP**  and  **Race Polymer Arts LLP**  for a total consideration of INR 18.75 crore. [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-137756-1968064835&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8312&hl=fse8312&page=1)  This followed an earlier exit from  **Milo Tile LLP**  in March 2025, which involved an INR 8.06 crore write-off as part of an amicable dispute settlement. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-536527__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8710&hl=fse8710,fse8814,fse8951&page=5)

The company is led by founder  **Vikram Somany**  (Chairman) and  **Deepshikha Khaitan**  (Vice Chairman), with promoters holding a stable 54.4% stake as of March 2026. [[8 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-675071__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14745&hl=fse14745,fse14781&page=31)  Key institutional investors include  **Nalanda India Equity Fund**  (9.6%) and several domestic mutual funds.
