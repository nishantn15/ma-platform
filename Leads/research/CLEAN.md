## M&A-Readiness Profile: Clean Science and Technology Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Clean Science and Technology has engaged in zero external M&A transactions over the past five years, focusing instead on internal capital reallocation . [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-689ad679c67d850810346e3a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse163315&hl=fse163315&page=64)  As of March 31, 2026, the company operates four wholly owned subsidiaries:  **Clean Fino-Chem Limited**  (CFCL),  **Clean Science Private Limited** ,  **Clean Organics Private Limited** , and  **Clean Aromatics Private Limited** [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-689ad679c67d850810346e3a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse163061&hl=fse163061&page=64) . The promoter group, led by the  **Boob family** , recently reduced its aggregate holding from 75% to 51.29% to facilitate estate planning . [[3 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-f17f369bafd7fdf7c2098759efa0abc2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11618&hl=fse11618&page=5)

> The promoter group plans to reduce its stake from 75% to 51%, with a 24% stake to be divested by a part of the Boob family for estate planning purposes. The company aims to strengthen its growth trajectory by entering higher-grade product segments, expanding its portfolio to align with European standards, increasing wallet share with existing customers, and targeting new international markets such as Vietnam. [[4 • Motilal Oswal • 18 Jul 25 • "1QFY26 Result Update | Sector: Chemicals Clean Science & Technology"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-f17f369bafd7fdf7c2098759efa0abc2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11618&hl=fse11618,fse11773&page=5)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

The company’s capital allocation is heavily skewed toward organic greenfield CAPEX . [[5 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05a84b2a62e81beddf5535&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17446&hl=fse17446&page=21)  In FY2026, it incurred approximately INR 220 crore in CAPEX, primarily directed toward  **Clean Fino-Chem Ltd**  (CFCL) . [[6 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05a84b2a62e81beddf5535&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3089&hl=fse3089&page=5)  Total investment in subsidiaries reached approximately INR 700 crore by January 2026, with a further INR 200 crore infusion approved for CFCL in May 2026 . [[7 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-274077810-1978618229&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5553&hl=fse5553&page=1) [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-550010__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1642&hl=fse1642&page=1)

While management consistently emphasizes "in-house" technological development and "ingenious technologies," there are no active signals for inorganic acquisitions . [[9 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05a84b2a62e81beddf5535&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7177&hl=fse7177,fse7260&page=10)  The company reported cash and cash equivalents of INR 28.20 crore as of March 2026, maintaining a zero-debt status to fund ongoing expansion . [[10 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-c8a523c288f02a8583f9038b9012d294&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1019&hl=fse1019&page=1) [[11 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-697ed3c9867ad4000725a22f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6300&hl=fse6300&page=10)
