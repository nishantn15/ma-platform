## M&A and Corporate Structure Brief

### Recent M&A Activity

CMSINFO ended a four-year deal drought in FY2026 with two strategic acquisitions totaling approximately  **INR 190 crore** . [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551331__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8455&hl=fse8455&page=4)  In  **July 2025** , the company acquired  **Securens Systems** , a Vision AI and remote monitoring specialist, for  **INR 75-80 crore**  to bolster its Technology & Payment Solutions platform. [[5 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-276b4bdc2761edb3ca817aee394c1cf9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2210&hl=fse2210&page=1) [[6 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551331__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3304&hl=fse3304,fse3321&page=8)  This was followed in  **March 2026**  by the  **INR 115 crore**  acquisition of  **FSS’s Managed Services**  business, adding ~8,000 ATM units and expanding its footprint with mid-sized banks. [[7 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69cd84910ec0a62e65733603&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1853&hl=fse1853,fse2053&page=2)

> In Jul-25, it announced acquisition of Securens for Rs800mn to build on its RMS business at ~1x P/Sales vs. ~3x for CMS & sees scope for further M&A opportunities. [[8 • Jefferies Research • 30 Sep 25 • "Analyst Day Takeaways: Moderate Outlook Drive Cuts; Vals Reasonable"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-e709eaeda761989bf9c2d3d71ae6f187&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2586&hl=fse2586&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management maintains a "disciplined" capital allocation strategy, prioritizing organic growth and accretive M&A over capital returns. [[14 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551331__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3157&hl=fse3157&page=8)  The company retains a  **cash warchest of INR 650 crore**  even after announcing its maiden  **INR 168 crore (3%) share buyback**  in May 2026. [[15 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0d872bdc453d98232e14f6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse16189&hl=fse16189,fse16376&page=6)  Inorganic growth is central to its  **FY2030 goal**  of reaching  **INR 3,750-3,950 crore**  in services revenue, with an active pipeline focusing on banking software and specialized logistics. [[16 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551331__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7714&hl=fse7714&page=16) [[17 • IIFL] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-IIFL-7c82f12ebdef9f9ba4b8b17a4e17ddd7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3072&hl=fse3072&page=1)
