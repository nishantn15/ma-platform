## M&A and Strategic Profile Brief

Coal India (CIL) is shifting from a pure-play coal miner to an integrated energy conglomerate via subsidiary value unlocking and large JVs. In early 2026 it listed BCCL (Jan 19, 2026, 10% divested) and CMPDI (Mar 30, 2026, 15% divested; IPO aggregated INR 1,837.84 crore). In May 2026 the Government initiated a 1% OFS. CIL is a Maharatna PSU under the Ministry of Coal; Government of India holds 63.13% (May 2026). Eight wholly/majority-owned coal subsidiaries plus JVs (HURL 30.10%, TFL 39.87%). Cash reserves exceed INR 52,000 crore (Q4 FY26). Inorganic intent: critical-mineral assets (secured Kawalapur REE block), ~INR 80,000 crore capex pipeline over 4-5 years: coal gasification JVs with BHEL/GAIL/BPCL; 1,600 MW DVC JV; 4,100 MW RRVUNL. Strategic review: phased subsidiary listings incl. upcoming MCL IPO. No traditional activist activity. Sources: HSBC (27 Apr 26), ICICI Direct (02 Mar 26), Jefferies (27 Apr 26), Prabhudas Lilladher (29 Apr 26), Motilal Oswal (06 Mar 26), CIL interim reports & offer docs.
