## M&A-Readiness Profile: Coforge Ltd (NSE: COFORGE)

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Coforge has executed a "bold bet" strategy, recently closing the industry’s largest-ever acquisition of  **Encora**  for an enterprise value of  **US$2.35 billion**  (approx. INR 19,622 crore) in May 2026. [[4 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-7e31a2604aea2c8dcb2f879a0dbf249f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse48752&hl=fse48752&page=21)

This followed the April 2026 completion of the  **Cigniti Technologies**  merger, which was finalized through a 1:1 share exchange ratio.  To fund the Encora transaction without further equity dilution, Coforge secured a  **US$550 million**  three-year loan at 4.6% interest, calling off earlier plans for a Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP). [[5 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f9dfbdb0a33bef7fc3f89a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse248&hl=fse248&page=1)

Management’s capital allocation prioritizes "client access" over pure capability buys, targeting assets that are EPS-accretive from the first year. [[6 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-d4c25917000fcdaf426c4742b0dab01d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1662&hl=fse1662,fse2238&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Status

Coforge is a widely held company with  **0% promoter holding**  as of March 2026. [[7 • IIFL] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-IIFL-f178f8c6bcb517d21f0a77171831b03a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse828&hl=fse828&page=1)

Following the exit of Baring Private Equity, the firm operates under a professional board, though the Encora deal granted board seats to  **Advent International**  and  **Warburg Pincus** . [[8 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-7e31a2604aea2c8dcb2f879a0dbf249f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse49102&hl=fse49102&page=21)  The company maintains a complex global structure with approximately  **48 subsidiaries** , including specialized units for Business Process Solutions (80% stake) and the newly integrated Encora and Cigniti entities. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68b096e82e3cd17000dbacdb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse407271&hl=fse407271&page=58)

> Headcount was up q-o-q, however, as Coforge completed the merger of Cigniti and management expects to see its minority shareholding absorbed in May 2026. Encora acquisition is now complete as well. [[10 • HSBC Global Investment Research • 05 May 26 • "Coforge Ltd (COFORGE IN) : Buy: Cleaner and better margin and FCF outlook"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-17164a9e23f8ca07001774bcea448854&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2790&hl=fse2790&page=1)

### Strategic Signals and Inorganic Growth Intent

The firm is undergoing a deliberate  **strategic reset** , which includes exiting a low-margin India-based government/travel project to improve earnings quality and free cash flow (FCF) conversion. [[11 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-1bc9ceeb8679129f6c4e0a9d18b0b993&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3234&hl=fse3234,fse3384&page=1)  Coforge has signaled an intense focus on  **AI-native engineering** , aiming for a US$5 billion revenue run-rate by FY30. [[12 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-682340__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse83469&hl=fse83469&page=169)  Management has upgraded its  **FCF to PAT conversion guidance**  to 100% for FY27, indicating a transition from heavy investment to strong cash generation. [[13 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-1bc9ceeb8679129f6c4e0a9d18b0b993&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1913&hl=fse1913&page=1)

No activist signals were identified; however, integration risks related to the massive Encora deal remain a key point of analyst monitoring. [[14 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-17164a9e23f8ca07001774bcea448854&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse22121&hl=fse22121&page=8)
