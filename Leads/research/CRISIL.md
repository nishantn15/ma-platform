## M&A and Strategic Readiness Profile

**Recent M&A Activity** CRISIL has focused on niche acquisitions to bolster its global benchmarking business. In early 2025, the company completed the acquisition of  **PriceMetrix**  from McKinsey to scale its wealth management and sector benchmarking capabilities for global banks and registered investment advisors . [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24864&hl=fse24864,fse24960&page=14)  Additionally, on June 4, 2025, CRISIL acquired a 4.08% stake in  **Online PSB Loans Limited**  for INR 33.25 crore to expand its digital lending solutions . [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse892179&hl=fse892179&page=318)

**Parent and Subsidiary Structure** CRISIL is majority-owned (66.64%) by  **S&P Global Inc.**  through multiple entities including S&P India, LLC (42.68%) and S&P Global Asian Holdings Pte. Ltd. (15.76%) . [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse691810&hl=fse691810,fse691886,fse691920&page=250)  The company operates through a global network of subsidiaries, including  **Crisil Ratings** ,  **Coalition Greenwich** , and  **Crisil Integral IQ** [[4 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-553999__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20906&hl=fse20906&page=28) . Notable minority shareholders include Rekha Rakesh Jhunjhunwala, who holds 5.19% . [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse908875&hl=fse908875&page=326)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** CRISIL maintains a "zero debt" status and a strong liquidity position, which it explicitly earmarks for financing acquisitions and future growth . [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse94211&hl=fse94211&page=49)  Management’s capital allocation strategy balances high dividend payouts with cash retention for strategic opportunities . [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse94856&hl=fse94856,fse94952&page=49)  Current strategic priorities include the full integration of PriceMetrix and expanding its reach in international markets through domain-led products and  **GenAI**  solutions . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-398744__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse83347&hl=fse83347,fse83514&page=43)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** No activist signals or formal strategic reviews aimed at divestiture were reported. Instead, analysts highlight CRISIL’s attractive risk-reward profile, noting that its diversified process-led research business (less than 10% of revenue) and unique data sets like those of  **Coalition Greenwich**  provide a competitive moat against AI-driven disruption . [[9 • IIFL] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-IIFL-29a8bb0de2e0fcb631746d027c423613&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1901&hl=fse1901,fse2089&page=1)  The company continues to invest in technology to advance its sustainability and analytical objectives .
