### M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Cummins India recently streamlined its operations by divesting its 100% stake in  **Cummins Sales & Service Private Limited (CSSPL)**  to PAL SVAM Power Solutions on April 1, 2025, recording a gain of INR 44.15 crore. [[1 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-538370__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24810&hl=fse24810,fse25023&page=11) [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-5c3cdf093a42bbe32dad205dfffa7e57&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse42812&hl=fse42812&page=27)  On the acquisition side, the company integrated assets from  **First Mode**  in February 2025 to bolster its hybrid powertrain and decarbonization portfolio. [[3 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-698574fe68b7ad000727ce07&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5636&hl=fse5636,fse5778&page=1)

The company is a 51%-owned subsidiary of  **Cummins Inc. USA** . [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-5c3cdf093a42bbe32dad205dfffa7e57&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse719066&hl=fse719066&page=305)  Following the CSSPL sale, its structure primarily consists of high-value joint ventures and associates, notably a 50% stake in  **Valvoline Cummins Private Limited**  and a 48.74% stake in  **Cummins Generator Technologies India Private Limited**  (increased from 48.54% in late 2024 via capital reduction). [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-538370__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24603&hl=fse24603,fse24810&page=11) [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-412689__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9976&hl=fse9976,fse10132&page=4)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

As of March 31, 2026, Cummins India maintains a cash warchest of INR 500.01 crore. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-538370__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse23455&hl=fse23455&page=10)  Management explicitly includes "Mergers & Acquisitions" as a core competency for its board, emphasizing a "build vs. buy" evaluation framework for inorganic growth. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-5c3cdf093a42bbe32dad205dfffa7e57&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse162992&hl=fse162992,fse163209&page=66)  Capital allocation is currently focused on "Destination Zero" initiatives and upgrading manufacturing lines to support 18-19% revenue growth in domestic markets. ### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

Management confirmed that the parent company conducts "continuous assessment" regarding a potential merger between Cummins India and other group entities like Cummins Technologies India Limited (CTIL), though no concrete plans were disclosed as of August 2025. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878459-1954615680&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse32402&hl=fse32402,fse32455&page=2)  There are no reported activist signals.

Brokers remain attentive to the parent's influence on capital allocation.

- **Nuvama Institutional Equities**  noted that any negative commentary regarding potential manufacturing mandate consolidation by the parent could impact the company's addressable market. [[12 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-8d164f68b103c280feccd7d2a1fbbc02&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18422&hl=fse18422&page=8)

- **JPMorgan Research**  highlighted that while data center demand is a strong driver, the current advanced business cycle and macro risks may cap near-term valuation upside.
