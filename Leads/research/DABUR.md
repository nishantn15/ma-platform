## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Dabur’s recent M&A is headlined by the  **INR 587.52 crore**  acquisition of a 51% stake in  **Badshah Masala**  in January 2023, marking a major entry into the branded spices market. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6717d40158117155cf8a102a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1207131&hl=fse1207131,fse1207162&page=503)  Management has since established  **Dabur Ventures** , a dedicated vehicle with an  **INR 500 crore**  outlay to acquire minority or majority stakes in "digital-first" brands within its existing Home and Personal Care (HPC), Healthcare, and Food & Beverage categories. [[4 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-881258-1966859401&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24117&hl=fse24117,fse24243&page=1)  As of March 31, 2026, the company maintains a consolidated cash and cash equivalent balance of  **INR 199.37 crore** . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-548312__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse27921&hl=fse27921&page=10)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Control

The  **Burman family**  remains the ultimate promoter, holding a stable  **66.27%**  stake in the company as of March 2025. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68711b3fb47f8506c4399f9f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse437192&hl=fse437192&page=180)  Dabur operates through  **27 subsidiaries**  globally, including major international hubs like  **Dabur International Limited**  (Isle of Man) and  **Namaste Laboratories**  (USA). [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68711b3fb47f8506c4399f9f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse887889&hl=fse887889&page=341)  Recent structural changes include the conversion of the Dubai branch into  **Dabur International FZE**  in December 2023 and the ongoing liquidation of  **Dabur Tunisie** , expected by December 2025. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6717d40158117155cf8a102a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse565559&hl=fse565559&page=260) [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68711b3fb47f8506c4399f9f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse806526&hl=fse806526&page=311)

### Strategic Signals and Governance

Management has launched a "Vision Refresh" initiative to accelerate growth through premiumization and distribution expansion. [[10 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-7b7f5a2f11585a1327a44a6ba95e011b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18267&hl=fse18267&page=7)

This strategy includes a "two-pronged" M&A approach: utilizing  **Dabur Ventures**  for early-stage digital brands and pursuing larger, "meaningful acquisitions" to pivot toward higher-growth segments. [[11 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-29fbe389616f0ba87e43bf327848c60d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7217&hl=fse7217&page=3)

The appointment of a new India CEO,  **Herjit S. Bhalla** , is viewed by analysts as a signal for greater focus on strategic "big bets" to reverse multi-year stock underperformance. [[12 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-4dc58e09c9e5d4dcc07f8c49de1a6b09&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2641&hl=fse2641,fse2758,fse2874&page=1)  No activist shareholder presence was identified in current research findings.
