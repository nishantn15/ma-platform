## M&A-Readiness Profile: Datamatics Global Services Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity

Datamatics has accelerated its inorganic growth, completing several strategic acquisitions between 2023 and 2025. In April 2023, subsidiary  **Lumina Datamatics**  acquired  **Diacritech Technologies**  for INR 21.20 crore to bolster digital content services. [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-c37a055a5e42b5eccc4978d11e8ec41f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse538491&hl=fse538491,fse538592,fse538820&page=148)  This was followed by the April 2024 acquisition of an 80% stake in  **Dextara Digital**  for approximately INR 33 crore, with the remaining 20% acquired in June 2025 to make it a wholly-owned subsidiary. [[2 • Arihant Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-c050803dc5b239643f2bcb96faac4c1c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3817&hl=fse3817&page=1) [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68b00bdac67d85078c8ca7f6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse54154&hl=fse54154,fse54256,fse54361&page=32)  The most significant recent move was the acquisition of an 80% stake in  **TNQ Tech**  in December 2024, which added high-margin digital content capabilities for scientific publishers.
