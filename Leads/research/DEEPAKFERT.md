## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

In May 2026, DFPCL’s subsidiary  **Deepak Mining Solutions Limited (DMSL)**  completed the 100% acquisition of  **Chardham Chemicals Private Limited (CCPL)**  for INR 121.45 crore to gain manufacturing licenses and strategic assets for its explosives vertical. [[2 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc8d3856f52f0693886db3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse824&hl=fse824&page=1)  This followed the February 2025 consolidation of its Australian subsidiary,  **Platinum Blasting Services (PBS)** , where DMSL increased its stake from 65% to 85% for AUD 11.78 million (approx. INR 64.10 crore) to accelerate growth in the Australian mining market. [[3 • Indian Manufacturing News] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-33178c1e592cfa8d296af64defb05f7aafb7be81&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse186&hl=fse186,fse434&page=1)  Additionally, in July 2024, the company entered a strategic partnership with  **Haifa Group**  to promote high-performance specialty fertilizers.

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is led by Chairman and Managing Director  **Sailesh Mehta** , with the promoter group holding a  **45.63%**  stake as of April 2025. [[4 • REGISTRATION] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-2a25ccebb6c908266590fe298c641ec4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6960&hl=fse6960&page=5)  Following a major NCLT-approved restructuring in FY25, DFPCL operates through three primary verticals:  **Deepak Mining Solutions Limited (DMSL)**  for technical ammonium nitrate (TAN),  **Mahadhan AgriTech Limited (MAL)**  for crop nutrition, and DFPCL (standalone) for industrial chemicals. [[5 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-29569ff4d89be0c2aa38b35c9007e199&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11966&hl=fse11966,fse12094&page=4)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management has signaled a clear intent for inorganic growth to achieve "forward integration" into mining services. [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-4481635-1954466485&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6189&hl=fse6189&page=1)  As of March 31, 2026, the company maintains a cash warchest of  **INR 662 crore** , comprising INR 397 crore in cash and equivalents, INR 139 crore in bank balances, and INR 126 crore in mutual fund investments. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a183f29a7cecefaa2037480&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse77393&hl=fse77393,fse77424,fse77451&page=41)  A significant capital infusion of  **INR 800 crore**  was raised via Compulsory Convertible Debentures (CCDs) from  **Tata Capital**  in FY26 to support these growth initiatives. [[8 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a183f29a7cecefaa203747e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6082&hl=fse6082&page=3)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no activist presence was reported, the company is under a long-term strategic review mandate involving  **McKinsey**  and  **BCG**  to optimize future business strategy. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-543692__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse45639&hl=fse45639&page=47)  Management has explicitly stated that the ultimate goal of the current restructuring is to  **list each business vertical separately**  over the next 2–3 years to unlock shareholder value.
