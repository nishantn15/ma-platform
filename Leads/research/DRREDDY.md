## M&A-Readiness Profile Analysis

### Recent M&A Activity

Dr. Reddy's has accelerated its acquisition pace, most notably acquiring  **Haleon's**  global nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) portfolio (excluding the US) for approximately  **GBP 500 million**  (INR 4,160 crore) in mid-2024. [[3 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-6f33bfc198ef60899dcdf98a83f20b90&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10778&hl=fse10778&page=5)

In October 2025, it acquired the  **Stugeron**  brand from Johnson & Johnson for  **USD 50.5 million**  (INR 422 crore) to bolster its presence in the Asia-Pacific and EMEA regions. [[4 • Elara Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-2354a62689948f118f4c0c9b0a0fef53&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10700&hl=fse10700&page=3)

Recent 2026 activity includes the acquisition of the  **Progynova**  and  **Saccharo-Progynova**  brands from Bayer for the India hormone replacement therapy segment. [[5 • Equirus Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EQRS-9c6836a7c602dbb56dfa13e3926fb56c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9570&hl=fse9570&page=4)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is widely held, with  **promoter holding**  stable at  **26.6%**  as of late 2025. [[6 • Equirus Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EQRS-9c6836a7c602dbb56dfa13e3926fb56c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3887&hl=fse3887&page=1)  The structure includes significant global subsidiaries such as  **Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Inc.**  (US),  **Dr. Reddy's Laboratories SA**  (Switzerland), and  **Dr. Reddy's Laboratories LLC**  (Russia). [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-687f37f2b47f85071e5fb0db&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse831091&hl=fse831091,fse831135,fse831186&page=150)  In May 2024, a structural shift occurred within the promoter group as the APS Trust transferred its  **20.59%**  stake directly to individual promoters Satish Reddy and G.V. Prasad. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-687f37f2b47f85071e5fb0db&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse802278&hl=fse802278&page=143)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management has signaled a clear intent to use its  **INR 3,270 crore**  net cash warchest for "growth rebuild" initiatives. [[9 • Antique Stockbroking] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ANTQ-02a95d42e4d9bb986918d7bc1a7f70fe&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1221&hl=fse1221&page=1)

Capital allocation is prioritizing branded markets (India, EMs) and specialized categories like  **GLP-1s (Semaglutide)**  and  **biosimilars**  to offset a sharp decline in US  **gRevlimid**  sales. [[10 • Equirus Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EQRS-9c6836a7c602dbb56dfa13e3926fb56c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2871&hl=fse2871&page=1)

The company also entered a high-profile nutrition-focused  **joint venture with Nestle India**  in 2024. [[11 • Expert Call] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=EC-050725-185334&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1&hl=fse1)

> Its recent M&A deals such as Haleon's NRT portfolio and the Nestle JV are unlikely to contribute meaningfully to earnings and growth respectively, in the near term. ### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no formal activist presence is reported, the company is undergoing a  **portfolio rationalization** , recently divesting non-core brands and deprioritizing its  **CAR-T**  therapy programs.

Analysts remain cautious, noting that while M&A is a priority, the company's track record in integrating large deals and the long lead times for biosimilars present execution risks.
