## M&A-Readiness Profile: Exide Industries Ltd

### Corporate Structure and Control

Exide Industries is controlled by its promoter,  **Chloride Eastern Limited, UK** , which holds a 45.99% stake as of March 31, 2026 . [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549906__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1058567&hl=fse1058567&page=322)  The company operates through six primary subsidiaries, including 100% owned domestic entities  **Chloride Metals Limited**  (lead recycling) and  **Exide Energy Solutions Limited**  ( **EESL** ), which is the vehicle for its lithium-ion cell manufacturing .  [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549906__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse422304&hl=fse422304&page=115) **Life Insurance Corporation of India**  (LIC) is the only other major shareholder with a 5.27% stake . [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549906__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1058878&hl=fse1058878&page=322)

> EXID's balance sheet is bolstered by substantial cash and cash equivalents of Rs. 7-8bn in FY24, and Rs.50bn as value of the stake in HDFC Life. [[8 • Avendus Spark • 19 Aug 25 • "1QFY26 Outlook review of Exide Industries Rating ADD TP Rs.385"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-05ed00c6acbcb3401727da45cfc70014&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1715&hl=fse1715,fse1797&page=1)

### M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Recent corporate activity has focused on internal equity infusion rather than external M&A. During FY26, Exide invested ₹1,500 crore into  **EESL** , bringing its total equity investment in the lithium-ion business to ₹4,802 crore as of March 2026 . [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549906__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse33097&hl=fse33097&page=11)  Management maintains a  **zero-debt balance sheet**  and holds a substantial "warchest" including a stake in  **HDFC Life**  valued at approximately ₹5,000 crore, providing significant liquidity for its ₹6,000 crore total capex plan for the 12 GWh lithium-ion plant . [[10 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-05ed00c6acbcb3401727da45cfc70014&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1535&hl=fse1535,fse1708&page=1) [[11 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f9fbbc6e86029f8a9ded1e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2509&hl=fse2509&page=4)

> Exide Industries has informed exchanges that it has further invested ₹ 100 crore by way of subscription in the equity share capital of its wholly owned subsidiary, "Exide Energy Solutions Limited" (EESL, executing Li-On cell manufacturing plant), on rights basis. [[12 • ICICI Direct • 25 Feb 26 • "Opening Bell (Feb 25)"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-91ff7ab06467bf19ef3f684125a85439&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14047&hl=fse14047&page=4)

### Strategic Signals

There are no current signals of activist pressure or formal strategic reviews for divestiture. Instead, the company has entered a multi-year technical tie-up with  **SVOLT Energy Technology**  for lithium-ion cell technology . [[13 • Investec Bank plc] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-INVT-a104320ed2e8564b98e30a95c1bd6ce2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1927&hl=fse1927&page=1)  Management's philosophy emphasizes "financial discipline," prioritizing the preservation of cash-generating lead-acid operations while selectively funding future growth through its lithium subsidiaries . [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549906__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse26737&hl=fse26737&page=10)  Inorganic growth appears focused on technology partnerships (like the  **SVOLT**  JV) rather than buying out competitors . [[15 • Investec Bank plc] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-INVT-a104320ed2e8564b98e30a95c1bd6ce2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1927&hl=fse1927&page=1)  Initial commercial production from its new lithium cell lines is targeted for FY27 .
