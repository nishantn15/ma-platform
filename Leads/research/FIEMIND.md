The company recently streamlined its international portfolio by exiting a joint venture in Italy while maintaining a lean, promoter-led structure focused on advanced automotive lighting technologies.

## M&A and Corporate Structure Profile

**Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure** Fiem Industries' recent inorganic activity is characterized by portfolio rationalization rather than aggressive acquisitions. During FY 2024-25, the company exited its 50:50 Italian joint venture,  **Centro Ricerche Fiem Horustech S.r.l.** , recording a nominal proceed of INR 0.01 lakh after the investment had been previously impaired. [[1 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1afc553ad9caf1c2185fe5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28059&hl=fse28059&page=8) [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-307aaefec117ae3f1c0caa121f8bea15&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28348&hl=fse28348,fse28392,fse28519&page=53)  The company maintains two 100% subsidiaries:  **Fiem Industries Japan Co., Ltd.**  (liaison and design) and  **Fiem Research and Technology S.r.l.**  in Italy (automotive lighting design). [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-307aaefec117ae3f1c0caa121f8bea15&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse52405&hl=fse52405&page=57) [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-7362bb71b133fa3d9074ee5657804930&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse57896&hl=fse57896&page=50)  It also holds a 50% stake in  **Fiem Kyowa (HK) Mould Company Limited** . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1afc553ad9caf1c2185fe5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse70079&hl=fse70079&page=21)

**Promoter and Shareholding** The company remains tightly held by the  **Jain family** , led by Chairman and founder  **J.K. Jain** . [[6 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-2728fbf1666c56870e528d2886841e1a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse63006&hl=fse63006,fse63144&page=14)  As of March 31, 2025, the promoter group held a  **58.96%**  stake, a decrease from 66.56% in the prior year following share sales by Rahul and Aanchal Jain. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-307aaefec117ae3f1c0caa121f8bea15&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse408633&hl=fse408633,fse408682&page=163)

**Capital Allocation and Growth Signals** Fiem is positioned for inorganic expansion with a  **cash and cash equivalent balance of INR 276.45 crore**  as of March 31, 2026. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1afc553ad9caf1c2185fe5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28945&hl=fse28945&page=8)  Management has repeatedly signaled a "ready for inorganic growth" stance, stating they are waiting for the right opportunity to react. [[9 • Mirae Asset Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-453097c6dd34ed7eda313d21773b21d9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2282&hl=fse2282&page=1)  Analysts expect the company to generate over  **INR 600 crore**  in free cash flow between FY25-27, further bolstering its warchest. [[10 • Mirae Asset Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-358e06a428c90fde7f7bd403af79ae82&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2422&hl=fse2422&page=1)  While open to technical assistance, management currently sees no immediate need for new JVs for ambient lighting, preferring in-house development or specific technical agreements. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-28009406-1968651013&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34792&hl=fse34792&page=2)  No activist or formal strategic review signals were identified in recent filings.
