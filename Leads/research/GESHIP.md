## M&A and Capital Allocation Profile

### Recent Deal Activity

**Great Eastern Shipping**  is currently focused on fleet modernization through "switch" trades—selling older vessels at cycle peaks and acquiring younger tonnage. [[3 • Ambit Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AMBT-21d0b778377b066560ddb7d36f90ea47&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2588&hl=fse2588&page=1)  In early 2026, the company purchased several secondhand vessels, including the  **Jag Vijay**  (VLGC),  **Jag Riddhi**  (Ultramax), and  **Jag Pranesh**  (MR Tanker). [[4 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0703d845690c8ef83c3998&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse16833&hl=fse16833,fse16880,fse16933&page=25)  Simultaneously, it divested older assets such as the  **Jag Aarati**  and  **Jag Vishnu** . [[5 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0703d845690c8ef83c3998&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17170&hl=fse17170,fse17224&page=25)  Management has also expressed interest in entering the  **container shipping**  segment if asset prices soften from currently elevated levels. [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878168-1967871272&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24187&hl=fse24187,fse24350&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is promoter-led by the  **Sheth family** , who hold a 30.00% stake as of March 2026. [[8 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0703d845690c8ef83c3998&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15885&hl=fse15885&page=22)  Other key subsidiaries include  **The Greatship (Singapore) Pte. Ltd.**  and the newly incorporated  **GESHIPPING (IFSC) Limited**  in GIFT City. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549086__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4285&hl=fse4285&page=3)  There are currently no plans to IPO the offshore subsidiary. ### Inorganic Growth Signals

GESHIP’s primary inorganic signal is its  **INR 7,000.00 crore**  net cash warchest. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878168-1979303592&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19213&hl=fse19213&page=1)  Management has explicitly stated they are "waiting for the right opportunity" to grow the fleet substantially, potentially by 50.00%, once asset prices become more attractive. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878168-1942456818&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse46413&hl=fse46413,fse46527&page=2)  While the company is open to large-scale capital deployment, it prioritizes  **value creation**  over sheer fleet size, maintaining high operating leverage by keeping most tankers on the spot market.
