## M&A-Readiness Profile Brief

### Recent M&A Activity and Structure

Gillette India has recorded  **zero M&A transactions**  over the past five years, maintaining a focused portfolio in grooming and oral care . [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-4a5bcb8b7a86a1c798bbad6f0821912b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse46326&hl=fse46326&page=17) [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6896204f49819147a5529533&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4718&hl=fse4718,fse4788&page=2)  The company is an 75%-held subsidiary of the  **Procter & Gamble Company, USA**  (Ultimate Holding Company), with ownership split between  **Procter & Gamble Overseas India BV**  (40.12%) and  **Gillette Diversified Operations Private Limited**  (34.88%) . [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-4a5bcb8b7a86a1c798bbad6f0821912b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse378017&hl=fse378017&page=133)  As of December 31, 2025, the company explicitly confirmed it has  **no subsidiaries, associates, or joint ventures** [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-405224__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10215&hl=fse10215&page=3) .

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Capital allocation is heavily weighted toward dividends and operational reinvestment. As of March 31, 2026, the company reported cash and cash equivalents of  **INR 266.82 crore** , down from INR 415.45 crore the previous year . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-543809__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse30729&hl=fse30729&page=12)  Significant cash outflows include an interim dividend of  **INR 65 per share**  (approx. INR 211.80 crore) paid in February 2025 and a recommended final dividend of  **INR 60 per share**  for FY26 . [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68999b3e2e3cd156b2eaeb28&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse344076&hl=fse344076&page=124) [[7 • NDTV Profit] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-553c91e134d6078bbfe954cfe43a67e0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1059&hl=fse1059,fse1154&page=1)  Additionally, the Board recommended a  **1:3 bonus share**  issue in May 2026 . [[8 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1fef9b1b3933707eb9a4e9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2758&hl=fse2758&page=1)

Management’s "Integrated Growth Strategy" prioritizes organic "superiority" in products, packaging, and retail execution over acquisitions . [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-4a5bcb8b7a86a1c798bbad6f0821912b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse58645&hl=fse58645&page=24)  Growth is fueled by  **productivity savings**  (INR 40 crore targeted in FY24/25) rather than inorganic expansion . ### Strategic Review and Activism

Research surfaced  **no signals**  of formal strategic reviews, divestiture plans, or activist investor pressure. The company recently transitioned its financial year-end from June 30 to  **March 31**  to align with standard Indian reporting cycles . [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-543809__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11305&hl=fse11305&page=5)  Current strategic focus remains on "constructive disruption" and strengthening market leadership in the blades and razors category .
