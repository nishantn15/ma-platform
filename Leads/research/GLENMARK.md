## M&A-Readiness Brief

**Recent Activity and Structural Evolution** Glenmark recently completed a transformative de-leveraging cycle, primarily through the March 2024 divestiture of its 75% stake in  **Glenmark Life Sciences (GLS)**  to Nirma Ltd for INR 5,651.5 crore. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-4171ddad672ae29aa206d19a2768a5f0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse890789&hl=fse890789,fse891046,fse891171&page=328)  This was followed by a landmark July 2025 out-licensing deal with  **AbbVie**  for the oncology asset ISB 2001, which provided an upfront payment of approximately INR 5,950 crore (USD 700 million). [[4 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-543693__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4825&hl=fse4825&page=4)  In June 2026, the company approved transferring its nebulizer portfolio to its subsidiary,  **Glenmark Healthcare Ltd (GHL)** , for INR 223 crore to streamline operations. [[5 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a26b702ec6b60614214de30&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2737&hl=fse2737&page=2)

**Parent and Subsidiary Structure** The  **Saldanha Family Trust**  remains the ultimate promoter, holding approximately 45.45% of the company as of March 2025, with the broader family reaching ~46.6%. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ac84b73962158f24103902&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1062363&hl=fse1062363&page=374)  Key subsidiaries include  **Ichnos Glenmark Innovation (IGI)** , the firm's global biotech arm, and GHL.  Following the Nirma deal, Glenmark retains a residual minority stake of 7.5% in GLS. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ac84b73962158f24103902&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1141769&hl=fse1141769&page=401)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** Management’s "Glenmark 3.0" strategy emphasizes a shift toward an asset-light, high-margin branded business. [[8 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-eb91e0c088186c61ada6d77a786fecf2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3016&hl=fse3016&page=1)

After years of debt-heavy operations, the company reached a net cash position of INR 1,200 crore by FY26. [[9 • ICICI Securities Limited] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICIC-66a38699c8ee3f2499eb8a39cb7025a7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5422&hl=fse5422,fse5635&page=2)  Strategic priorities for capital include "ROCE-gated" selective M&A and in-licensing global novel assets, particularly in dermatology and respiratory therapies. [[10 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a282b1fca388b425605fdc8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse23298&hl=fse23298&page=37)  Recent in-licensing deals with  **Hengrui Pharma**  and  **Hansoh Pharma**  for oncology assets underscore this trend. [[11 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-543693__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5002&hl=fse5002&page=4)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** No activist investor activity was reported.  However, the company has undergone a self-directed strategic review, resulting in the "Glenmark 3.0" roadmap aimed at achieving 70% of revenue from branded products by FY30. [[12 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-215500cd8963e3206275bfa2671fd6eb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse773&hl=fse773&page=1)  The company is also exploring a potential IPO for IGI to make the unit self-sustaining.
