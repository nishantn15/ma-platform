## M&A Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity** In early 2026,  **Gujarat Gas**  (rebranded as  **Gujarat Energy Limited**  or  **GEL** ) completed a major "Scheme of Arrangement" involving the amalgamation of its parent,  **GSPC** , and the transmission giant  **GSPL**  into itself. [[3 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-713d11095165515352982054b0d7ffbc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse826&hl=fse826&page=1)  Simultaneously, the gas transmission business was demerged into a new entity,  **GSPL Transmission Limited** , effective April 2025. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544023__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse27238&hl=fse27238&page=9)

In September 2025, GEL increased its stake in  **GSPC LNG Limited**  from 14% to 36.8% by converting approximately INR 200 crore of dues into equity, making it a subsidiary. [[5 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-742597bea4c13c86402fc48a0c00180c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7739&hl=fse7739&page=3)

**Structure and Ownership** The company remains under the control of the  **Government of Gujarat** , with a stable promoter holding of 60.89% as of March 2026. [[6 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-713d11095165515352982054b0d7ffbc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse416&hl=fse416&page=1)

Post-merger, GEL operates as an integrated energy major with six primary subsidiaries, including  **GSEG** ,  **GIPL** , and  **GPPC** , alongside a 22.47% joint venture stake in  **Sabarmati Gas Ltd** . [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544023__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse92294&hl=fse92294&page=24) [[8 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-742597bea4c13c86402fc48a0c00180c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10743&hl=fse10743&page=6)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** GEL has signaled a strong intent for inorganic expansion, having appointed  **McKinsey & Company**  as a strategic consultant to evaluate growth opportunities in existing businesses and new energy segments. [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-222471323-2002143497&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13565&hl=fse13565&page=1)

Management highlighted a "cash warchest" exceeding  **INR 5,000 crore**  (including financial assets) and is expected to unveil a formal capital allocation framework by late 2026. [[10 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-742597bea4c13c86402fc48a0c00180c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7982&hl=fse7982&page=3)  The company is also evaluating a potential foray into propane import infrastructure near  **Morbi** . [[11 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-742597bea4c13c86402fc48a0c00180c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6699&hl=fse6699&page=2)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** There are no reported activist signals.

However, a comprehensive  **strategic review**  is underway to transform the entity into an integrated gas player, with a focus on sourcing optimization and digital transformation through AI-enabled analytics and ERP expansion. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-222471323-2002143497&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13206&hl=fse13206&page=1) [[13 • Elara Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-b97556659b1a5d962a3371035982f325&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse168&hl=fse168&page=1)

Analysts identify "aggressive acquisitions of other Gas Authorities" as a key potential upside risk for the company. [[14 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-a2e7f4d4710e23c6731bf6bd19ad9020&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse47076&hl=fse47076&page=22)
