## M&A and Strategic Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity and Deal Sizes** HCLTech has accelerated its inorganic growth through significant acquisitions in the 2025-2026 period. In December 2025, the company signed a definitive agreement to acquire  **Jaspersoft** , a business unit of Cloud Software Group, for approximately INR 2,157 crore ($240 million) to enhance its data intelligence and GenAI capabilities. [[1 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-540318__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12733&hl=fse12733,fse13048&page=5)  Simultaneously, it announced the acquisition of  **Telco Solutions**  from HPE for $160 million (approx. INR 1,341 crore) to bolster its engineering and AI-led network propositions for communication service providers. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69e799392ffe637ea2eb23f2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse72414&hl=fse72414,fse72602&page=24)  Other recent deals include the acquisition of  **Wobby** , an AI-driven platform. [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-777058-1975999660&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24379&hl=fse24379&page=1)  On the divestiture side, HCLTech exited its joint venture with  **State Street International Holdings**  in April 2024, receiving a net consideration of $172 million (approx. INR 1,544 crore). [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6977fc0ab261f00007cc4a5c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse74322&hl=fse74322&page=24)

**Parent and Subsidiary Structure** The company is part of the HCL Group, with  **Vama Sundari Investments (Delhi) Private Limited**  and  **HCL Holdings Private Limited**  acting as key promoter entities that receive significant interim dividends. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6977fc0bb261f00007cc4a62&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse112386&hl=fse112386,fse112473&page=44)  HCLTech operates a complex global network of 131 subsidiaries as of March 2026, including major hubs like  **HCL Software Products Limited** ,  **Actian Corporation** , and  **DWS Pty Limited** . [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69e799392ffe637ea2eb23f2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse162916&hl=fse162916&page=63) [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-362394__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse33809&hl=fse33809&page=15)

**Capital Allocation and Growth Signals** HCLTech exhibits high M&A readiness supported by a "robust cash position" and an annual  **Free Cash Flow (FCF)**  of INR 21,153 crore ($2.5 billion) as of FY25. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6977fc12b261f00007cc4a6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse148783&hl=fse148783,fse148903&page=66)  The company’s capital allocation strategy explicitly prioritizes inorganic growth in high-growth areas such as AI, cybersecurity, semiconductors, and telecom. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6977fc12b261f00007cc4a6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse245585&hl=fse245585&page=91)  Its industry-leading ROIC of 40.3% as of April 2026 provides the financial headroom to finance strategic initiatives without over-reliance on external debt. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6977fc12b261f00007cc4a6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse148903&hl=fse148903&page=66) [[11 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-536486__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6657&hl=fse6657&page=5)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** There are no current signals of activist pressure or formal strategic reviews aimed at a company-wide sale.  Instead, management is focused on an "all-weather portfolio" and "asset-light" operations, using strategic acquisitions to stay nimble during technology cycles like the shift to GenAI.
