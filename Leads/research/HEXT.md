## M&A-Readiness Profile: Hexaware Technologies Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity

**Hexaware Technologies**  has significantly accelerated its inorganic growth since being acquired by the  **Carlyle Group**  in 2021. [[1 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-21fcfaecc28b22b0a8da384dc078d6f3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse30139&hl=fse30139&page=13)  In July 2025, the company acquired  **SMC Squared**  for approximately US$90 million (INR 754 crore) to bolster its Global Capability Center (GCC) expertise. [[2 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-cc69ea26877cd2b4a76c699b5b76f86b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse49652&hl=fse49652&page=18)  This was followed in November 2025 by the acquisition of  **CyberSolve**  for US$150 million (INR 1,250 crore) to strengthen AI-led cybersecurity capabilities. [[3 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-646aa4502356d72ee70340f4c05f36aa&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse22645&hl=fse22645&page=9)  Most recently, in May 2026,  **Hexaware**  entered an agreement to acquire  **Consulting Professionals Services (CPS)**  for up to GBP 11 million (INR 116 crore) to enhance its cloud transformation advisory for FTSE 100 clients. [[4 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0df0872f8454fde685ce4d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse113&hl=fse113&page=1) [[5 • PROXY] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a10e5ef1e133d34f276347d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5609&hl=fse5609&page=3)

### Ownership and Subsidiary Structure

The company is majority-owned by the  **Carlyle Group**  through  **CA Magnum Holdings** , which maintains a 74.49% stake as of March 2026. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc9b41227b5a598f9a503a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse65345&hl=fse65345&page=22)   **Hexaware**  operates a global network of 31 subsidiaries, including major hubs like  **Hexaware Technologies Inc. (USA)**  and  **Hexaware Technologies UK Limited** . [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc9b41227b5a598f9a503a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse46210&hl=fse46210,fse46212&page=14)  It has strategically utilized joint ventures to enter Middle Eastern markets, notably with  **Al-Balagh**  in Qatar (65% stake) and  **Novelty Group**  in the UAE (70% stake). [[8 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-5bb0aaa6226c5ccdd92c6347a19eaf03&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse58479&hl=fse58479&page=25)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management has articulated a "US$3bn by CY29" revenue ambition, requiring a 17.5% CAGR that includes a 2–2.5% annual contribution from inorganic growth through 2–3 acquisitions per year. [[9 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-cc69ea26877cd2b4a76c699b5b76f86b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse50131&hl=fse50131&page=19)  As of March 31, 2026,  **Hexaware**  maintains a robust cash warchest of INR 2,089.10 crore to fund these efforts. [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc9b41227b5a598f9a5035&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6099&hl=fse6099&page=5)  Target areas for future acquisitions include  **ServiceNow** , data analytics, and product engineering, with a geographic focus on Latin America and Eastern Europe. [[11 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-cc69ea26877cd2b4a76c699b5b76f86b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse47349&hl=fse47349,fse47520&page=18)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no formal strategic review is active, brokers note that  **Carlyle**  may look to pare down its 75% stake over the next few years, which remains a key monitorable for investors. [[12 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-cc69ea26877cd2b4a76c699b5b76f86b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse74480&hl=fse74480&page=28)  There are currently no reports of activist investor activity.
