## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

**Hindalco**  has targeted strategic acquisitions to enhance resource security and specialty product portfolios. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-bd303c60fdae0d69903ed9a3f1f740b7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse123878&hl=fse123878&page=47)  In June 2025, the company announced the $125 million (approx. INR 1,050 crore) acquisition of US-based  **AluChem Companies, Inc.**  to enter the low-soda tabular alumina segment. [[4 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-3da17794f45dff892fbe01c9fca8f99e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse854039&hl=fse854039,fse854287&page=160)  In December 2025, it completed the acquisition of  **EMIL Mines and Mineral Resources Limited (EMMRL)**  from  **Essel Mining** , securing the  **Bandha**  coal mine to provide a 45-year fuel supply for its  **Mahan**  smelter. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-552621__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse77373&hl=fse77373&page=25) [[6 • Transcript - Prepared Remarks & Follow Ups] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-ET-68368e61c67d8507b2493985&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19431&hl=fse19431&page=11)  Previous activity includes the FY22 acquisitions of the  **Kuppam**  extrusion unit and  **Birla Copper Asoj** . [[7 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-3da17794f45dff892fbe01c9fca8f99e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1527967&hl=fse1527967&page=275)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is part of the  **Aditya Birla Group** , with promoter holding at approximately 26.96% as of March 2025, primarily through  **IGH Holdings**  (15.58%) and  **Birla Group Holdings**  (11.38%). [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68882b352e3cd13bb01cef33&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1551168&hl=fse1551168,fse1551235&page=264)   **Hindalco**  operates a vast global network of 61 subsidiaries, most notably  **Novelis Inc.** , the world’s largest aluminum recycler. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-552621__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse54751&hl=fse54751&page=18) [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-566b6cee93a2b25b07024cf34c23fc6e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse87128&hl=fse87128,fse87161&page=38)  Key domestic subsidiaries include  **Utkal Alumina International**  and  **Minerals and Minerals Limited** . [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-bd303c60fdae0d69903ed9a3f1f740b7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1290173&hl=fse1290173&page=233)

**Hindalco**  is executing a $10 billion growth capex plan (FY26–FY29) funded by robust cash flows (INR 8,762 crore generated in H1 FY26) and a strong balance sheet.
