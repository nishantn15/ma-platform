## M&A-Readiness Profile: Hindustan Unilever (HINDUNILVR)

### Recent M&A Activity

HUL has executed significant portfolio rotations over the past 24 months. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a200be5a444c863d862b2b5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse161135&hl=fse161135,fse161296,fse161483,fse161624&page=82)  In April 2025, it completed the  **₹2,706 crore**  acquisition of a 90.5% stake in  **Minimalist**  (Uprising Science), a digital-first skincare brand. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a200be5a444c863d862b2b5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1245348&hl=fse1245348&page=442)  In February 2026, the board approved acquiring the remaining 49% of  **OZiva**  (Zywie Ventures) for  **₹824 crore** , following a successful majority stake period where the brand scaled 4x in three years. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a200be5a444c863d862b2b5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1251242&hl=fse1251242&page=445) [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544024__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse164039&hl=fse164039&page=44)  Conversely, HUL completed the demerger of its ice cream business (Kwality Wall's) on December 1, 2025, and divested its 19.8% stake in  **Nutritionalab**  for  **₹307 crore**  in March 2026. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1edbc0834ad0af33d2d67d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse246541&hl=fse246541&page=52)

### Corporate Structure and Parentage

The ultimate parent is  **Unilever PLC** , which holds a 47.4% direct stake; total promoter holding stands at 51.9% as of March 2026. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a200be5a444c863d862b2b5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1133491&hl=fse1133491,fse1133550&page=399) [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544024__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1134986&hl=fse1134986&page=202)  The subsidiary network includes core entities like  **Unilever India Exports**  and  **Lakme Lever** , alongside newly integrated "market maker" platforms like  **Minimalist**  and  **OZiva** . [[10 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878395-1954488968&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3746&hl=fse3746&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

HUL maintains a standalone cash balance of  **₹2,286 crore**  and generated  **₹10,496 crore**  in cash from operations in FY26. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a200be5a444c863d862b2b5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse857313&hl=fse857313&page=303) [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544024__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse164202&hl=fse164202,fse164213&page=44)  Management explicitly signals an intent to pursue "bolt-on" acquisitions that align with premiumization and digital-first growth spaces. [[13 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-9d5dd60e742245de350d3fb9a54a505f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8406&hl=fse8406&page=3) [[14 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878395-1996466109&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse21112&hl=fse21112&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

A comprehensive strategic review led to the ice cream demerger to unlock value and increase agility. [[15 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-67494bb971c0873bfc34f1a4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6046&hl=fse6046&page=16)  While the global parent (Unilever PLC) announced a massive foods divestment in April 2026, HUL clarified that its Indian food business remains ring-fenced due to its distinct high-growth profile and local relevance. [[16 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-d4e3e427126b5a15de7a0e0d272d99d5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse750&hl=fse750&page=1)  No material activist presence was identified; however, the board holds regular strategy sessions to evaluate further category separations. [[17 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1edbc0834ad0af33d2d67d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse597504&hl=fse597504&page=109)
