## M&A-Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure** Honeywell Automation India Limited (HAIL) has not engaged in any acquisitions, divestitures, or joint ventures over the last five years.  The company operates as a zero-subsidiary entity, confirming in its August 2025 and November 2025 financial reports that it does not have any subsidiary, associate, or joint venture companies. [[1 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68a318f8581171769299fa16&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8386&hl=fse8386&page=5)  The corporate structure is highly centralized under its promoter,  **HAIL Mauritius Limited** , which holds a 75.00% stake in the company. (USA)** . [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68475fc471c087078cfe744b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse453605&hl=fse453605&page=136)

**Capital Allocation and Liquidity** As of March 31, 2025, HAIL possesses a substantial "cash warchest" totaling  **INR 3,130.40 crore**  (reported as INR 31,304 million in cash and cash equivalents). [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-4d71d69621d1bece5d0c6862a4d506e4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse394225&hl=fse394225&page=119)  This liquidity has grown steadily from INR 2,822.40 crore in March 2024. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68475fc471c087078cfe744b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse393925&hl=fse393925&page=118)  The company’s capital allocation is primarily focused on organic operations, dividend payments (INR 88.40 crore paid in FY2025), and fulfilling lease liabilities. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-4d71d69621d1bece5d0c6862a4d506e4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse394049&hl=fse394049,fse394092&page=119)  Its cash is maintained in current accounts and demand deposits with original maturities of less than three months, providing immediate deployability. While the Audit Committee’s terms of reference include reviewing the rationale for schemes involving mergers or demergers, no such transactions are currently underway.
