## M&A-Readiness Profile: Hyundai Motor India Ltd (HMIL)

### Recent M&A Activity and Structure

Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL) is a majority-owned subsidiary of South Korea's  **Hyundai Motor Company (HMC)** , which maintains an  **82.50%**  promoter holding following the company's 2024 IPO. [[2 • PROSP] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-SBI-68a005f446ba374d4ede1baea5493591&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse97036&hl=fse97036,fse97072,fse97087,fse97107,fse97124,fse500719,fse500817,fse500883,fse500919,fse500931,fse500951,fse501013,fse503465,fse503485,fse503969,fse1004151&page=22) [[3 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-e767bb683eae6528d630ccedf6671268&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25840&hl=fse25840,fse25915&page=12)  The company operates through two wholly-owned subsidiaries:  **Hyundai Motor India Engineering Private Limited**  (R&D focus) and  **Hyundai India Insurance Broking Private Limited** . [[4 • PROSP] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-SBI-8309ac43a49578200a22bee65897c42f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1443801&hl=fse1443801&page=365)

Inorganic activity has primarily focused on manufacturing infrastructure rather than corporate acquisitions. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6892b3302e3cd13f2dd27b6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse79404&hl=fse79404&page=28)  A defining transaction was the  **2023 acquisition**  of the  **Talegaon plant**  from General Motors, which began commercial operations in October 2025 and is set to scale capacity to 250,000 units by 2028. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6892b3302e3cd13f2dd27b6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse79868&hl=fse79868&page=28) [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-405732__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19012&hl=fse19012&page=9)  Recent minority activity includes a  **26.13% stake**  acquisition in  **FPEL TN Wind Farm Private Limited**  in June 2025 to support renewable energy goals. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6892b3302e3cd13f2dd27b6a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse160502&hl=fse160502&page=47)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

HMIL maintains a significant cash warchest of  **₹4,504.29 crore**  (as of June 2024). [[9 • PROSP] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-SBI-8309ac43a49578200a22bee65897c42f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse332091&hl=fse332091,fse1152036&page=75)  Management has signaled strong inorganic intent through the  **Hyundai Innovation Challenge 2026** , which offers startups proof-of-concept grants and potential for long-term strategic investment in EVs, AI, and ADAS. [[10 • Indiantelevision.com] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-7599d309e1ee84f6cbccd0af058bacf882c946bf&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse499&hl=fse499&page=1)  While no major corporate M&A is currently under public discussion, management continues to evaluate opportunities in high-growth, synergistic sectors. [[11 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-ad18effe04580e8b164d927f73e0eab0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse111542&hl=fse111542&page=45)  Capital expenditure is heavily geared toward the EV transition, with a planned  **₹7,500 crore**  push in FY27 for manufacturing upgrades and a localized dedicated EV pipeline. [[12 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-2866497a5cdd043c28d0c538c567faaf&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8266&hl=fse8266&page=3) [[13 • Indian Manufacturing News] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-331e1ab305f8df0e518a0d2c68d996be091b3aab&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse170&hl=fse170&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are currently no signals of activist presence or formal "for sale" strategic reviews. [[14 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-ad18effe04580e8b164d927f73e0eab0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse111542&hl=fse111542&page=45)  Strategic focus remains on regaining market share through a "product offensive," particularly in the SUV segment where it faces stiff competition from Tata Motors and Mahindra & Mahindra. [[15 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-2866497a5cdd043c28d0c538c567faaf&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18806&hl=fse18806&page=8) [[16 • Motown India] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-86f6a10f4c20edbca263ab95c6376e82&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse166&hl=fse166&page=1)

Performance is highly leveraged to its parent's global technology, paying approximately 3% of sales in royalties for access to global innovation. [[17 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-a71af625d144bf4496ebafc065d43354&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13285&hl=fse13285&page=6)
