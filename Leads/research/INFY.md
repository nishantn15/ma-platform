## M&A-Readiness Profile: Infosys Ltd (NSE: INFY)

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Infosys has significantly increased its M&A velocity, closing or announcing several multi-billion rupee deals in late FY26 and early FY27. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a19c73464b7cfcb6276d78e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse148462&hl=fse148462,fse148611&page=39)  The largest recent transaction is the acquisition of  **Optimum Healthcare IT**  for approximately ₹4,410 crore ($465 million), which closed in May 2026 to strengthen its healthcare digital transformation presence. [[5 • Supplemental Financials] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2081d34429869c4b28b20a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3247106&hl=fse3247106,fse3247605&page=1140)  Other notable deals include the ₹3,228 crore acquisition of  **in-tech Holding GmbH**  in July 2024 to enhance Engineering R&D, and the ₹901 crore acquisition of  **Stratus Global LLC**  in April 2026 to expand insurance consulting capabilities. [[6 • Supplemental Financials] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2081d34429869c4b28b20a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3247670&hl=fse3247670,fse3248175&page=1140)

Management has signaled a clear inorganic intent to fill capability gaps in "high-value" areas like AI strategy, cybersecurity, and cloud-native services. [[7 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-398006-1961160189&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse26069&hl=fse26069&page=2)  While the company continues to return roughly 85% of free cash flow to shareholders through dividends and buybacks—including an ₹18,000 crore buyback completed in late 2025—its debt-free status and ₹22,201 crore cash reserve provide ample liquidity for further bolt-on acquisitions. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a19c73464b7cfcb6276d78e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1008070&hl=fse1008070&page=310) [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-398006-1974297816&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9361&hl=fse9361,fse9513&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

As of March 31, 2026, the promoter group holds 14.38% of equity, with  **Sudha Gopalakrishnan**  (2.35%) as the largest individual promoter shareholder. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a19c73464b7cfcb6276d78e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1049561&hl=fse1049561&page=322)  The company is widely held, with institutional investors like  **LIC of India**  (10.67%) and  **Vanguard**  (3.88%) maintaining significant stakes. [[11 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-fc01a5cc4f53d1cc35474e1445e33302&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19261&hl=fse19261&page=8)  Infosys operates through 30 direct and 84 step-down subsidiaries, including major entities such as  **Infosys BPM**  and  **Infosys Consulting Holding AG** . [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a19c73464b7cfcb6276d78e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse150361&hl=fse150361&page=39)  Minority interests are present in strategic joint ventures, most notably a 25% stake held by  **ABN AMRO**  in  **Stater N.V.**  and a 40% stake held by  **Temasek**  in  **Infosys Compaz** .
