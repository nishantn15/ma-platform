## M&A and Strategic Growth Brief

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Ion Exchange (India) has focused on localized joint ventures and technology partnerships rather than large-scale outright acquisitions. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-689d979171c08707b402b07f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse48817&hl=fse48817,fse48852&page=18)  In May 2026, the company entered a  **Project JV**  in  **Malawi**  valued at approximately USD 18.1 million (INR ~151 crore) for water treatment infrastructure. [[4 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a198a453e9f188bef7bbde7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7330&hl=fse7330&page=7)  It also operates a JV in  **Oman**  to recognize consolidated revenue from regional projects. [[5 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20345316-2001290755&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41197&hl=fse41197&page=2)  A strategic technology-sharing partnership with  **Mann+Hummel**  was established in November 2025, focusing on membrane manufacturing and global distribution without a formal JV equity structure. [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20345316-1968419545&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25691&hl=fse25691,fse25721&page=1)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is promoter-led, with the  **Rajesh Sharma**  family and associates holding a 25.75% stake as of March 2025. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fd1612437c0fc37f57c839c7a4509e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse423036&hl=fse423036&page=145)  The group maintains 20 subsidiaries and associates, including wholly-owned entities in  **Portugal (Mapril)** ,  **USA** , and  **Singapore** . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fd1612437c0fc37f57c839c7a4509e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse584665&hl=fse584665&page=202) [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-697c9594dd55d600079803a4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15680&hl=fse15680&page=6)  Notable investment subsidiaries include  **Aqua Investments**  and  **Watercare Investments** , which both hold stakes in the parent company. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fd1612437c0fc37f57c839c7a4509e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse422921&hl=fse422921,fse422976&page=145)

### Inorganic Growth and Strategic Signals

Management has signaled a strong intent for inorganic growth through "strategic local partnerships" to strengthen market access in  **Southeast Asia** , the  **Middle East** , and  **Europe** . [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fd1612437c0fc37f57c839c7a4509e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse49537&hl=fse49537&page=19)  The company holds a substantial cash warchest, including INR 149.29 crore in cash and INR 316.51 crore in other bank balances as of March 2025. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fd1612437c0fc37f57c839c7a4509e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse686864&hl=fse686864&page=240)  Capital is being aggressively reinvested into capacity enhancements, such as the  **INR 400 crore Roha plant expansion** . [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20345316-1954904886&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41338&hl=fse41338&page=2)  While no activist signals were identified, a 2024-2025 restructuring process aimed at expanding distribution and system improvements indicates an ongoing internal strategic review to optimize for global scale. [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fd1612437c0fc37f57c839c7a4509e20&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse60150&hl=fse60150&page=21)
