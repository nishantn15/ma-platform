IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd (IRB) is positioned as a highly active M&A player in the Indian infrastructure sector, transitioning from a traditional developer to a sponsor-led asset manager. The company employs a "B.E.S.T." (Bid, Execute, Stabilize, Transfer) strategy to recycle capital from mature assets into new high-yield concessions, primarily through its two Infrastructure Investment Trust (InvIT) platforms.

## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Asset Rotation

IRB is executing an aggressive asset-rotation strategy. In November 2025, the company facilitated the transfer of three BOT assets from its  **IRB Infrastructure Trust**  (Private InvIT) to its  **IRB InvIT Fund**  (Public InvIT) for an enterprise value of INR 8,436 crore, unlocking INR 4,905 crore in equity. [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-417119__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse43681&hl=fse43681&page=14)  Additionally, in December 2025, IRB sold its  **VM7 Expressway**  subsidiary for INR 513 crore to the Public InvIT. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-557622__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse40138&hl=fse40138&page=15)  Most recently, in May 2026, the Private InvIT offered two more BOT assets (Solapur Yedeshi and CG Tollway) to the Public InvIT with an enterprise value of INR 4,663 crore. [[4 • PROXY] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a06186d15448e68bec10598&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1898&hl=fse1898,fse1419&page=1)

> The InvIT more than doubled its market cap and made itself a high growth InvIT via M&As, while its toll revenue growth accelerated to 15% YoY (same store basis (SSB)) vs 1HFY26 5% YoY driving Ebitda growth 51.6% YoY. Its reported Ebitda grew 51.6% YoY after it concluded the M&A of three high-growth tollways which, not only lifted its weighted average life from 14 to 17 years, but the InvIT to a high growth asset. ### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is led by Chairman  **Virendra D. Mhaiskar** , with the promoter group holding 34.39% as of March 2024. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66d9db7858117130668f13c7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1798&hl=fse1798&page=2)  Significant minority partners include  **Cintra**  (Ferrovial) and  **GIC** , who hold roughly 24.86% and 16.94% stakes respectively. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-91e625aab26f86c9cd8145d79cb69a0c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse794027&hl=fse794027,fse794095&page=149)  IRB operates through a complex structure of 20 direct and indirect subsidiaries, including its EPC arm  **Modern Road Makers Private Limited** . ### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

IRB's capital allocation focuses on reinvesting unlocked equity into new  **Toll-Operate-Transfer (TOT)**  and  **Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT)**  projects. [[13 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-417119__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse45182&hl=fse45182,fse45350&page=15) [[14 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-37592988-1983752602&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1805&hl=fse1805&page=1)  Management aims to expand the group asset base from INR 940 billion to INR 1.4 trillion by FY2029 while targeting a net-debt-free balance sheet at the holdco level by FY2030.
