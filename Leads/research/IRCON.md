## M&A-Readiness Profile: IRCON International Ltd

### Recent M&A and Corporate Structure

**IRCON International**  is a  **Government of India**  (GoI) PSU, with the President of India holding a  **65.17%**  stake as of March 2025. [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-829d70cd69a0a7ed9ca16c3a441bf531&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12383&hl=fse12383&page=11)  The GoI divested an  **8.01%**  stake via an Offer for Sale (OFS) during FY24 to meet public shareholding requirements. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-829d70cd69a0a7ed9ca16c3a441bf531&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1018000&hl=fse1018000&page=334)  The group structure comprises  **11 subsidiaries**  (mostly 100%-owned SPVs for highway projects) and  **7 joint ventures** . [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-829d70cd69a0a7ed9ca16c3a441bf531&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12682&hl=fse12682&page=11)

Recent transactional activity is defined by strategic exits and new sector entries. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68ac311b71c087374e2dc81e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse85538&hl=fse85538&page=39)  In November 2024,  **IRCON**  initiated the voluntary liquidation of its JV,  **Indian Railway Stations Development Corporation (IRSDC)** , expecting to recover approximately  **INR 60.34 crore** . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-672d26ab2e3cd1691c9d3123&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13304&hl=fse13304,fse13591&page=4)  Conversely, it committed  **INR 370.80 crore**  to  **Ircon Renewable Power Limited (IRPL)** , a 76:24 JV with  **Ayana Renewable Power**  (a subsidiary of  **NIIF** ), to develop a 500 MW solar plant. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68ac311b71c087374e2dc81e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse799071&hl=fse799071&page=263) [[7 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-10438100-1889635984&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29457&hl=fse29457&page=2)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

**IRCON**  maintains a strong liquidity position with total cash and bank balances of  **INR 4,124 crore**  as of March 31, 2025, though its "own cash" (excluding client advances) is approximately  **INR 950 crore** . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-829d70cd69a0a7ed9ca16c3a441bf531&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse95878&hl=fse95878&page=42) [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-10438100-2001073491&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14516&hl=fse14516&page=1)  Management has signaled a clear intent for  **inorganic growth**  through business diversification into  **renewable energy**  and  **hydro power** . [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-829d70cd69a0a7ed9ca16c3a441bf531&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse104183&hl=fse104183,fse104295&page=44)  The company also plans to invest  **INR 880 crore**  over the next 2–3 years in its road and infrastructure SPVs. [[11 • Equirus Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EQRS-70978d3604569ef6aac24f98e849a66a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2379&hl=fse2379&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activism

While no formal activist presence is reported, the  **Ministry of Railways**  exercises total control over board appointments, leading to some regulatory friction regarding the number of independent directors. [[12 • PROXY] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fd0a4171f2962231856b23&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2114&hl=fse2114,fse2399&page=1)  A key monitorable is the management's decision to  **monetize 100%-owned subsidiaries**  as they become operational and profitable, moving toward a "project development and operation" model. [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-10438100-1943481740&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11352&hl=fse11352&page=1) [[14 • Business Line (India)] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-3468f7673281758bba166efe10836bb60637b3ee&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse187&hl=fse187&page=1)   **Prabhudas Lilladher**  notes that  **IRCON**  has a strong order book of  **INR 25,000 crore** , providing 2.5–3 years of execution visibility. [[15 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-8689d6cdd91c5b4283f754a2e09e45eb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3407&hl=fse3407&page=1)
