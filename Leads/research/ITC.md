## ITC Ltd M&A-Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

ITC has transitioned from a historical focus on organic growth to a proactive  **M&A-led expansion** .  Major recent moves include the  **100% acquisition**  of  **Blazeclan Technologies**  for approximately  **INR 416 crore**  in October 2024 to bolster its cloud capabilities and the acquisition of  **Sresta Natural Bioproducts**  (24 Mantra brand) for  **INR 285 crore**  in 2025. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-685ec49ac67d8507b0e485cb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse810849&hl=fse810849&page=242) [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549157__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20061&hl=fse20061&page=8)  Other key strategic entries include  **Sproutlife Foods**  (Yoga Bar) and  **Mother Sparsh**  in the health and baby care segments. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-685ec49ac67d8507b0e485cb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13336&hl=fse13336,fse13373&page=15)  In March 2025, it signed an agreement to acquire the  **Century Pulp & Paper**  division from Aditya Birla Real Estate to scale its paperboard business. [[9 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-67eacc07c67d854e0f0a4436&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7492&hl=fse7492,fse7601&page=5)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Status

ITC is a  **widely held**  conglomerate with no identifiable "promoter" group. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-685ec36ac67d8507b0e485be&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse948747&hl=fse948747&page=287)  Significant institutional shareholders include  **British American Tobacco (BAT)**  (c. 19-20%) and  **LIC**  (c. 15-16%). [[12 • CFRA Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-CFRA-a3afbe660e8a0c90e8723d745f9a9edc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7320&hl=fse7320&page=2)  As of March 2026, ITC manages over  **40 subsidiaries**  across FMCG, Agri, and IT segments. [[13 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549157__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse74477&hl=fse74477&page=24)

A major structural shift occurred on  **January 1, 2025** , with the demerger of its  **Hotels business**  into  **ITC Hotels Ltd** , in which ITC retains a  **40% associate stake** .
