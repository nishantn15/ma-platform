Jamna Auto Industries Ltd (EQJAMNAAUTO.IN) is positioning itself for a new phase of growth through its "Lakshya Rise 5000" vision, supported by a stable promoter structure and a concentrated group of core subsidiaries. While major inorganic acquisitions have been absent over the last five years, the company's shift toward becoming a full-suspension system supplier and its expansion into export markets signal potential for future strategic moves.

> With the aim to diversify its business model, Jamna Auto has outlined its Lakshya Rise 5000 vision wherein its intent is to achieve ₹ 5,000 crores sales (~2x FY26E run-rate), New Markets (Indian After Market +Export) to be 40% of revenue (currently 23%), deliver 40% ROCE (currently ~30%) and 50% Dividend payout. ### M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Jamna Auto's M&A activity has been minimal in the last five years, with no major acquisitions or divestitures of business units reported as of late 2025. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68d2a11f71c08707de2d42d2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse82949&hl=fse82949&page=27)  In February 2026, the company approved a small strategic investment of approximately INR 0.82 crore to acquire a stake of up to 26% in  **Fourth Partner Energy Private Limited**  for captive renewable energy procurement. [[3 • PROXY] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-698f0f9bb2b79d00072b678f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1186&hl=fse1186&page=1)  The company remains under the leadership of the  **Jauhar family** , who hold a 49.95% stake in the entity. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-74b5ae3e9c015403986cfcc8c95689fb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse661845&hl=fse661845&page=224)  Its operational structure consists of three primary subsidiaries:  **Jai Suspensions Limited**  (100%),  **Jai Automotive Components Limited**  (100%), and  **Jai Suspension Systems Private Limited**  (99.99%). ### Strategic Outlook and Capital Allocation

The company has signaled a clear intent for inorganic and organic growth through its  **Lakshya Rise 5000**  vision, which aims to double revenues to INR 5,000 crore, achieve a 40% ROCE, and maintain a 50% dividend payout. [[6 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-a80c2bf6eb83d7926380e9f6204381b4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2032&hl=fse2032&page=1)  As of March 31, 2026, the company maintains a cash warchest of INR 65.50 crore, comprising INR 61.40 crore in cash and equivalents and INR 4.10 crore in other bank balances. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a195042f736910006b1e485&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25114&hl=fse25114,fse19948&page=13)  Strategic growth is focused on diversifying from traditional leaf springs into trailer suspensions and lift axles, highlighted by a recent export agreement with  **Stellantis Group**  to expand its global OEM footprint.
