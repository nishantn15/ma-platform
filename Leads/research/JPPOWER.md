## M&A-Readiness Profile: Jaiprakash Power Ventures Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

**Jaiprakash Power Ventures (JPPOWER)**  is the subject of intense M&A interest as of May 2026 .  [[3 • Newsx] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-53b963e0b6208555747991fe9b245699&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1&hl=fse1) **Adani Power**  entered a definitive agreement in May 2026 to acquire a  **24% stake**  from  **Jaiprakash Associates Limited (JAL)**  for  **INR 2,993.59 crore** [[4 • NDTV Profit] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-3f96d57f8633e913c6219d75c671360b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse789&hl=fse789,fse983&page=1) . Historically, JPPOWER divested its  **Baspa II**  and  **Karcham Wangtoo**  hydro projects to  **JSW Energy**  for  **INR 9,275 crore**  in 2015 to reduce debt . [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d6c6377d565da473af84c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse351928&hl=fse351928&page=137)  More recently, a 2020 debt resolution with JSW Energy saw  **INR 351.77 crore**  of debt converted into a ~5% equity stake . [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d6c6377d565da473af84f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse193862&hl=fse193862,fse193918&page=81)  As of March 31, 2026, the company maintains a cash balance of approximately  **INR 754.95 crore** [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551378__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse22982&hl=fse22982&page=9) .

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Environment

The company is currently an associate of  **Jaiprakash Associates Limited (JAL)** , which holds  **24%**  of the equity . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-f09da4a2ea0c8acb6d5ad939449222ae&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse740436&hl=fse740436&page=180)  JPPOWER operates through four wholly owned subsidiaries:  **Jaypee Arunachal Power** ,  **Sangam Power Generation** ,  **Jaypee Meghalaya Power** , and  **Bina Mines and Supply** [[9 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-a5a3380d6e908388477d68c6efc4a44b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7951&hl=fse7951&page=4) . However, several subsidiaries face material uncertainty regarding their "going concern" status due to eroded net worth . [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551378__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse136101&hl=fse136101&page=43)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

The company is under significant strategic pressure due to the insolvency of its promoter, JAL . [[11 • Financial Services Monitor Worldwide] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-47c5c69488f9de2dda294605b6c61f9bb411cc3f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse860&hl=fse860&page=1)  Lenders, led by  **ICICI Bank** , are exploring the sale of  **INR 3,800 crore**  in Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS), which could grant a buyer an additional  **25% stake**  and trigger an open offer for majority control . [[12 • Financial Services Monitor Worldwide] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-47c5c69488f9de2dda294605b6c61f9bb411cc3f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse968&hl=fse968,fse1069&page=1)  Activist and legal signals are high; the  **National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited (NARCL)**  filed for insolvency proceedings against JPPOWER in early 2026 over corporate guarantees provided to JAL . [[13 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-551378__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse26629&hl=fse26629&page=10)  Furthermore, the  **Enforcement Directorate (ED)**  arrested a key promoter figure in November 2025, heightening governance risks .
