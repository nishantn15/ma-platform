## M&A-Readiness Profile: Jubilant Pharmova Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Jubilant Pharmova has been highly active in optimizing its portfolio to align with its  **Vision 2030**  objectives. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e4ac6d47dfbcb89fb3f217bfdacab92e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse67377&hl=fse67377&page=23)  In May 2024, the company successfully exited its 25.8% stake in  **Sofie Biosciences Inc.**  for approximately USD 143.20 million (~INR 1,224 crore), significantly strengthening its balance sheet. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e4ac6d47dfbcb89fb3f217bfdacab92e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse925365&hl=fse925365,fse925415&page=177)  Internally, the company executed a slump sale of its  **API business**  (valued at INR 595.60 crore) to its wholly-owned subsidiary,  **Jubilant Biosys Limited** , effective September 1, 2025, to integrate drug discovery and API manufacturing into a single CRDMO platform. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-422719__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse49576&hl=fse49576,fse49924&page=19)

The group's corporate structure is complex, comprising 40 subsidiaries as of March 2026. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-422719__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17252&hl=fse17252&page=7)  Promoters, primarily through the  **Bhartia family trusts** , hold a 47.70% stake. [[8 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-422719__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse44616&hl=fse44616&page=82)  Key acquisitions include an 80% stake in  **Jubilant Biosys France**  (formerly a Pierre Fabre R&D center) in March 2025 for Euro 4.4 million to expand European biologics and ADC capabilities. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68923c79581171619de11b9d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse969458&hl=fse969458,fse969716&page=185) [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e4ac6d47dfbcb89fb3f217bfdacab92e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse165967&hl=fse165967&page=54)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Growth Signals

Management's capital allocation is currently dominated by organic capex (INR 1,668 crore in FY2026) directed toward expanding capacity in  **Spokane**  and  **Montreal** . [[11 • CIRC] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a16f63f7c7f42991b196f70&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17591&hl=fse17591&page=8)  However, inorganic signals remain strong: the company is actively pursuing "in-licensing" of new products to build its branded generics business in  **India**  and international markets. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e4ac6d47dfbcb89fb3f217bfdacab92e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse176209&hl=fse176209&page=57)  With a cash position of INR 1,088.30 crore as of March 2025, the company used Sofie sale proceeds to reduce leverage and fund growth. [[13 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68923c79581171619de11b9d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse862342&hl=fse862342&page=161) [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e4ac6d47dfbcb89fb3f217bfdacab92e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse937033&hl=fse937033&page=181)

> Revenue outperformance was primarily led by exceptional growth in CDMO-Sterile Injectables (up 57.4% YoY), aided by faster- than-expected ramp-up of Spokane Line-3 and technology transfer revenues, including onboarding of one of the world's largest oncology products. [[15 • Systematix Institutional Research • 25 May 26 • "RESULT UPDATE : Jubilant Pharmova Ltd : CDMO Momentum Continues"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-ef5e6bcabf63ff48aa77df452bf238e3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse799&hl=fse799&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no public activist signals were surfaced, the company is under a self-imposed  **strategic review**  titled "Vision 2030." [[16 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e4ac6d47dfbcb89fb3f217bfdacab92e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse67377&hl=fse67377&page=23)  This includes pivoting the  **Generics**  business to a leaner, outsourced manufacturing model and focusing on specialty "high entry barrier" markets. [[17 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68923c79581171619de11b9d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse990938&hl=fse990938&page=190)  The transition of leadership, including the appointment of  **Ashish Mukkirwar**  (formerly of Glenmark with M&A experience) as CFO in May 2026, further signals a focus on strategic value creation. [[18 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-ef656607d6378ee29838eee6f12adb7f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse57159&hl=fse57159&page=10) [[19 • PROXY] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a105034f6a894d2f5cc2971&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2940&hl=fse2940,fse3095&page=4)

### Broker Perspectives

Core growth is expected from the  **CDMO-Sterile Injectables**  segment, which grew 57.4% YoY in Q4FY26. [[20 • Systematix Institutional Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-ef5e6bcabf63ff48aa77df452bf238e3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse799&hl=fse799&page=1)

Despite regulatory challenges at the Montreal facility, brokers view the company's expansion in  **Radiopharma** —specifically adding six new PET radiopharmacies—as a primary value driver through FY2028.
