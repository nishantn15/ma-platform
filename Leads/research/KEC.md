## M&A-Readiness Profile: KEC International Ltd

### Recent M&A and Strategic Activity

KEC has utilized targeted acquisitions to enter new verticals and geographies. In October 2021, the company acquired  **Spur Infrastructure Private Limited**  for approximately INR 62 crore to scale its Oil & Gas pipeline presence. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66aa37aab47f85076f5465be&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse850962&hl=fse850962&page=143) [[5 • PROSP] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66f33211c07adf5fa1de3ca7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse696223&hl=fse696223&page=195)  Earlier in 2021, it acquired a tower manufacturing facility in Dubai to localize its Middle East T&D operations. [[6 • PROSP] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66f33211c07adf5fa1de3ca7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse695907&hl=fse695907,fse696001&page=195)  Most recently, in May 2026, the Board approved a scheme of merger to absorb its wholly owned subsidiary,  **KEC Spur Infrastructure** , back into the parent to streamline operations. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-545933__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41115&hl=fse41115&page=13)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

KEC is the flagship entity of the  **RPG Group** , led by Chairman Harsh Vardhan Goenka. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-400a59aa683a817750cd06af53fbca03&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25609&hl=fse25609&page=12)  As of March 31, 2025, the promoter group held a majority stake, with individual promoters and family trusts accounting for significant portions. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-400a59aa683a817750cd06af53fbca03&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1082040&hl=fse1082040&page=180)  The company operates 17 subsidiaries, including the global  **SAE Towers**  footprint and the recently incorporated  **KEC Asian Cables Limited**  (January 2025). [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6878e0bdc67d8507922480bc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse680353&hl=fse680353&page=113)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

Management has explicitly stated an intent to explore both organic and inorganic growth in adjacent verticals. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-42109771-1888442609&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13073&hl=fse13073&page=1)  A primary strategic signal is the "value unlocking" roadmap for the cables business, which was hived off into a separate wholly owned subsidiary to enable optimal capital allocation. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-42109771-1888442609&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12121&hl=fse12121,fse12370&page=1) [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-42109771-1918092614&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1885&hl=fse1885,fse2084&page=1)  Management indicated that while capital raises are not immediate, they may consider diluting a stake in the cables business or KEC itself within 12–18 months to fund future growth. [[14 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-42109771-1944070807&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25991&hl=fse25991&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are no active activist signals; however, the company is undergoing a voluntary strategic realignment. [[15 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-42109771-1901991540&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2174&hl=fse2174&page=1)  This includes a focus on asset-light models, debt reduction—aided by a  **INR 870 crore QIP**  in late 2024—and potential demergers to surface value in non-T&D segments.
