## M&A-Readiness Profile: Kitex Garments Ltd

### Recent M&A and Restructuring

Kitex Garments' recent activity is dominated by internal consolidation. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-b892cf6bc1b9b996ccdcdb6f246b7c3a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse227950&hl=fse227950,fse228139&page=75)  In February 2025, the Board approved a  **Scheme of Arrangement**  to demerge the Textile Business Division of  **Kitex Childrenswear Ltd (KCL)**  into KGL, a transaction expected to conclude by December 2025. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-b892cf6bc1b9b996ccdcdb6f246b7c3a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse228820&hl=fse228820&page=75)  KGL will issue approximately 9.22 crore new shares to KCL shareholders at a ratio of 9,706 KGL shares for every 100 KCL shares. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-b892cf6bc1b9b996ccdcdb6f246b7c3a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse228331&hl=fse228331&page=75)  Additionally, KGL maintains a 70:30 joint venture with KCL in  **Kitex Apparel Parks Ltd (KAPL)** , which recently saw an additional investment of INR 171.23 crores in March 2026. [[6 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f0b034aa7a603e0a276c31&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1272&hl=fse1272&page=1)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is promoter-led by  **Sabu M. Jacob** , who holds a nearly 100% stake in KCL and serves as Chairman and Managing Director of KGL. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68af25d22e3cd1703407e7b6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse139121&hl=fse139121,fse139266&page=29)  KGL operates six wholly-owned subsidiaries (Babywear, Kidswear, Knits, Littlewear, Packs, and Socks) and holds a 50% stake in  **Kitex USA LLC** . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68af258c2e3cd1703407e7b3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse221858&hl=fse221858,fse221937&page=73) [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-f615f36058cd2f9b7032eab659d263a1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse663797&hl=fse663797&page=216)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Growth is currently fueled by a massive capital expenditure program in  **Telangana** , targeting INR 5,000 crores in revenue at full capacity. [[10 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66e71dea71c087a95e3f8f90&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5140&hl=fse5140&page=6)  As of March 31, 2025, KGL reported cash and cash equivalents of INR 39.40 crores. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-b892cf6bc1b9b996ccdcdb6f246b7c3a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse698047&hl=fse698047&page=226)  In August 2025, the company sought shareholder approval for related-party transactions with KAPL up to INR 3,600 crores to fund the Telangana expansion. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68af25d22e3cd1703407e7b6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse107715&hl=fse107715,fse107792&page=21)

### Strategic Review and Activism

The proposed merger with KCL serves as a formal strategic review intended to optimize business operations and achieve vertical integration.
