## M&A and Corporate Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

KPIL recently completed the 100% stake sale of  **Vindhyachal Expressway Private Limited**  (VEPL) to  **Actis Atlantic Holdings Limited**  for INR 215.86 crore in January 2026. [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-548252__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse57658&hl=fse57658,fse57872&page=19)  It also finalized the monetization of the  **Indore real estate project**  by March 2026. [[5 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9440441-1981178828&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4085&hl=fse4085&page=1)  Conversely, the company has faced challenges with its 2021 acquisition of  **Fasttel**  in Brazil, which filed for judicial reorganization in March 2026, leading KPIL to fully impair its investments and goodwill in the subsidiary. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-548252__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse32284&hl=fse32284&page=11)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

The company is part of the  **Kalpataru Group** , with  **Kalpataru Ltd**  serving as the flagship promoter entity. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-33ef10411826b9758259797b0c3cf021&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse39317&hl=fse39317&page=14)  As of March 31, 2026, promoter holding stood at 33.60%, with 24.59% of that holding pledged. [[8 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-57c1246534be12a7c99b5cb38797c8a5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse975&hl=fse975&page=1) [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-30e1ef55c97a129add0db85f51e3fd93&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse472924&hl=fse472924&page=113)  KPIL operates 23 subsidiaries, including significant international units like  **Linjemontage**  (Sweden) and  **Kalpataru Power Chile SpA** . [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-b7bf7ab5f61426f8019927e877009830&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15478&hl=fse15478&page=4) [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-67d08d6fc67d85ceff57abb9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9565&hl=fse9565&page=6)

> KPIL's balance sheet strengthened significantly during FY26, with consolidated net debt reducing by over 50% YoY to Rs9.1bn and net debt/equity improved sharply to 0.1x. ### Capital Allocation and Strategic Signals

Management has signaled a transition toward disciplined capital allocation, prioritizing the core EPC business over non-core asset ownership. [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9440441-1981178828&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4085&hl=fse4085,fse4262&page=1)  With a consolidated cash and investment balance of INR 1,842 crore as of FY26, the company is targeting organic growth in high-margin segments like  **Transmission & Distribution**  (T&D) and  **Buildings & Factories**  (B&F). [[14 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-2fd4918f7b1430cfc0ea9893013cbb9c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3832&hl=fse3832&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Activity

While no formal activist presence is reported, KPIL has undergone a self-directed strategic review resulting in the "portfolio rationalization" of its road and logistics assets. [[15 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-c2cd5c735defea90e25735c6ca4ede1e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11445&hl=fse11445&page=5)

Management indicated that additional strategic actions regarding  **Shree Shubham Logistics**  and other non-core assets may materialize during FY27-28.
