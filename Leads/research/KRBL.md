### M&A Readiness Profile: KRBL Ltd (India Gate)

**Recent M&A and Strategic Activity** KRBL's primary recent inorganic activity is a  **INR 403 crore**  acquisition of 125 acres in  **Samalkha, Panipat**  through a successful bid in November 2025. [[2 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20350886-1969514834&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9088&hl=fse9088&page=1)  Management has earmarked  **INR 1,000–2,000 crore**  for future "distressed" real estate acquisitions via NCLT or municipal auctions, targeting 30–50% discounts to market value. [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20350886-1969514834&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25284&hl=fse25284&page=1) [[4 • Mirae Asset Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-9daca5feb53e3b23213607e9c3f7a648&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10340&hl=fse10340&page=5)  While analysts have suggested adjacent FMCG acquisitions, the company is currently focused on internal land monetization, specifically its 110-acre  **Ghaziabad**  site valued at up to  **INR 4,000 crore**  post-development. [[5 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20350886-1969514834&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9858&hl=fse9858,fse10014&page=1)

**Corporate Structure and Ownership** As of March 2026, the  **Promoter Group**  holds a  **60.17%**  stake, primarily through the  **Anil Mittal** ,  **Arun Kumar Gupta** , and  **Anoop Kumar Gupta Family Trusts** . [[6 • Arihant Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-3a67906dd1174e7ddb346d9cf3dc00df&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3136&hl=fse3136&page=1) [[7 • Dion Global Solutions] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-ec9a5a90cab019450adba8173c3b8b4310fbab55&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse316&hl=fse316&page=1)  The company operates through three key subsidiaries:  **KRBL DMCC**  (100%),  **KRBL LLC**  (100% step-down), and  **K B Exports Private Limited**  (70%). [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68c2a89a2e3cd173fff918ff&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse244392&hl=fse244392&page=74)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** KRBL maintains a significant cash warchest of  **INR 2,157 crore**  (as of September 30, 2025) and unutilized bank limits of  **INR 2,500 crore** . [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20350886-1969514834&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse26416&hl=fse26416&page=1)  The "Inorganic Intent" flag is set to 1 due to the explicit strategy to deploy surplus funds into real estate for better ROI than treasury instruments. [[10 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20350886-1969514834&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9271&hl=fse9271&page=1)  Management is evaluating a separate subsidiary structure for these real estate ventures. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20350886-1969514834&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29223&hl=fse29223&page=2)

**Strategic Review and Governance Signals** A  **Strategic Review**  flag is active following the September 2025 resignation of Independent Director  **Anil Kumar Chaudhary** , who alleged governance lapses regarding board processes and write-offs.
