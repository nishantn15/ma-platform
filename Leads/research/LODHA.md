Lodha Developers Ltd (officially rebranded from Macrotech Developers Ltd in June 2025) is currently in an "aggressive expansion" phase, utilizing its strong balance sheet and robust cash flows to consolidate its market leadership in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) and scale up in Pune, Bengaluru, and NCR. [[1 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-4811dc3c895c5098bd1fdbe742befeee&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1828&hl=fse1828&page=1) [[2 • Outlook Business] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-10c03873ce8d908819e6320c0cd7c56d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse553&hl=fse553&page=1)  The company's M&A activity is strategically focused on increasing stakes in its digital infrastructure platform and resolving distressed assets like V Hotels, while internal restructuring has led to the merger of several wholly-owned subsidiaries into the parent entity to simplify the corporate structure. ## M&A and Corporate Profile

### Recent M&A and Growth Activity

Lodha’s inorganic strategy prioritizes distressed asset resolution and increasing ownership in its high-growth  **Digital Infrastructure**  platform. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6681f8c471c08758da52a9f1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse317689&hl=fse317689&page=82) [[7 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-73049509-1917562533&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7110&hl=fse7110&page=1)  In June 2023, the company was declared the successful resolution applicant for  **V Hotels Limited**  under the CIRP process. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6681f8c471c08758da52a9f1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse317689&hl=fse317689&page=82)  In January 2025, management confirmed increasing its stake in the digital infrastructure platform from JV partners to consolidate its annuity income stream. [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-73049509-1917562533&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7110&hl=fse7110&page=1)  To streamline operations, Lodha is merging three listed subsidiaries— **National Standard (India) Ltd** ,  **Roselabs Finance Ltd** , and  **Sanathnagar Enterprises Ltd** —into itself, following the successful 2023 merger of five wholly-owned construction subsidiaries. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6681f8c471c08758da52a9f1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse316697&hl=fse316697,fse317236&page=82) [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686fd8c371c087079702d65e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse253743&hl=fse253743&page=82)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

As of mid-2025, the company operates with  **23 subsidiaries** . [[13 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-92c5609b3239319590054897db302170&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3285&hl=fse3285&page=2)  On June 16, 2025, the company officially changed its name from  **Macrotech Developers**  back to  **Lodha Developers**  after settling a trademark dispute. ### Capital Allocation and Strategic Signals

Lodha maintains a "cash warchest" of approximately  **INR 3,300 crore**  as of early 2025, supported by a  **INR 3,280 crore QIP**  completed in 2024 to fund land acquisitions and debt reduction. [[15 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6681f8c471c08758da52a9f1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse316127&hl=fse316127,fse316479&page=82) [[16 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-73049509-1917562533&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse50784&hl=fse50784&page=2)  Management has signaled a clear intent for inorganic growth in its  **Annuity business** , targeting INR 1,500 crore in annual rental income by FY31. [[17 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-31d8b00fcacf4be09b0d1f1dd8412f56&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6409&hl=fse6409&page=2) [[18 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6794d66c1478857b01ddd05b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3656&hl=fse3656&page=4)
