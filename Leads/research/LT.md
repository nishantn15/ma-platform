## M&A and Strategic Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity** L&T's recent activity is defined by the "asset-light" strategy under  **Lakshya 2026**  and  **Lakshya 2031** . [[5 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-874836-1997795122&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9630&hl=fse9630&page=1) [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547824__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse65725&hl=fse65725&page=34)  In April 2026, it signed agreements to divest its 100% stake in  **Nabha Power**  (Enterprise Value: INR 6,889 crore) and its entire stake in  **L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad)**  to the Telangana government for approximately INR 2,000 crore. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05cf6075dc5f259d841f29&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse22698&hl=fse22698,fse22840&page=9) [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-874836-1966308651&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse59311&hl=fse59311&page=3)  Conversely, it is making targeted tech acquisitions, including a 21% stake in  **E2E Networks**  (INR 1,080 crore for the first 15%) and  **Intelliswift**  via its subsidiary LTTS in late 2024. [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-874836-1917234388&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse42484&hl=fse42484,fse42539&page=2) [[10 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-fd57d853829bbe3164149437c522e518e9f39664&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse197&hl=fse197&page=1)  In April 2026, L&T Realty acquired  **International Green Scapes**  for INR 1,123 crore to expand its Gurugram footprint. [[11 • The Financial Express (India)] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-fed172ef084e4b5808518ff5bfc90d45&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse155&hl=fse155&page=1)

**Corporate Structure** L&T is a widely held company with  **zero promoter holding** ; the  **L&T Employees Trust**  (14.44%) and  **LIC**  (12.38%) are the largest shareholders as of March 2026. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547824__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1479661&hl=fse1479661,fse1479721&page=535) [[13 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fe1a066c893288fcad83ed&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5316&hl=fse5316,fse5379&page=8)  It operates via a hybrid structure of internal "Independent Companies" and major listed subsidiaries including  **LTIMindtree**  (68.53%),  **LTTS**  (73.57%), and  **L&T Finance**  (65.99%). [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547824__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1904180&hl=fse1904180&page=670)

**Capital Allocation and Growth Signals** The company maintains a standalone cash warchest of  **INR 37,200 crore**  and a low gross debt-to-equity ratio of 0.16x. [[15 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05cf6075dc5f259d841f29&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse92986&hl=fse92986&page=40) [[16 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fe1a066c893288fcad83ed&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse26981&hl=fse26981&page=46)  Under  **Lakshya 2031** , L&T has committed a massive  **INR 43,000–45,000 crore**  capex plan. [[17 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-fa038870702120d5af8a89dc8f3089bf&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7242&hl=fse7242&page=2)

Key allocations include  **Green Hydrogen**  (INR 15,000 crore),  **Data Centers**  (INR 10,000 crore),  **Industrial Electronics**  (INR 5,000 crore), and  **Semiconductors**  (INR 3,000 crore). [[18 • DAM Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DAMC-c9cdbcf32b095cddf677d699e02b791f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2009&hl=fse2009&page=1)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** Management is conducting an ongoing strategic review to simplify the group, including the "slump sale" consolidation of all realty assets into  **L&T Realty Properties**  and the carve-out of the  **SuFin**  B2B platform.
