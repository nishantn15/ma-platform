## M&A-Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A Activity

LTIMindtree (LTM) announced a major acquisition on May 22, 2026, purchasing  **Randstad’s**  technology and consulting services business in Europe and Australia for an enterprise value of €160 million (approximately ₹1,440 crore). [[3 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-678907__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse131&hl=fse131&page=1)  This unit generates over $500 million in annual revenue and adds 2,900 billable experts across France, Germany, and Australia. [[4 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-678907__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1031&hl=fse1031&page=4)  Additionally, LTM formalized a Joint Venture with  **Aramco Digital**  in July 2025 to provide information technology services in Saudi Arabia. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ea5f4d6c6882e56e587ca8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13871&hl=fse13871,fse13948,fse13958&page=5)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

LTM is a  **Larsen & Toubro (L&T)**  company, with the parent group acting as the ultimate promoter. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537620__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse77272&hl=fse77272&page=63)  As of March 31, 2026, promoter holding stands at 21.30%. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537620__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse52932&hl=fse52932&page=26)  The company operates through 19 active subsidiaries globally, including major hubs in the  **USA** ,  **UK** , and  **Germany** . [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ea5f4d6c6882e56e587ca8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13668&hl=fse13668,fse13868&page=5)  While most foreign units are wholly owned, it maintains a 69.58% stake in  **LTIMindtree South Africa** . [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-698c5fa756a3750007f0693e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1097506&hl=fse1097506&page=199)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

Management has declared LTM is in a deliberate "investing phase" to scale its portfolio and pursue strategic acquisitions. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-dede63154daf4cead0a0e69013c45dd9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse333115&hl=fse333115&page=76)  The "Turbocharged Portfolio" strategy explicitly lists "boosting capabilities through acquisitions" as a key pillar to reach its  **$10 billion revenue ambition** . [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-dede63154daf4cead0a0e69013c45dd9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse114223&hl=fse114223,fse114246&page=31)  As of March 31, 2026, LTM maintains a significant "warchest" of $1.63 billion (approximately ₹13,610.50 crore) in cash and investments. [[12 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537620__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse52909&hl=fse52909&page=26)  The Board remains committed to shareholder returns alongside this growth, approving an interim dividend of ₹22 per share in late 2025. [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-4440082-1963558174&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11113&hl=fse11113&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are no current reports of activist investor pressure or formal strategic reviews aimed at divestitures.  Instead, current leadership under CEO  **Venu Lambu**  is focused on internal transformation through the "Fit4Future" program and a strategic pivot to an AI-centric organizational structure. [[14 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-537620__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse55366&hl=fse55366,fse55525&page=33)
