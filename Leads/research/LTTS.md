### M&A and Strategic Profile Brief

**L&T Technology Services (LTTS)**  is executing a major strategic pivot via its  **Lakshya 31**  roadmap, characterized by the divestment of non-core assets and targeted acquisitions in high-growth technology sectors. [[3 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ca814243268feb0a3f9766&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1055&hl=fse1055,fse1173&page=1) [[4 • Arihant Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-49a472b79f449da7a83190ae66d1e56c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7306&hl=fse7306&page=3)

**Recent M&A Activity** In March 2026, LTTS announced the divestment of its  **Smart World and Communication (SWC)**  business to  **AMI Paradigm Solutions**  for ₹452 crore. [[5 • Dalal Street Investment Journal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-f977bbb717ebe44ac39acb5c0eb84df2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse734&hl=fse734&page=1)

This followed the 2024 acquisition of  **Intelliswift**  for approximately $330 million (approx. ₹2,750 crore) to bolster its software and digital engineering capabilities. [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-256490655-1975216218&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse36298&hl=fse36298&page=2)  Notably, the SWC divestment represents a ~45% haircut from its 2023 purchase price, reflecting a deliberate exit from low-margin, volatile public sector projects. [[7 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-f02278cb10436ddb955366e1743c4bb1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1437&hl=fse1437&page=1)

**Corporate Structure and Parentage** The company remains a key subsidiary of  **Larsen & Toubro Limited** , which holds a  **73.57%**  stake as of March 31, 2026. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1e9fde919acd501de8d6e0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse800220&hl=fse800220&page=306)  LTTS operates through a network of wholly owned subsidiaries, including  **L&T Technology Services LLC**  (USA) and  **Intelliswift Software (India)** . [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ffa009a1ee3e7bb1332577&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1123700&hl=fse1123700&page=412)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** LTTS maintains a robust cash warchest of  **₹3,160 crore**  (as of December 2025). [[10 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-256490655-1975216218&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14816&hl=fse14816&page=1)

Management has explicitly flagged intent for "tuck-in acquisitions" to support its 13-15% revenue CAGR target through 2031. [[11 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-bf91dd79d6c179e1d8294cbcb18d0a2f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1801&hl=fse1801&page=1)

Capital allocation is being redirected toward six "big bets," including  **Software-Defined Mobility** ,  **NexGen Compute** , and  **AI Infrastructure** . [[12 • Auerbach Grayson] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AUBH-6c8224596b3f1d179e21819229f98e0f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1247&hl=fse1247&page=1)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** A comprehensive  **strategic review**  is underway, evidenced by the "Lakshya 31" plan launched in April 2026. [[13 • IDBI Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-7f70399a6f8f22c7b73f5963e7482b55&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5114&hl=fse5114&page=2)  This review has already resulted in the closure of non-core regional offices in Europe and the Middle East, alongside the exit from low-margin telecom infrastructure contracts. [[14 • IDBI Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-7f70399a6f8f22c7b73f5963e7482b55&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse829&hl=fse829&page=1)

While no formal activist pressure was cited, the portfolio "clean-up" appears driven by consistent underperformance against previous growth guidance. [[15 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-f02278cb10436ddb955366e1743c4bb1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12364&hl=fse12364&page=5)
