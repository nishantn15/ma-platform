## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Vedant Fashions has not completed a major acquisition in the last five years, with its last landmark deal being the strategic acquisition of  **Mebaz**  in 2017 . [[1 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-556612__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9700&hl=fse9700&page=9)  The company is led by founder  **Ravi Modi** , who serves as Chairman and Managing Director . [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-556612__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse26450&hl=fse26450&page=9)  As of December 31, 2025, Vedant Fashions reported no investments in subsidiaries, associates, or joint ventures, meaning consolidated financial results are not applicable . [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-406862__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9242&hl=fse9242&page=4)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

The company maintains a "disciplined approach towards acquisitions" as part of its core growth strategy . [[4 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-556612__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10194&hl=fse10194&page=11)  While it prioritizes organic growth through brands like  **Manyavar** ,  **Mohey** , and the rapidly growing premium label  **Twamev** , it retains a significant warchest . [[5 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-253108251-1983436773&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6407&hl=fse6407&page=1)  As of March 31, 2026, the company held  **INR 688.43 crore**  (INR 6,884.25 million) in non-current investments, reflecting high liquidity and strong cash generation . [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-556612__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13483&hl=fse13483&page=5)  This is supported by a 95% cash conversion ratio (Operating Cash Flow to PAT) . [[7 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-253108251-1983436773&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5966&hl=fse5966&page=1)  Recent capital allocation has focused on dividends, with the Board recommending a final dividend of  **INR 7.75**  per share for FY26 . [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-556612__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1854&hl=fse1854&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activism

There are no current signals of a formal strategic review or activist investor pressure .  Management recently engaged in a "calibrated" review of its retail footprint, rationalizing smaller format and underperforming locations to optimize ROI . [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-253108251-1983436773&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2636&hl=fse2636&page=1)  The company's business model remains asset-light and franchisee-led, focusing on maintaining industry-leading margins rather than aggressive, high-risk expansion .
