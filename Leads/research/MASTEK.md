## Mastek Ltd M&A Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity** Mastek has been consistently active in mid-sized acquisitions to bolster its platform capabilities. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-1a080eba641cdf22873fef3e3e02065e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20317&hl=fse20317&page=9)  Key deals include the  **August 2022**  acquisition of  **MST Solutions**  for approximately  **INR 856.25 crore**  (USD 76.6 million upfront) to enhance its Salesforce ecosystem presence. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68b526122e3cd108aac13dd2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1069568&hl=fse1069568,fse1069671,fse1069863&page=168)  Earlier, in  **March 2020** , it completed the acquisition of  **Evosys**  for  **INR 482.04 crore**  (USD 64.9 million) to gain Oracle Cloud expertise. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-1a080eba641cdf22873fef3e3e02065e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse794842&hl=fse794842,fse795226&page=127)  Its most recent major move was the  **July 2023**  acquisition of  **BizAnalytica LLC** , a data cloud and modernization specialist, which has already yielded synergy wins in the UK banking sector. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-1a080eba641cdf22873fef3e3e02065e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse160283&hl=fse160283&page=36) [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-f18e232f7ac3ff1718c5a971a721ecb0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse81264&hl=fse81264&page=27)

**Parent and Subsidiary Structure** Mastek remains promoter-led, with founders including  **Ashank Desai**  holding a combined  **36% stake**  as of March 2026. [[7 • ICICI Direct] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICDR-4ff0379b4a65a323f7b9f8df3db53a23&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3311&hl=fse3311&page=1)  The company recently simplified its structure by merging its wholly-owned subsidiary  **Mastek Enterprise Solutions Private Limited**  into the parent entity, effective  **April 1, 2024** . [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-539639__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8659&hl=fse8659&page=5)  Global operations are managed through key hubs:  **Mastek (UK) Limited**  (serving the core UK government/healthcare markets) and  **Mastek Inc.**  (USA). [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-1a080eba641cdf22873fef3e3e02065e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse274214&hl=fse274214&page=49)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** Management maintains a "Lead with AI" strategy, signaling that future M&A will pivot from pure technology skill-sets (like Salesforce/Oracle) toward  **vertical-specific domain expertise** . [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ebb3932433997dc2848ac3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse39845&hl=fse39845,fse40049&page=15)  With a cash warchest of  **INR 938 crore**  as of March 31, 2026—a  **51% year-on-year increase** —the company is actively evaluating the "best use of cash," including inorganic opportunities that provide core domain capabilities. [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69ebb3932433997dc2848ac3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12718&hl=fse12718&page=6)

However, the company is undergoing a  **strategic reset**  in its North America business to stabilize leadership and focus on target markets.
