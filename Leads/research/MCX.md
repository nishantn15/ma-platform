## M&A-Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A and Corporate Structure

MCX has not engaged in any major acquisitions or divestitures over the last five years, focusing instead on internal ecosystem development.  The company is a  **widely held** , public limited entity with  **0.0% promoter holding** .  Key institutional shareholders as of March 2026 include various mutual funds and  **Kotak Mahindra Bank**  (15%). ### Strategic Reviews and Activist Signals

Research did not surface any formal "strategic reviews" or signs of  **activist shareholder**  activity.  Analysts at  **Elara Securities**  (May 2026) view the company as a structural growth play with a dominant 98%+ market share.

**Avendus Spark**  (May 2026) notes that the technology migration to  **TCS**  will significantly enhance operating leverage and EBITDA margins, potentially freeing up further capital for inorganic pursuits.  The board's August 2025 approval of a  **1:5 stock split**  further signals a focus on improving retail liquidity and shareholder accessibility.
