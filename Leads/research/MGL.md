## M&A Readiness Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

Mahanagar Gas (MGL) completed its most significant recent transaction on February 1, 2024, by acquiring a 100% stake in  **Unison Enviro Private Limited (UEPL)**  for INR 562.09 crore. [[2 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-a94564be8e47ecc0309e098797ed9d46&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse706070&hl=fse706070&page=140)  This entity was subsequently amalgamated with MGL effective July 23, 2025, to streamline operations across its six geographical areas. [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-21817445-1967344329&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2306&hl=fse2306&page=1)  In early 2025, MGL diversified into the EV sector by acquiring strategic minority stakes in  **3EV Industries**  (25.90%) and  **International Battery Company India**  (44.00%). [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547442__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse62351&hl=fse62351&page=23)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is promoted by  **GAIL (India) Limited** , which holds a 32.50% stake, and the  **Government of Maharashtra** , which holds 10.00%. [[5 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547442__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7952&hl=fse7952,fse7957,fse7964,fse7990&page=15)  Following the UEPL merger in 2025, MGL has no direct operational subsidiaries, instead holding its new energy ventures as associates. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547442__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34405&hl=fse34405&page=14)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

MGL maintains a significant "warchest" for inorganic growth.  As of March 31, 2026, the company held INR 57.00 crore in cash and cash equivalents, supplemented by INR 1,036.00 crore in mutual fund investments intended for strategic deployment. [[7 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547442__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4239&hl=fse4239&page=9)  Management explicitly stated in October 2025 that they are "waiting and scouting" for new M&A opportunities to further consolidate the CGD sector. [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-21817445-1967344329&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse40950&hl=fse40950&page=2)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are currently no reported activist signals or formal strategic reviews aimed at divestment.  The board remains focused on a "New Growth Pillars" strategy to future-proof the business through strategic acquisitions in biogas, LNG, and hydrogen. [[9 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-a94564be8e47ecc0309e098797ed9d46&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse70405&hl=fse70405&page=18)  While the company has invested in EV-related associates, management clarified in early 2026 that there are no immediate plans for listing these investments until they reach a more mature operational phase. [[10 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-21817445-1967344329&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse55301&hl=fse55301,fse55439&page=3)
