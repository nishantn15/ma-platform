## M&A-Readiness Profile: Samvardhana Motherson International (SAMIL)

### Recent M&A Activity and Structure

SAMIL is currently in an intense acquisition phase, completing or announcing 16 deals since late 2022 to diversify its portfolio. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66b06257c67d858646cd2c32&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse127651&hl=fse127651,fse127708&page=44)  Notable recent transactions include the August 2025 announcement to acquire 81% of  **Yutaka Giken Co.**  and the March 2026 100% acquisition of  **Yutaka Autoparts India** . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-670933__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse64526&hl=fse64526,fse64691&page=20)  Other strategic moves include acquiring  **ADI Group**  (aerospace) for INR 281.20 crore in May 2024 and  **Irillic**  (medical electronics) for INR 46.60 crore in April 2024. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6978a1196e88640008dac642&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse124919&hl=fse124919&page=43)

The company operates through a complex global structure with over 190 subsidiaries, often using  **Motherson Global Investments B.V.**  (formerly SMRC) as a vehicle for international holdings. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-670933__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18105&hl=fse18105&page=7) [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6978a1196e88640008dac642&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1621745&hl=fse1621745&page=355)  As of March 2026, the  **Sehgal family (promoters)**  held a 48.6% stake, maintaining tight control over strategic direction. [[9 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-a1bcae71412869377d8a2b6f08583e49&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4341&hl=fse4341&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

SAMIL’s "Vision 2030" serves as the primary signal for continued inorganic growth. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-688cc5455811714a0b123425&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41238&hl=fse41238&page=10)  Management actively seeks "distressed" or "customer-encouraged" acquisitions where they can provide supply chain stability. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66b06257c67d858646cd2c32&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse127364&hl=fse127364,fse127564&page=44)  Financial readiness is high; as of May 2026, the group reported total liquidity of  **INR 14,759 crore** , including INR 7,858 crore in unrestricted cash. [[12 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a16f5ac7614b423f3f10ec6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse31621&hl=fse31621&page=34)

Broker analysis highlights that SAMIL's net debt-to-EBITDA improved to a record low of  **0.8x**  by March 2026, significantly below its internal comfort threshold of 1.5x, providing ample headroom for large-scale deals. [[13 • Arihant Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-af9b0ddcdbb8f0ad4a2b9e610120224b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11178&hl=fse11178&page=3)  The company recently raised funds through a USD 350 million bond issuance in July 2024 and a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) of Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) in September 2024 to optimize its balance sheet for future growth. [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6978a1196e88640008dac642&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse133291&hl=fse133291&page=44) [[15 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-688cc5455811714a0b123425&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse583406&hl=fse583406&page=143)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are no current reports of activist pressure or formal "strategic reviews" aimed at divestment. Instead, the company is focused on "transformative actions" to turn around underperforming acquired units and realize group synergies. [[16 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6978a1196e88640008dac642&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse122354&hl=fse122354&page=42) [[17 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20356896-1965987544&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4078&hl=fse4078&page=1)  Research indicates strong institutional support, with mutual funds and FPIs holding approximately 33.5% of the company as of early 2026. [[18 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-a1bcae71412869377d8a2b6f08583e49&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4341&hl=fse4341&page=1)
