## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

Mphasis has pivoted toward targeted tuck-in acquisitions and contract "buyouts" to accelerate its transition to a platform-led model. [[3 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-dee0973b2ead08d64e7687de9deaf55d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse881&hl=fse881&page=1)  In April 2026, it acquired  **Theory and Practice (TAP)**  for up to CAD 30 million (~INR 182 crore) to integrate the  **Continuum AI**  decisioning platform. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f255621bc46b748955b159&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse203432&hl=fse203432&page=48)  Simultaneously, it acquired customer contracts from  **OKIN Process**  for up to USD 5.5 million (INR 52 crore) to expand its BPO footprint. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544818__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25353&hl=fse25353&page=6)  Other recent deals include the 2025 acquisition of  **Locate Software's**  digital transformation business (USD 27 million) and the 2024 takeover of  **EDZ Systems'**  cybersecurity business. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544818__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20535&hl=fse20535&page=6)

> Capital allocation priorities remain focused on: i) organic investments, incl AI platform investments (~1.5% of revenue), SET investments (~0.3%), and strategic client investments; ii) targeted tuck-in acquisitions like TAP to support unified Tria architecture; and iii) maintaining 60% payout track record. [[7 • Emkay Research • 27 May 26 • "Mphasis: Aspires to transition to a platform-led growth model; ADD; Target: Rs2,500"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-49a71510fdefd4e1a30668172b05d857&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1949&hl=fse1949&page=1)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is  **Blackstone-backed** , with  **BCP Asia (SG) Mirror Holding Pte Ltd**  serving as the ultimate promoter holding 30.55% as of March 2026. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f255621bc46b748955b159&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse118626&hl=fse118626&page=26) [[9 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-76adc814ebc622330114a92c2c30f6dc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20495&hl=fse20495&page=7)  Mphasis operates through 44 subsidiaries, with major hubs including  **Mphasis Corporation**  (USA),  **Mphasis Consulting**  (UK), and  **Mphasis Ireland** . [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f255621bc46b748955b159&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18998&hl=fse18998&page=8) [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544818__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse50524&hl=fse50524&page=14)  In early 2026, it consolidated ownership by purchasing the remaining 49% of  **Mrald Limited** . [[12 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544818__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24041&hl=fse24041&page=6)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management maintains a disciplined capital allocation strategy, prioritizing organic AI investments (~1.5% of revenue), tuck-in M&A, and a 60% dividend payout. [[13 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-49a71510fdefd4e1a30668172b05d857&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1949&hl=fse1949&page=1)

A core strategic signal is the "vendor consolidation playbook," where Mphasis uses its balance sheet to "buy out" tail-vendor contracts at roughly 1.25x revenue, aiming to scale sub-USD 10 million accounts into USD 75 million+ strategic partnerships. [[14 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-dee0973b2ead08d64e7687de9deaf55d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2800&hl=fse2800&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

No formal strategic review or activist activity was reported as of June 2026.  However, analysts note a persistent risk of a  **Blackstone**  stake sale, given the fund's typical exit horizons. [[15 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-76adc814ebc622330114a92c2c30f6dc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20681&hl=fse20681&page=7)

The company's focus remains on transitioning to a platform-plus-outcomes model with reported ARR metrics starting in FY28. [[16 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-5e0f2afb1149de5f0ec93bc541b46de3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1211&hl=fse1211,fse1412&page=1)
