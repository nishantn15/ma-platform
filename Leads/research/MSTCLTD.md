## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

MSTC completed the strategic disinvestment of its 100% owned subsidiary,  **Ferro Scrap Nigam Limited (FSNL)** , on January 21, 2025, for approximately INR 273.50 crore. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68d6e2e35ed39b0007d2233f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17218&hl=fse17218&page=23) [[4 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-22596323-1982189992&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9544&hl=fse9544&page=1)  Following this divestiture, MSTC's consolidated financials primarily reflect its 50-50 joint venture with  **Mahindra Auto** , known as  **Mahindra MSTC Recycling Private Limited (MMRPL)** , which focuses on end-of-life vehicle recycling. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68d6e2e35ed39b0007d2233f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17460&hl=fse17460,fse17550&page=23) [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-22596323-1982189992&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11137&hl=fse11137,fse11250&page=1)  The  **Government of India**  remains the ultimate parent and promoter, maintaining a 64.75% stake since the company's IPO in 2019. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68d6e2e35ed39b0007d2233f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse420649&hl=fse420649&page=151)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

As of March 31, 2025, MSTC holds a substantial cash warchest, with bank balances (including fixed deposits) totaling INR 1,150.46 crore. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68d6e2e35ed39b0007d2233f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse417252&hl=fse417252&page=149)  Management currently indicates a preference for internal growth over large acquisitions, focusing on "asset-light" digital initiatives like the  **EPR Trading Platform**  and a new centralized  **Travel Booking Platform**  scheduled for a 2026 launch. [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-22596323-1982189992&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18705&hl=fse18705&page=1) [[10 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-698d776d30d77f0007be2c33&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6706&hl=fse6706&page=7)  While the company has expressed a general objective to enter joint ventures offering e-commerce synergy, recent signals emphasize a "tapering down" of non-core business segments. [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-22596323-1982189992&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20726&hl=fse20726&page=1) [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-d17c60e818d6f6f9b74408648cd1bf06&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse74714&hl=fse74714&page=26)

### Strategic Review and Governance

The company is undergoing a significant strategic review, planning a complete exit from its legacy trading vertical during FY 2026-27 to focus exclusively on e-commerce and digital services. [[13 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1acd3d61dceda889230fc9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6007&hl=fse6007&page=6)  No activist investor pressure has been reported; however, the company is actively addressing a long-standing legal dispute with  **Standard Chartered Bank**  regarding a 2008 receivable purchase agreement, which recently saw a favorable ruling from the Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunal in August 2023.
