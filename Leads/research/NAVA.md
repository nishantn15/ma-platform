## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Nava's recent inorganic activity is characterized by niche technology acquisitions and joint ventures (JV) focused on operational scale. [[3 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-284deebf10f211f67805ce13e3757f87ab14aafa&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2643&hl=fse2643&page=1) [[4 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-da0e7556f152bf5522ab31d848dd717b3e4d1cf5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse316&hl=fse316&page=1)  In February 2026, the company acquired  **Beam** , a cloud-based SaaS product, to broaden its technology stack for government service delivery. [[5 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-da0e7556f152bf5522ab31d848dd717b3e4d1cf5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse163&hl=fse163,fse316&page=1)  Additionally, Nava established a JV with  **ZCCM-IH**  for the  **Maamba Solar (MSEL)**  project, in which it holds a 65% stake. [[6 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-284deebf10f211f67805ce13e3757f87ab14aafa&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2643&hl=fse2643&page=1)  Financially, Nava is exceptionally well-capitalized, having received ₹705 crore via dividends and a $50 million (approx. ₹450 crore) share buyback from its subsidiary  **Nava Global**  in early 2026. [[7 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-55339f7da93160594105dab5abc3fb78331d08b4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse642&hl=fse642&page=1) [[8 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-4fbbe41a3421db5d4ff0668202986a6d2a43e43b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse186&hl=fse186,fse295&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

Nava operates through 17 direct and step-down subsidiaries. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-548368__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20204&hl=fse20204&page=7)  The  **Promoter Group** , led by  **Ashok Devineni** , maintains an aggregate holding of approximately 61.27% as of February 2025, providing strong internal control for strategic shifts. [[10 • PROSPEQ] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-67c70680c67d856e4628983b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse70069&hl=fse70069&page=18)  Key entities include  **Nava Global Pte Ltd**  (100% owned), which serves as the investment hub for international ventures in mining, energy, and agriculture. [[11 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-284deebf10f211f67805ce13e3757f87ab14aafa&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2772&hl=fse2772&page=1) [[12 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-4fbbe41a3421db5d4ff0668202986a6d2a43e43b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse402&hl=fse402&page=1)  Its most significant asset is  **Maamba Energy Limited**  (65% stake), the largest independent power producer in Zambia. [[13 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-55339f7da93160594105dab5abc3fb78331d08b4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6166&hl=fse6166&page=1)

### Inorganic Growth Signals

Management has explicitly stated that the $50 million buyback proceeds will be used to "augment liquidity to pursue new acquisitions and projects." [[14 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-4fbbe41a3421db5d4ff0668202986a6d2a43e43b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1012&hl=fse1012&page=1)  The company is centralizing its global business under  **Nava Global**  to achieve "higher and independent enterprise value," a move that often precedes further corporate actions or fundraising. [[15 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-284deebf10f211f67805ce13e3757f87ab14aafa&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2643&hl=fse2643,fse2772&page=1)  Diversification into  **Avocado**  and  **Sugar**  plantations in Zambia further signals a move toward non-cyclical, asset-heavy industrial plays. [[16 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=PR-55339f7da93160594105dab5abc3fb78331d08b4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3206&hl=fse3206&page=1)  No activist or formal strategic review signals were identified in recent filings.
