## M&A-Readiness Profile: NBCC (India) Ltd

### Corporate Structure and Control

NBCC is a  **Navratna CPSE**  under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), with the  **President of India**  holding a 61.75% stake as of March 31, 2026. [[5 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a23f4ab9261670e25d85209&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9385&hl=fse9385&page=6) [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68b718932e3cd15b82d877cb&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse819250&hl=fse819250,fse819344&page=255)  Its subsidiary structure includes  **HSCC (India) Ltd**  (100% stake acquired for INR 285 crore in 2018),  **NBCC Services Ltd**  (100%), and  **Hindustan Steelworks Construction Ltd**  (51% stake acquired for INR 36 crore in 2017). [[7 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-674464f1c67d859f0b53cb60&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20195&hl=fse20195&page=26)  Recent international expansion led to the incorporation of  **NBCC Overseas Real Estate LLC**  in Dubai on April 23, 2025, to facilitate UAE project development. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6842103158117104634e34f6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse33586&hl=fse33586&page=9)

### Recent M&A and Strategic Review

In April 2026, the Government of India issued a "No Objection" memorandum for the  **merger of HSCC (India) Limited**  into NBCC. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a143a7c679a8df1109f2cef&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse32186&hl=fse32186&page=9)  A Board-level Merger Committee and a Transaction Advisor have been appointed to oversee this consolidation. [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-538962__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse88334&hl=fse88334&page=26)  Strategic reviews are ongoing for non-operational units; the company recently dissolved its  **NBCC International**  and  **NBCC Environment Engineering**  subsidiaries through voluntary liquidation in late 2023. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-16cb5aaf7e793b8c7260e6058280f87e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse142707&hl=fse142707&page=66)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

As of March 31, 2026, NBCC reported a consolidated "own" cash balance of  **INR 939 crore** , distinct from client funds totaling over INR 5,000 crore. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9663761-1983581033&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34262&hl=fse34262,fse34339&page=2)  Management signals strong inorganic intent for international markets, highlighted by a  **November 2025 MoU**  with  **Pantheon Elysee Real Estate Development**  for UAE projects and a strategic agreement with  **Goldfields Australia**  for Yigarn Terrane region developments.
