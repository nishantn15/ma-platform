## M&A-Readiness Profile: Nestle India Ltd (NSE: NESTLEIND)

### Recent M&A Activity

**Nestle India**  has recently transitioned from a purely organic growth model to a more active transaction-led strategy. [[1 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-f45ab937b0acd3f6601f2ee1bbf4043a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2497&hl=fse2497&page=1)  In April 2024, the company entered a significant  **Joint Venture (JV)**  with  **Dr. Reddy's Laboratories**  to scale its nutraceutical and health science portfolio in India and Nepal. [[2 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a06da4bbc313553b8985295&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1&hl=fse1,fse100)  As part of this,  **Nestle India**  subscribed to equity shares worth INR 705.60 crore for a 49% stake. [[3 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a06da4bbc313553b8985295&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse691&hl=fse691&page=1)  Simultaneously, it executed a slump sale of its existing medical nutrition and nutraceuticals (NHSc) business to the JV for INR 218.90 crore, effective August 1, 2024. [[4 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a06da4bbc313553b8985295&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1325&hl=fse1325,fse1535&page=1) [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-018dca64f2d2f5eb8c6db457d4c68bd2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse362014&hl=fse362014&page=102)  Additionally, the company divested its  **Nestle Business Services (NBS)**  division to a fellow subsidiary of  **Nestle S.A.**  for INR 76.58 crore in July 2024 to streamline operations. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-913e5a1edeb80f2e5875a6646f2ca1af&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse361196&hl=fse361196&page=102)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

**Nestle India**  is a subsidiary of  **Nestle S.A.**  (Switzerland), which maintains an aggregate 62.76% promoter holding as of March 31, 2025. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-683f47ce58117139e799e6df&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse665184&hl=fse665184&page=214)  This holding is split between  **Nestle S.A.**  (34.28%) and its subsidiary  **Maggi Enterprises Limited**  (28.48%). [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-683f47ce58117139e799e6df&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse384974&hl=fse384974,fse385024&page=112)  The company operates as a standalone entity in India with no direct subsidiaries; however, it holds a 49% associate stake in the  **Dr. Reddy’s and Nestle Health Science Limited**  JV. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-683e0446c67d8507cd731f57&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse604238&hl=fse604238,fse604352&page=204)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

The company's cash warchest reached a six-year high of  **INR 1,320.57 crore**  (INR 13,205.7 million) as of March 31, 2026, driven by strong operating cash flows exceeding INR 5,000 crore. [[10 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-4df460abdb384cd30a4bcd96e4ff2a84&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2794&hl=fse2794&page=1) [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-541341__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7290&hl=fse7290&page=3)  Management, led by Chairman  **Manish Tiwary** , has signaled a commitment to reinvesting in brands and capacity. [[12 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-4df460abdb384cd30a4bcd96e4ff2a84&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2691&hl=fse2691&page=1)  While large-scale acquisitions are rare, the formation of the **Dr.
