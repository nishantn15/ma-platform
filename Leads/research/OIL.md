## M&A-Readiness Profile: Oil India Ltd (EQOIL.IN)

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

**Oil India**  has streamlined its international portfolio, completing the divestment of its  **Niobrara**  shale asset in January 2022 and exiting a non-performing block in  **Gabon**  in November 2025 with a provision of ~INR 2,300 crore. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-34d078724f521cb2d2803859244301ec&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse569710&hl=fse569710&page=244) [[6 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-ff5538d1045d6635a55639afe4ddb44f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2765&hl=fse2765&page=1)

In contrast, its domestic inorganic activity is accelerating through strategic joint ventures. In November 2025, OIL formed  **Arunachal Gas Private Limited**  (50% stake) with  **BPCL**  for city gas expansion and signed a partnership with  **TotalEnergies**  for deepwater exploration. [[7 • Antique Stockbroking] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ANTQ-b0d4da5ad1ddf551db21d4a03dcf5912&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5397&hl=fse5397&page=2) [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-550788__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34365&hl=fse34365&page=13)  Most recently, in June 2026, it signed an MoU with  **CSIR**  to advance energy innovation.
