## M&A and Corporate Structure Brief

**Recent M&A Activity** Paradeep Phosphates (PPL) completed a landmark merger with  **Mangalore Chemicals & Fertilizers (MCFL)**  in October 2025 via a scheme of arrangement (1.87 PPL shares for every 1 MCFL share). [[3 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-70750642964594ba79d058080e05fa62&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse979&hl=fse979&page=1) [[4 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a032698fb8286b155cc8982&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2827&hl=fse2827&page=2)  Concurrently, in September 2025, MCFL (prior to its final dissolution into PPL) acquired a  **Single Super Phosphate (SSP)**  plant in Mahad from Zuari Agro Chemicals Ltd (ZACL) on a slump sale basis. [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-547634__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25415&hl=fse25415,fse25557,fse25564&page=10)

These follow the FY23 acquisition of ZACL's Goa plant for ~INR 2,135 crore. [[6 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-ba3d92dbcc4c615588271eee7d26f984&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28862&hl=fse28862&page=14)

**Parent and Subsidiary Structure** PPL is a joint venture between the  **Adventz Group**  (Zuari Agro Chemicals) and  **OCP Group**  of Morocco, held through the promoter entity  **Zuari Maroc Phosphates Private Limited (ZMPPL)** , which owns 56.05% of the company as of March 2025. [[7 • SKP Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-ce953fa52c5e302a460ac452d18145b9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse513&hl=fse513&page=1) [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-8dd154bac73da5d891a4c7c5c99a4cd1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse498082&hl=fse498082,fse498176&page=103)  While PPL has no direct subsidiaries, it holds a 50.00% stake in a joint venture,  **Zuari Yoma Agri Solutions Limited**  (Myanmar). [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-8dd154bac73da5d891a4c7c5c99a4cd1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse168381&hl=fse168381&page=53)

**Capital Allocation and Growth Signals** Management has signaled a shift toward intensive organic growth, announcing an  **INR 3,600 crore investment program**  to be executed by FY29. [[10 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-22709447-1968451936&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3908&hl=fse3908,fse4048&page=1)  This plan includes adding 1.0 MMTPA of granulation capacity and achieving 100% backward integration for phosphoric and sulfuric acid across all sites. [[11 • SKP Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-ce953fa52c5e302a460ac452d18145b9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4218&hl=fse4218&page=1)

The "cash warchest" stood at INR 873.73 crore (as cash and cash equivalents) as of March 31, 2025, though broker estimates suggest this may moderate to ~INR 363.50 crore by FY26 due to heavy capex. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-8dd154bac73da5d891a4c7c5c99a4cd1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse421694&hl=fse421694&page=92) [[13 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-79aa1751a4205d786ed8c2e1145fc1ec&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1048&hl=fse1048&page=1)

**Strategic and Activist Signals** There are no current signals of activist activity or formal "for sale" strategic reviews.  Instead, PPL is focused on realizing synergies from the MCFL merger, including southern market access and logistics optimization. [[14 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-ba3d92dbcc4c615588271eee7d26f984&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse31830&hl=fse31830&page=15)  A 10-year exclusive supply agreement with  **OCP Group**  provides long-term raw material security. [[15 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-79aa1751a4205d786ed8c2e1145fc1ec&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5095&hl=fse5095&page=1)
