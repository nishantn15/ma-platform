## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent Activity and Corporate Structure

One97 Communications (Paytm) is a  **professionally managed company**  with  **zero promoter holding** , as disclosed in May 2026. [[5 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-7b41da34c5e030691e93d5ba47d0208e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3515&hl=fse3515&page=1)  Significant recent transactions include the August 2024 sale of its movie and events ticketing business to  **Zomato**  for INR 2,048.40 crore to sharpen focus on core financial services. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6891fb865811712739fb21a1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse677996&hl=fse677996,fse678341&page=234)  Additionally, the company realized over INR 2,000 crore from the sale of Stock Acquisition Rights in  **PayPay Corporation**  (Japan) during FY 2025. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6891fb865811712739fb21a1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse54105&hl=fse54105&page=24)  Internally, it executed a slump sale of its offline merchant business to subsidiary  **Paytm Payments Services Limited (PPSL)**  for INR 975 crore in November 2025 to meet regulatory Payment Aggregator guidelines. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452949__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse36693&hl=fse36693&page=13)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Growth Signals

As of March 31, 2026, Paytm maintains a massive  **cash warchest of INR 13,315 crore** . [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc6803ea7b85338bd81bf9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18666&hl=fse18666&page=12)  Management has explicitly stated intent to use this "dry powder" for  **selective inorganic action** , specifically targeting  **AI capabilities**  that are strategically additive. [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc6803ea7b85338bd81bf9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18957&hl=fse18957&page=12) [[11 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-3f7632f5be20270e3efb6934236d968b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse22517&hl=fse22517&page=7)  Recent small-scale international investments include a 25% stake in US-based  **Seven Tech LLC**  (February 2025) and the incorporation of  **PT Paytm Indonesia Teknologi**  (April 2026) with an initial investment of approximately INR 8 crore. [[12 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fc6803ea7b85338bd81bf9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse16376&hl=fse16376&page=11) [[13 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6891fb865811712739fb21a1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse819009&hl=fse819009&page=273)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no formal activist presence is reported, the company has undergone significant  **regulatory-driven strategic restructuring** . [[14 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-7b41da34c5e030691e93d5ba47d0208e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1026&hl=fse1026&page=1)  Following the RBI’s cancellation of the  **Paytm Payments Bank (PPBL)**  license in April 2024, Paytm terminated all commercial agreements with the associate and fully impaired its investment by March 2024. [[15 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-7b41da34c5e030691e93d5ba47d0208e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse777&hl=fse777&page=1)  Management emphasizes an  **asset-light distribution model** , showing no interest in pursuing an NBFC license, preferring to partner with blue-chip lenders. [[16 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-b27f6426159f91d7e4fb1a01334bd009&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse21925&hl=fse21925&page=7)  The grant of a final  **Payment Aggregator license**  in November 2025 is viewed as a validation of the company's improved compliance posture. [[17 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-7b41da34c5e030691e93d5ba47d0208e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1026&hl=fse1026&page=1)
