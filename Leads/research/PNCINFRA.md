## M&A Readiness Profile: PNC Infratech Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Asset Monetization

In March 2026,  **PNC Infratech**  concluded a landmark strategic divestment initiative, selling its equity stake in 12 road assets to  **Vertis Infrastructure Trust**  (sponsored by  **KKR**  affiliates) for an enterprise value of approximately INR 9,006 crore and an equity value of INR 2,517 crore. [[2 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-554190__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24995&hl=fse24995&page=26)  The final asset transferred in Q4 FY26 was  **PNC Challakere (Karnataka) Highways Private Limited** , which had an individual enterprise value of INR 683.84 crore. [[3 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69c75794f90e9d2b41b160ad&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1123&hl=fse1123&page=1)  This program included 11 National Highway HAM projects and one State Highway BOT Toll asset, covering approximately 3,800 lane-km.  Beyond divestitures, the company acquired a 74% stake in  **PW Infratech Private Limited**  in May 2025.
