> Hitachi Energy India raised ~Rs25bn, via Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) in March, 2025, with funds intended to accelerate growth through capacity expansion and strengthening the balance sheet. Hitachi has continued to invest in localization and leads the HVDC market in India, winning two large HVDC orders over the last 2 years, and committing Rs20bn+ capex over th next 3-4 years. ## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent Activity and Parent Structure

Hitachi Energy India has not completed any major inorganic acquisitions or divestitures in the past five years, prioritizing organic expansion instead. [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-368630__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12320&hl=fse12320&page=4)  The company is a 75%-owned subsidiary of  **Hitachi Energy Ltd**  (Switzerland), which is itself a wholly-owned subsidiary of the ultimate parent,  **Hitachi Ltd**  (Japan). [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-0a4b22ba20e825880c33d4bf35abeb99&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse355457&hl=fse355457&page=124)  As of March 2026, the promoter holding remains at 75.00%. [[5 • Equirus Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EQRS-d44be648a68e39e166ce42c1f011dc9f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse100&hl=fse100&page=1)  The company reported having no subsidiaries or joint ventures as of September 30, 2025. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-368630__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12320&hl=fse12320&page=4)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

In March 2025, the company raised approximately INR 2,520.82 crore via a  **Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP)** . [[7 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-e869bb0d0ca9940402b0571b92cd1cd3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2024&hl=fse2024,fse2190&page=2)  While approximately INR 1,500 crore of this was earmarked for capital expenditure—including a cumulative INR 4,000 crore investment plan for greenfield transformer facilities in Karjan—the management also highlighted that the funds provide "flexibility to react to any potential initiatives" and "strategic initiatives expansions". [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-605258434-1917741112&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse24167&hl=fse24167&page=1) [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-544850__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1899&hl=fse1899,fse2083&page=1)  As of March 31, 2026, the company held a substantial cash and bank balance of INR 4,689.50 crore. [[10 • ICICI Securities Limited] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ICIC-a67f68f406ab91512b51ba49146bdb52&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15087&hl=fse15087&page=8)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are no current signals of activist involvement or formal strategic reviews aimed at divestiture or restructuring.  Management's focus remains on leveraging its  **High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC)**  leadership and catering to high-growth segments like data centers and renewables. [[11 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-ffe105963c903b0f5eda4bb4ff9c4872&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse81016&hl=fse81016&page=36)  A minor regulatory reclassification of certain promoter group entities was completed in October 2023 to align with standard listing requirements. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-0a4b22ba20e825880c33d4bf35abeb99&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse594004&hl=fse594004&page=177)
