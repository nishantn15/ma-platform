## M&A Readiness Brief: Praj Industries Ltd (NSE: PRAJIND)

### Recent M&A and Strategic Activity

**Praj Industries**  has shifted its inorganic strategy toward technology-led joint ventures rather than outright acquisitions. [[1 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-141129-1983333179&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse42478&hl=fse42478&page=2)  Key recent moves include a  **JV with Bharat Petroleum (BPCL)**  (2024) to develop Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) projects and a partnership with  **Thyssenkrupp Uhde**  for polylactic acid (PLA) production. [[2 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68504f4514788506c7e3fd9b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17130&hl=fse17130,fse17179&page=9)  Historically, it entered the high-purity water segment through an acquisition in 2014, but recent years have focused on MOUs with majors like  **IOCL**  for Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) and ethanol. [[3 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68504f4514788506c7e3fd9b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse16656&hl=fse16656&page=9) [[4 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69a6042d9797c60007ea348a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15985&hl=fse15985&page=9)

> Praj is focusing on brownfield upgrades, lifecycle services, and new opportunities in international markets, where policy support for low-carbon fuels is strengthening. [[5 • JPMorgan Research • 05 Jun 26 • "In case you missed it, Asia’s Most Read Publications : 26 May to 1 Jun 2026"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-545da43ae86e3f05193f8d1efd3ed0a1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10627&hl=fse10627&page=3)

The company is led by promoter  **Dr. Pramod Chaudhari** , who holds a  **21.05%**  stake, with total promoter group holding at  **32.81%**  as of March 2026. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-376394__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse14724&hl=fse14724,fse14888&page=7)  The engineering arm,  **Praj Engineering & Infra** , has a minor non-controlling interest of  **0.33%** .
