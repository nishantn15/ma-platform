## M&A-Readiness Brief

**Raymond Lifestyle Limited (RLL)**  has undergone a transformative period of corporate restructuring to simplify its group structure into pure-play entities. [[1 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-08643e7981ba874dadb6f612833e37d9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4010&hl=fse4010&page=3)  A pivotal transaction occurred in April 2023 when the group sold its FMCG business to  **Godrej Consumer Products**  for INR 2,825 crore, which was instrumental in transitioning RLL to a net-cash position. [[2 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-08643e7981ba874dadb6f612833e37d9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9377&hl=fse9377&page=4) [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-68075e0a3bac3a671015712bd551c6ae&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29239&hl=fse29239&page=10)  RLL was officially listed on September 5, 2024, following its demerger from  **Raymond Ltd** . [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-376399__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse38257&hl=fse38257&page=16) [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-68075e0a3bac3a671015712bd551c6ae&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse63529&hl=fse63529&page=19)

> The lifestyle business was listed as Raymond Lifestyle Ltd. on September 5, 2024, while the real estate business was recently listed as Raymond Realty Ltd. on July 1, 2025 and Raymond Ltd. now comprise of the Engineering/ Auto Components and Aerospace businesses. [[6 • Antique Stockbroking • 18 Jul 25 • "Raymond Ltd.Ready for take off"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ANTQ-7eea00a90976c013085b84ab3b7e65e4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8776&hl=fse8776&page=5)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

The company is led by Executive Chairman  **Gautam Hari Singhania** , with the promoter group holding a 59.5% stake as of March 2026. [[7 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452880__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7557&hl=fse7557,fse7579&page=22) [[8 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-e8d71041b586bc9c26d1fe2204019940&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1055&hl=fse1055&page=1)  RLL operates a consolidated structure with key subsidiaries including  **Silver Spark Apparel Limited** ,  **Raymond Luxury Cottons Limited** , and international arms in the US, Ethiopia, and the Middle East. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-376399__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28139&hl=fse28139,fse28171,fse28203&page=12)

> Raymond lifestyle, post the split, has presence in four sub-segments including Branded Textiles, Branded Apparel, Garmenting and High-value cotton shirting. [[10 • Jefferies Research • 24 Sep 24 • "Feedback from 3rd Jefferies India Forum"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-3b5984859d257c3759ddb12dd0716c35&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34823&hl=fse34823&page=9)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

RLL maintains a robust balance sheet with a net-cash position of INR 179 crore as of FY26. [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452880__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5215&hl=fse5215&page=25)  The company’s primary capital allocation focus is an "asset-light" franchise model to expand its retail footprint, targeting 650+ new stores through FY27. [[12 • Motilal Oswal] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-MOTL-08643e7981ba874dadb6f612833e37d9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9462&hl=fse9462&page=4)  While organic expansion in branded apparel (e.g.,  **Ethnix** ,  **Sleepz** ) is the priority, management has signaled openness to inorganic opportunities. [[13 • Mint (New Delhi)] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-760fceea2de3e43d3da4d97d7b1ebba4db1fecd4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3860&hl=fse3860&page=1) [[14 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-452880__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7013&hl=fse7013&page=26)  Chairman Singhania recently noted that while lifestyle growth is largely organic, the group remains open to acquisitions in engineering or auto segments if opportunities arise. [[15 • Mint (New Delhi)] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-760fceea2de3e43d3da4d97d7b1ebba4db1fecd4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3860&hl=fse3860&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

There are currently no reported activist signals.  However, the company has concluded a massive strategic review that resulted in the vertical demerger of its  **Real Estate**  (listed July 2025) and  **Lifestyle**  businesses, aiming to unlock shareholder value by creating independent management teams for each vertical. [[16 • Antique Stockbroking] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ANTQ-7eea00a90976c013085b84ab3b7e65e4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8776&hl=fse8776&page=5) [[17 • BusinessWorld (India)] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-3d074697f637f9a91f2a269f1a6685cc4319a7e0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1317&hl=fse1317&page=1)  Management has declared FY27 as a "Year of Consolidation" focused on sustainable profitability and digital agility.
