## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Corporate Structure

Reliance Industries operates through a complex structure of over 200 subsidiaries, primarily organized under  **Jio Platforms**  (Digital),  **Reliance Retail Ventures**  (Retail), and  **Reliance New Energy** [[1 • Ambit Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AMBT-7443f16c00f44fc042b567edc4261444&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4898&hl=fse4898&page=2) . Significant recent activity includes the late 2024 consolidation of its media assets via the  **Star India**  merger and the acquisition of  **Paramount Global’s**  stake in  **Studio 18** , raising its effective shareholding in the  **JioStar**  entity to over 50% [[2 • Ambit Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AMBT-7443f16c00f44fc042b567edc4261444&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6108&hl=fse6108&page=2)

- In April 2026, the company signed a major  **INR 25,110 crore ($3 billion)**  15-year green ammonia supply agreement with  **Samsung C&T** [[3 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-e11f267534c15a49cd2320d36b732656&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse32003&hl=fse32003&page=13) . Other notable acquisitions include the health food brand  **Manna**  and Australia's  **Goodness Group Global**  to bolster its FMCG vertical,  **Reliance Consumer Products (RCPL)** [[4 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-e11f267534c15a49cd2320d36b732656&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse27119&hl=fse27119,fse27238&page=11) .

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Growth Signals

As of March 31, 2026, Reliance maintains a massive cash warchest of  **INR 2,43,400 crore** , with a robust credit profile and net leverage of approximately 0.9x [[5 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-f6930a4d854ba9d28c8a5c6b22a9ec80&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19053&hl=fse19053,fse19188&page=5)

- Management has signaled a shift to a "calibrated investment approach," moderating annual cash capex to approximately  **INR 1.4-1.5 trillion**  in FY25-26, down from previous peaks as it moves toward a monetization phase . [[6 • Ambit Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AMBT-bfcf8bd762af5d19287a4278ab36a9df&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2369&hl=fse2369,fse2433&page=1)  Future inorganic growth is expected to target "deep-tech" areas, including  **Agentic AI**  (via  **JioBrain** ),  **5G/6G core technology** , and green hydrogen . [[7 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2e8d5c581a29943571f489&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse840&hl=fse840&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Listing Signals

A formal strategic review is underway for the  **listing of Jio Platforms** , which the company targets for  **mid-2026** [[8 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-SGX-0020b8b4938deadce2e12095f9e179ab&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8470&hl=fse8470&page=5) . This IPO is viewed as a "defining milestone" to unlock value from its 524 million subscribers and shift toward a pure-play digital identity [[9 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-5a5c3fbce2bade76c6eecfa7fb29bcc4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2329&hl=fse2329&page=1)

- While no activist threats are currently documented, the company faces some pressure from Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) selling, which management aims to counter through improved disclosures and the successful execution of its  **New Energy**  giga-factories . [[10 • Ambit Capital] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AMBT-7443f16c00f44fc042b567edc4261444&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3970&hl=fse3970&page=1)  The promoter group, led by the  **Ambani Family** , maintains tight control with a  **49.11%**  holding . [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a2255540ea341e867ca1de5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse238245&hl=fse238245&page=32)
