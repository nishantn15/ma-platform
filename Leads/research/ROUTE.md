## Route Mobile M&A Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A Activity and Structure

In May 2024,  **Proximus Global**  completed the landmark acquisition of a 57.56% stake in  **Route Mobile**  for approximately INR 5,922.42 crore (EUR 643 million), followed by an open offer that initially raised its holding to 83.1%. [[2 • Auerbach Grayson] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AUBH-c1066c63dc4f65e22c1b7fd4e0036bba&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8706&hl=fse8706&page=4)

To comply with India's 25% minimum public shareholding (MPS) rule, Proximus conducted an Offer for Sale (OFS) in September 2024, reducing its stake to 74.90%. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68a480ec5811716a95436a13&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse111415&hl=fse111415,fse111603&page=29)

Route Mobile now operates as the core of Proximus’s "International" segment, integrated with  **BICS**  and  **TeleSign** . [[4 • Deutsche Bank Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DBR-47503abb05916a3d87abc0f66692f38b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4801&hl=fse4801&page=2)  The group maintains 35 subsidiaries globally, including major previous acquisitions like  **Masivian**  (Latin America) and  **MR Messaging**  (Europe). [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66a330731478856e00820300&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29066&hl=fse29066&page=21) [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fcd7eac92666de9a31c90f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse32415&hl=fse32415&page=10)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

Management has signaled a clear strategic shift toward "AI-led, capability-driven bolt-on acquisitions" as a primary pillar of its May 2026 growth playbook. [[7 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-34f495725e6762e573c8aacde571b6d5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1101&hl=fse1101&page=1)  With a cash reserve of INR 1,388.7 crore as of March 31, 2026, the company is targeting small-to-mid-sized companies in  **Conversational AI** ,  **Chatbots** , and  **CPaaS-adjacent**  areas. [[8 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fdf250f8568dd7569ddcd4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18017&hl=fse18017&page=22)  The goal is to compress the organic development of new technologies from 2–3 years to 12–18 months. [[9 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fdf250f8568dd7569ddcd4&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18081&hl=fse18081&page=22) [[10 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-34f495725e6762e573c8aacde571b6d5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3392&hl=fse3392&page=1)  Capital allocation also prioritizes shareholder returns, with a sustainable dividend payout increased to INR 16.50 per share for FY27. [[11 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fce3fdeff167341718253c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2078&hl=fse2078&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

While no formal activist pressure was identified, the company underwent a major "Strategic Reset" in early 2026 following integration challenges and a structural decline in its legacy ILD A2P SMS business. [[12 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-34f495725e6762e573c8aacde571b6d5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2936&hl=fse2936&page=1)  This review resulted in a leadership transition on February 9, 2026, where  **Tushar Agnihotri**  was elevated to CEO, while founder  **Rajdip Gupta**  shifted to Managing Director and Advisor to focus on Proximus Global partnerships. [[13 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-698b3c30fc34000007982ce3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2257&hl=fse2257&page=1) [[14 • Emkay Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-EMKR-159cbc4224b651554a6c83e8898eaa4f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1440&hl=fse1440&page=1)

Proximus also recorded a EUR 275 million non-cash impairment on its Global segment in early 2026, intensifying the focus on synergy execution. [[15 • Deutsche Bank Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-DBR-374c7b92395a8aa57b0edfb14215c411&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20355&hl=fse20355&page=8)
