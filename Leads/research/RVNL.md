## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent Activity and Structure

RVNL operates through an extensive network of  **21 SPVs** , including  **9 subsidiaries**  and  **13 joint ventures**  as of mid-2025. [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-25251964-1890624818&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19057&hl=fse19057&page=1) [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-eaaf599c2478225ed74f973598338c14&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse756065&hl=fse756065&page=362)  Major recent deals are primarily large-scale JVs, such as the  **INR 14,400 crore**  Vande Bharat sleeper project via  **Kinet Railway Solutions**  (with Metrowagonmash) and an  **INR 1,286 crore**  solar project with  **Jakson Green**  (January 2024). [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-eaaf599c2478225ed74f973598338c14&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse226297&hl=fse226297&page=115) [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-25251964-2001102979&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6466&hl=fse6466&page=1)  More recently, a JV with  **GPT Infraprojects**  emerged as the L1 bidder for an  **INR 1,201.35 crore**  bridge project in January 2026. [[7 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-bde427b7358fa9cd1c9b540fec85ec7d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1249&hl=fse1249,fse1380&page=1)  In May 2026, RVNL formalized a JV with  **Texmaco Rail & Engineering**  to expand its rolling stock and EPC presence. [[8 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a042d5a2f4b5c632bf9fbaa&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3671&hl=fse3671&page=5)

### Parent and Promoter Holding

The company remains a government-controlled PSU, with the  **President of India (Ministry of Railways)**  holding  **72.84%**  of equity shares as of March 31, 2025. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-eaaf599c2478225ed74f973598338c14&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse755436&hl=fse755436&page=362)  This follows a  **6.85%**  stake sale via an Offer for Sale (OFS) during FY2024. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-71cd9e567084b9a84001909fa57403d2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse815129&hl=fse815129&page=293)

### Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals

RVNL maintains a substantial cash "warchest" of  **INR 3,044.80 crore**  (as of March 31, 2025). [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-eaaf599c2478225ed74f973598338c14&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse577827&hl=fse577827&page=298)  Management signals a strong shift toward  **competitive bidding**  and  **inorganic expansion**  through international subsidiaries in  **Uzbekistan, Oman, Dubai, and South Africa**  to de-risk its portfolio from purely domestic rail projects. [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-25251964-1890624818&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29353&hl=fse29353&page=2) [[13 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-25251964-1943320061&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3033&hl=fse3033&page=1)  Recent entry into  **telecom (BharatNet)**  with a massive  **INR 13,200 crore**  order underscores this diversification intent. [[14 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-25251964-1931247178&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15390&hl=fse15390,fse15446&page=1)

### Strategic Review and Activist Activity

No activist activity or formal strategic reviews aimed at divestment were reported; however, the company is under continuous internal transition to manage its new "Navratna" status and expanding international footprint. [[15 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-eaaf599c2478225ed74f973598338c14&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse755199&hl=fse755199&page=362) [[16 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-25251964-1943320061&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1968&hl=fse1968&page=1)  High-profile monitorables include the turnaround of the  **Krishnapatnam Railway Company (KRCL)** , which recently turned profitable and is expected to sustain this over the next two years. [[17 • Prabhudas Lilladher] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-PRLL-be7c0adadf54cd74608aa2ffaedc3488&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse535711&hl=fse535711&page=167)
