## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent Activity and Corporate Structure

**Saregama India Ltd**  is a key entity within the  **RP-Sanjiv Goenka (RPSG) Group** , which maintains a 60.84% promoter stake as of March 2026. [[3 • Dion Global Solutions] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-7850c1caad3cdd4e404f52beb976fd6acb9f6a5c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse228&hl=fse228&page=1) [[4 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05982d15582fded1d97f21&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3565&hl=fse3565&page=3)  The company operates a global subsidiary structure including  **Saregama Limited (UK)** ,  **RPG Global Music Limited** , and  **Saregama FZE** . [[5 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-410667__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9775&hl=fse9775&page=7)

The company's M&A strategy has intensified recently, headlined by the acquisition of  **Pocket Aces**  in November 2023 for a total consideration of INR 375.14 crore, providing a major foothold in digital short-format content. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-689ef65471c087079c37adf9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse813540&hl=fse813540&page=350)  In January 2026,  **Saregama**  completed a strategic minority investment in  **Bhansali Productions** , securing a 28% to 49% stake. [[7 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-550733__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse17934&hl=fse17934,fse18210,fse18296&page=7)  This deal grants  **Saregama**  exclusive rights to all future film music from the production house under a pre-agreed formula, eliminating competitive bidding. [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9707980-1981223827&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5345&hl=fse5345,fse5500&page=1)  Additionally, subsidiary  **Pocket Aces**  acquired  **Finnet Media**  in November 2025 for INR 8.95 crore to bolster its influencer management vertical. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6982de67424996000725da54&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18468&hl=fse18468,fse18772&page=9)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

Management has signaled a clear inorganic intent, earmarking approximately INR 1,000 crore for content and strategic investments between FY25 and FY27. [[10 • Arihant Capital Markets] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-4dbbee3e28c9cdf386c953e404204684&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2265&hl=fse2265&page=1)  As of March 31, 2026, the company held a cash warchest of roughly INR 300 crore, comprising INR 111.20 crore in cash and bank balances plus INR 188.90 crore in mutual fund investments. [[11 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-550733__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19000&hl=fse19000&page=36)

### Strategic Review and Business Pivot

A major strategic shift is underway as  **Saregama**  consciously winds down its in-house film production business over 12–15 months to reallocate capital toward higher-margin IP licensing and its partnership with  **Bhansali Productions** . [[12 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9707980-1981223827&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9768&hl=fse9768,fse9886&page=1) [[13 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a05982d15582fded1d97f21&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1445&hl=fse1445,fse1523&page=1)  No activist investor signals were identified.
