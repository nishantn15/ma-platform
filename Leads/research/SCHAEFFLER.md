## M&A-Readiness Profile: Schaeffler India Ltd

**Recent M&A Activity** Schaeffler India's primary inorganic move in the last five years was the 100% cash acquisition of  **KRSV Innovative Auto Solutions (Koovers)** , completed in September 2023 for INR 142.40 crore . [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69d2d4cdadb5a2f0d60abe10&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse944418&hl=fse944418,fse944705&page=175)  This B2B e-commerce platform targets the automotive aftermarket and is expected to break even by 2027 . [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9429928-1967344254&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse43075&hl=fse43075&page=2)  Historically, the company consolidated its footprint in 2018 through a merger with  **INA Bearings India**  and  **LuK India** [[4 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-3b46676dc506ef06131878b5b92d4e96&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15623&hl=fse15623&page=7) .

**Corporate Structure** The company is part of the global  **Schaeffler Group** , with the ultimate parent entity being  **INA Holding Schaeffler GmbH & Co. KG** , Germany . [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69d95495fd928a53a7f436b9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse892930&hl=fse892930&page=163)  Promoter entities hold a total stake of 74.13% as of March 2026 .  [[6 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-3b46676dc506ef06131878b5b92d4e96&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse423&hl=fse423&page=1) **KRSV Innovative Auto Solutions**  remains its only wholly-owned subsidiary . [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69d2d4cdadb5a2f0d60abe10&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse952852&hl=fse952852&page=176)

**Capital Allocation and Inorganic Signals** As of December 31, 2025, Schaeffler India maintains a robust cash warchest of INR 963.80 crore . [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-400425__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse810541&hl=fse810541&page=150)  Capital allocation is disciplined, prioritizing a 30%–50% dividend payout and localized manufacturing investments . [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9429928-1967344254&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse45771&hl=fse45771&page=2) [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-400425__annual_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse80865&hl=fse80865&page=31)  Management indicates a readiness for further investment if market dynamics, such as "China Plus One" strategies, warrant it . [[11 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9429928-1940754321&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse48144&hl=fse48144&page=2)

**Strategic Review and Activist Signals** While no activist activity is reported, a major strategic integration is pending following the global merger of  **Schaeffler AG**  and  **Vitesco Technologies**  in October 2024 . [[12 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-1d5f922322ff1df4f2a85fbf6dd1fd64&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1215&hl=fse1215&page=1)  The India unit expects to eventually consolidate Vitesco’s local operations, which would add electronic and software capabilities to its powertrain portfolio . [[13 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-1d5f922322ff1df4f2a85fbf6dd1fd64&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3263&hl=fse3263&page=1) [[14 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-9429928-1932073090&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse33108&hl=fse33108&page=2)  Broker analysis suggests that strong parentage reduces the immediate need for broad inorganic capability builds, though M&A remains a potential lever for specific high-growth adjacencies like e-axles .
