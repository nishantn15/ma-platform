## Shakti Pumps (India) Ltd M&A-Readiness Brief

**M&A Activity and Capital Allocation** Research indicates zero external M&A deals in the last five years; instead, Shakti Pumps is focused on internal "inorganic-style" growth through new subsidiary formation . [[1 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-546568__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse40905&hl=fse40905&page=51)  In July 2025, the company raised INR 292.60 crore via a  **Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP)**  to fund a greenfield solar cell and module plant . [[2 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686b9771c67d8507cf08af41&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse438&hl=fse438&page=1)  Significant internal investments include INR 65 crore cumulative infusion into  **Shakti EV Mobility**  as of April 2026 and multiple tranches totaling over INR 40 crore into  **Shakti Energy Solutions Limited**  for solar manufacturing . [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fd8f9b134da341377abf1b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7302&hl=fse7302&page=4)

**Structure and Ownership** The company is led by Chairman  **Dinesh Patidar** , with a promoter group including the Patidar family and entities like  **Shakti Sons Trust** [[4 • ESG- Other topic-specific reports] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0c77540f58989640e80390&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse23615&hl=fse23615&page=15) . The corporate structure consists of five primary wholly-owned subsidiaries:  **Shakti Energy Solutions**  (domestic),  **Shakti EV Mobility**  (domestic), and foreign units in the  **USA** ,  **UAE** , and  **Bangladesh** [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69fd8f9c134da341377abf1c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2030&hl=fse2030&page=1) . Minority relationships exist with entities like  **Shakti Irrigation India Limited** , which are classified as enterprises where management exerts significant influence . [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0c77540f58989640e8038b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse20792&hl=fse20792&page=9)

**Inorganic Growth and Strategic Signals** Management has not signaled intent for external acquisitions, opting for a  **backward integration**  strategy to manufacture solar DCR cells and EV components in-house . [[7 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686b9771c67d8507cf08af41&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1181&hl=fse1181&page=1)  The "warchest" is largely committed to a  **INR 1,700 crore CapEx plan**  over two years . [[8 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20386596-1954934921&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5045&hl=fse5045&page=1)  As of March 31, 2025, consolidated cash and bank balances stood at  **INR 190.60 crore** [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-ae6e8c91502bc5097549808cdb16bebc&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse796769&hl=fse796769&page=287) . No activist signals or formal strategic reviews for divestiture were identified; current efforts focus on scaling the rooftop solar business to a leadership position by 2030 .
