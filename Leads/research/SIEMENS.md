## M&A and Structural Profile

**Siemens Ltd**  is currently executing a multi-year portfolio simplification strategy aligned with its global parent,  **Siemens AG** , which maintains a 75.00% promoter stake. [[1 • Auerbach Grayson] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AUBH-366ff625f68d5900844a127edd0250be&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6902&hl=fse6902&page=2)  A landmark demerger of the  **Energy**  business into  **Siemens Energy India Limited**  was completed in March 2025, involving a transfer of net assets valued at INR 3,784.60 crore. [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-539964__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse33673&hl=fse33673&page=12)  In May 2026, the company announced that the divestment of its  **Low Voltage Motors (LVM)**  business—valued at approximately INR 2,200.00 crore—is on track following CCI approval. [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-539964__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse36432&hl=fse36432&page=12) [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-419286__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse19678&hl=fse19678&page=7)

Management has signaled a clear intent for further inorganic growth, specifically targeting the "buildings area" to replicate the success of the  **C&S Electric**  acquisition. [[5 • Transcript - Shareholder/Analyst Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878441-1973569802&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29592&hl=fse29592&page=2)  The company is also streamlining its remaining structure, having approved the amalgamation of its wholly-owned subsidiary,  **Siemens Rail Automation Private Limited** , into the parent entity in early 2026. [[6 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-539964__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2382&hl=fse2382&page=1)

Capital allocation is focused on capacity expansion and localization to support a robust order book of INR 45,000.00 crore. [[7 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-f7c913b8c1282d15bf2d335caf42f161&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1125&hl=fse1125&page=1)

As of late 2025, the company maintained a net cash position of approximately INR 1,556.50 crore. [[8 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-9f6b167fe0535a35426d32f26eda7e66&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse21092&hl=fse21092&page=10)  While no activist signals are present, the company has undergone significant strategic reviews leading to the recent demerger and the transition of its financial year to an April-March cycle starting in 2026. [[9 • Transcript - Shareholder/Analyst Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-878441-1973569802&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2370&hl=fse2370&page=1) [[10 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1485174c496ff9e1bd2fd7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse910&hl=fse910&page=1)

Broker sentiment remains cautious due to margin headwinds in the mobility and digital industries segments despite strong order inflows from railways and data centers. [[11 • HSBC Global Investment Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-HSBC-f7c913b8c1282d15bf2d335caf42f161&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2447&hl=fse2447&page=1)
