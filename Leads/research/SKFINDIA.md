## M&A and Strategic Readiness Brief

**SKF India's**  recent M&A activity is dominated by a major internal restructuring rather than external acquisitions. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686e92b5581171515886b1d8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse22398&hl=fse22398,fse22594&page=18)  In October 2025, the company completed the demerger of its industrial business into  **SKF India (Industrial) Ltd** , which listed on December 5, 2025. [[8 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-536927__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse38332&hl=fse38332,fse38417&page=13) [[9 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-95d53dae23fd4c32ac4ce85e62c6608c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse10513&hl=fse10513&page=4)

This strategic separation follows a 1:1 share entitlement ratio aimed at unlocking value through dedicated management and capital allocation. [[10 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-536927__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse38131&hl=fse38131&page=13) [[11 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-1dbf8fc42dface63160a27c825b1dd2b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse907&hl=fse907&page=1)  Other recent transactions include minority equity investments in renewable energy special purpose vehicles (SPVs) like  **Sunstrength Renewables**  (26.74%) and  **Clean Max Taiyo**  (26.0%) to comply with captive power regulations. [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-fbc50b2858f7c605a9a2acb399026aee&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse179736&hl=fse179736&page=94) [[13 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-01b0c1c5f7a911be299424c92a66e266&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse388983&hl=fse388983&page=170)

The company is a subsidiary of the Swedish  **AB SKF**  group. [[14 • PROSPEQ] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-693296e0ecda80000725d5f3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse208201&hl=fse208201&page=73)  As of March 2026, the promoter holding stands at 52.58%, primarily held through  **SKF Vertevo AB**  (formerly  **SKF Interim AB** ). [[15 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-536927__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse70371&hl=fse70371,fse70564&page=22)  Following the demerger,  **SKF India**  no longer holds traditional operating subsidiaries, though it maintains its 26% interests in energy associates. [[16 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686e92b5581171515886b1d8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse259908&hl=fse259908&page=131)

Management has signaled a shift toward aggressive organic growth, doubling its annual capex run rate to INR 200–300 crore to enhance localization and expand capacity in  **Pune** ,  **Bangalore** , and  **Haridwar** . [[17 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-9f4ebed7fb6c0c795945a8d1238f7ded&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28417&hl=fse28417,fse28558&page=11)

The "cash warchest" remains substantial at INR 710.76 crore as of March 31, 2025, though recent dividend payouts have been high.
