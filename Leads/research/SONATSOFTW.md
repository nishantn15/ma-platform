## M&A and Corporate Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

**Sonata Software’s**  inorganic strategy was punctuated by the March 2023 acquisition of  **Quant Systems**  for USD 159 million (approx. INR 1,303 crore), its largest deal to date. [[3 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-672b63e1c07adf716e7ed5e8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse37360&hl=fse37360&page=14)  This transaction included a significant contingent consideration component, though recent adjustments led to a write-back of INR 65.38 crore after performance terms were renegotiated. [[4 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-549383__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse55639&hl=fse55639,fse55923,fse56111&page=19)  Other key deals in the five-year window include  **Encore Software Services**  (2021) and the finalization of the  **GBW**  and  **Scalable Data Systems**  integrations. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6694a5702e3cd104f1e74f7e&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse78291&hl=fse78291&page=59)  Currently, management states there are no plans to grow through M&A leverage in the near term, focusing instead on capability expansion. [[6 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-1514022-1998440083&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse23478&hl=fse23478&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

The company is widely held with a promoter holding of  **28.20%**  as of March 2026. [[7 • Anand Rathi Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-ANRS-8ffe7894043b13565b6d7a241ef77701&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse475&hl=fse475&page=1)  It operates through approximately 22 subsidiaries globally. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68839ec2b47f8506daa4874f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4856&hl=fse4856&page=3)   **Sonata**  is currently executing a major corporate simplification, merging subsidiaries like  **Encore IT Services**  and  **Sonata Software LLC**  into their respective regional parents (SSNA in the US and the standalone entity in India) to reduce compliance costs and improve operational efficiency. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-672b63e1c07adf716e7ed5e8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse36229&hl=fse36229&page=14) [[10 • Moneycontrol] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-19ee1d921411767a15e882b82bf976c7&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse184&hl=fse184,fse382&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

As of Q4 FY26 (May 2026),  **Sonata**  maintains a gross cash position of  **INR 605.5 crore** .
