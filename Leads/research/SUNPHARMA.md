## M&A and Capital Allocation Profile

### Recent Deal Activity

Sun Pharma's M&A strategy has shifted from "tuck-in" acquisitions to transformative global deals. [[3 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-141232-1979322912&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12692&hl=fse12692,fse12875&page=1)  On April 27, 2026, the company announced its largest-ever acquisition of  **Organon & Co.**  for an enterprise value of  **US$11.75 billion**  (approx. INR 99,287 crore). [[4 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-a33f1dd60e37c472b54593360d805d94&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2741&hl=fse2741&page=1)

This all-cash transaction aims to establish Sun as a global leader in Women's Health and Biosimilars. [[5 • JPMorgan Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-b49c30b800941848ecf88a937a139bcd&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse6431&hl=fse6431&page=3)

Other notable recent deals include the  **US$355 million**  acquisition of  **Checkpoint Therapeutics**  (March 2025) to bolster its onco-derm franchise and the  **US$576 million**  acquisition of  **Concert Pharmaceuticals**  (January 2023) for alopecia treatments. [[6 • Auerbach Grayson] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AUBH-4313f6fa8cd730c297b8c316591769b3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3481&hl=fse3481,fse3718&page=2) [[7 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-567804__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse35921&hl=fse35921&page=56)  The company also completed the merger of its long-standing subsidiary  **Taro Pharmaceutical Industries**  in 2024 to consolidate its dermatology leadership. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-668bd28071c08707bd84203a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5547&hl=fse5547,fse5665&page=2)

> Today, Organon is a global healthcare company with a portfolio of over 70 products across Women's Health and General Medicines (which includes Biosimilars and Established Brands), focusing on addressing health needs that uniquely, disproportionately, or differently affect women, while expanding access to essential treatments in more than 140 countries and territories, selling products through various channels including drug wholesalers and retailers, hospitals, government agencies, and managed healthcare providers. Organon has a legacy spanning 100 years, with a proven ability to develop complex products such as Nexplanon and NuvaRing, and Sun Pharma's Ilumya and many other innovative biologics have roots in Organon labs. [[9 • JPMorgan Research • 27 Apr 26 • "Sun Pharma : Acquires US-based Organon for $11.75bn"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JPMR-b49c30b800941848ecf88a937a139bcd&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11787&hl=fse11787,fse12308&page=4)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is promoter-led, with  **Dilip Shanghvi**  and associated entities (primarily  **Shanghvi Finance Private Limited** ) holding a  **54.48%**  stake as of March 31, 2025. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68689ace71c08707dce621b6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse266091&hl=fse266091&page=88)  Sun Pharma operates through a vast network of at least 94 subsidiaries globally. [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-539644__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse41576&hl=fse41576&page=14)  Key domestic subsidiaries include  **Sun Pharma Laboratories Limited** , while international operations are funneled through entities in the  **US** ,  **Israel**  (Taro),  **Japan** , and  **Mexico** . [[12 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-668bd2d871c08707bd842046&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse173734&hl=fse173734,fse173793&page=49)

> Organon acquisition at $11.75 Bn price tag adds about $1.5-2Bn in Synergies over next two-three years. [[13 • Bernstein Research • 21 May 26 • "India Healthcare: Innovation Power Decade: Rainmakers - initiating on six stocks with a positive sector view"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-BERN-4e97c2cab001f96264fb43304866e5f8&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5794&hl=fse5794&page=2)

### Inorganic Growth Signals

Management has explicitly flagged continued interest in acquisitions that strengthen their  **Global Specialty**  portfolio, particularly in dermatology, ophthalmology, and oncology. [[14 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68689ace71c08707dce621b6&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25681&hl=fse25681&page=11)  With a net cash position of  **US$3.20 billion**  (approx. INR 27,040 crore) reported in May 2026, the company possesses significant "firepower" for further deals. [[15 • Earnings Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a10394e8667f002910ae158&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1904&hl=fse1904&page=3)  While the  **Organon**  deal will involve taking on approximately  **US$9.25 billion to US$9.75 billion**  in debt, Sun intends to use its strong free cash flow (projected at US$2.50 billion annually) to deleverage rapidly. [[16 • CLSA] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-a33f1dd60e37c472b54593360d805d94&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse8248&hl=fse8248&page=3)
