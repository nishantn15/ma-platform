## M&A-Readiness Profile

### Recent Activity and Corporate Structure

**Sun TV Network** 's recent M&A has shifted from traditional media to sports. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-BSE-14e5f73c02f36e8ed9899bb7aec69e52&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse657301&hl=fse657301&page=257)

The company is 75% owned by promoter  **Kalanithi Maran**  as of March 2026, leaving it largely insulated from external takeover threats. ### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

With a "cash warchest" of approximately INR 6,300 crore in cash and mutual fund investments,  **Sun TV**  maintains one of the strongest balance sheets in the Indian media sector. [[10 • Elara Securities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=SSRFS-00-455498784c5d9e91d32e7c2425286158&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2427&hl=fse2427&page=1)

Future inorganic intent is primarily signaled through global T20 cricket opportunities, which analysts view as the company's primary growth engine as legacy TV advertising faces structural resets. [[11 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-50f247d04482d13f220a0a40c1cfde82&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse100&hl=fse100&page=1) [[12 • Avendus Spark] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-AVSP-3c468e01e42732cc7c12f820a2f1cb97&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3728&hl=fse3728&page=1)
