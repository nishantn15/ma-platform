## M&A-Readiness Brief

**Recent M&A Activity** Supreme Industries' most significant recent transaction was the  **INR 302.08 crore**  slump-sale acquisition of  **Wavin India’s**  plastic pipe business, effective August 1, 2025. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d1c0d5d48fe29af7859a5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse605683&hl=fse605683&page=173)  This deal added three manufacturing units with 71,000 MT capacity and 120 sales personnel. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d1c0d5d48fe29af7859a5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse605683&hl=fse605683&page=173) [[4 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20375456-1967378709&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse18745&hl=fse18745&page=1)  Previously, the company acquired  **Parvati Agro Plast**  in FY2023-24 to address specific regional capacity requirements. [[5 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-20375456-1888215892&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse50086&hl=fse50086&page=2)

**Corporate Structure** The company is controlled by the  **Taparia family**  through promoter entities including  **Boon Investment** ,  **Jovial Investment** , and  **Venktesh Investment** , which collectively hold  **48.96%**  as of March 31, 2026. [[6 • OWNER] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69df799c722b0b0468bd900a&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse12189&hl=fse12189,fse12245,fse12298&page=18) [[7 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-540571__slides&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse11558&hl=fse11558&page=20)  Its structure includes one wholly-owned foreign subsidiary,  **The Supreme Industries Overseas FZE**  (Dubai), and a  **30.78%**  stake in associate  **Supreme Petrochem Ltd** . [[8 • CIRC] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a227b3ebd1ac16fabb5e920&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4137&hl=fse4137,fse4175&page=3)

**Capital Allocation and Growth Signals** Supreme maintains a "robust, debt-free balance sheet" with a net cash surplus of  **INR 648.45 crore**  as of March 31, 2026. [[9 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d1c0d5d48fe29af7859a5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse202106&hl=fse202106&page=61)  Management explicitly states an intent to explore both  **organic and inorganic growth**  avenues, focusing on businesses that offer technical synergy or fill capacity gaps. [[10 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d1c0d5d48fe29af7859a5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse201648&hl=fse201648&page=61)  The company planned an  **INR 1,000 crore**  capex for FY2026-27, entirely funded through internal accruals. [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a1d1c0d5d48fe29af7859a5&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse206692&hl=fse206692&page=62)

**Strategic and Activist Signals** No activist signals or formal third-party strategic reviews were reported in FY2025-26.  Internal strategic focuses remain centered on  **digital transformation** ,  **cost optimization** , and maintaining  **ROCE in excess of 25%**  across all verticals.
