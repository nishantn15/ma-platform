## M&A and Corporate Structure Profile

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

Syngene recently executed two major asset acquisitions to pivot toward large-molecule manufacturing. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6862687ec67d8507af09735b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse13264&hl=fse13264&page=11)  In December 2023, it completed the acquisition of a multi-modal biologics facility ( **Unit 3** ) from  **Stelis Biopharma**  for INR 617 crore (~$86 million), adding 20,000L of drug substance capacity. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66889b045811712f95282565&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse519018&hl=fse519018,fse519161&page=166)  In March 2025,  **Syngene USA Inc.**  acquired a biologics site in Baltimore from  **Emergent BioSolutions**  for ~$36 million (INR 301.10 crore), expanding total single-use bioreactor capacity to 50,000L. [[4 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-686092c871c0877aade6a812&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse215118&hl=fse215118,fse215404&page=91)  As of March 31, 2026, Syngene maintains a "warchest" with a net cash balance of  **INR 1,800.30 crore** . ### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

Syngene is a subsidiary of  **Biocon Limited** , which holds a  **53%**  promoter stake as of March 2026. ### Strategic Signals and Leadership Transition

Inorganic intent remains high for "adjacent modalities" like  **ADCs**  and  **peptides** , though current focus has shifted to organic utilization of newly acquired capacity. [[10 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-67d27bb6b47f8540a249b5a1&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7791&hl=fse7791&page=7) [[11 • Expert Call] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=EC-1000000-199964&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34815&hl=fse34815&page=2)  No activist signals were identified, but a significant leadership transition occurred in April 2026: founder  **Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw**  transitioned to Executive Chairperson, and  **Siddharth Mittal**  (former Biocon CEO) was appointed MD & CEO effective July 1, 2026. [[12 • Press Release] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-69f21c5e030817f73e8259a0&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4531&hl=fse4531,fse4679,fse4839&page=2)  Management has guided for a "transient" FY27 with flat revenue growth due to destocking of the  **Librela**  product by a major client.
