TBO Tek Ltd (NSE: TBOTEK) is actively consolidating the fragmented global B2B travel distribution market, recently completing its largest acquisition to date with the $125 million purchase of U.S.-based Classic Vacations in October 2025. This deal significantly expands TBO's footprint in the high-margin luxury travel segment and North American market, following a string of European and regional acquisitions including Jumbonline and BookaBed.

## M&A-Readiness Profile: TBO Tek Ltd

### Recent M&A Activity and Strategy

TBO Tek has established M&A as a primary growth lever, completing several acquisitions to enhance supply depth and geographical reach. [[1 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-691744987b6d1b00077178c2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15080&hl=fse15080&page=32)  The most significant recent transaction was the  **$125 million (approx. INR 1,050 crore)**  acquisition of  **Classic Vacations**  on October 1, 2025, which provides a gateway into the luxury travel advisor segment in the U.S. [[2 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-QUART-TQTR-408651__interim_report&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse29261&hl=fse29261&page=8)  Previous acquisitions include  **Jumbonline**  (INR 219.6 crore in 2023) for European expansion and  **BookaBed**  (INR 90.4 crore in 2023) to increase market share in Ireland and the UK. [[3 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-691744987b6d1b00077178c2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15445&hl=fse15445&page=32)  Management intends to continue acquiring companies with strong supply or distribution capabilities to drive network effects. [[4 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-691744987b6d1b00077178c2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15080&hl=fse15080&page=32)

### Corporate Structure and Ownership

The company is promoter-led, with  **Gaurav Bhatnagar**  and  **Ankush Nijhawan**  heading the group; total promoter holding stood at  **44.41%**  as of March 2025. [[5 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68764151581171162c89a372&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse254467&hl=fse254467&page=84)  Significant institutional backers include  **General Atlantic** , which held approximately  **14.4%**  post-IPO. [[6 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-edd88658bb7ef27613f7f1c0aeb4a396&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse122537&hl=fse122537&page=49)  TBO operates its international business primarily through its wholly-owned subsidiary  **Tek Travels DMCC**  (Dubai), which governs a network of over 12 step-down subsidiaries across Europe, Asia, and the Americas. [[7 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-edd88658bb7ef27613f7f1c0aeb4a396&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse122861&hl=fse122861,fse122999&page=49)

### Capital Allocation and Signals

As of March 31, 2025, TBO maintained a cash warchest of  **INR 1,285.06 crore** , bolstered by its 2024 IPO proceeds. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-68764151581171162c89a372&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse303458&hl=fse303458,fse303608&page=100)  Capital is strictly prioritized for "value-accretive" acquisitions and technology investments to drive operating leverage. [[9 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-691744987b6d1b00077178c2&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse15815&hl=fse15815,fse15850&page=32)  Management has highlighted that integration synergies from Classic Vacations are expected to drive margin expansion through 2026. [[10 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-3c2405ba2aad4423c880e9fbf468160b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse112966&hl=fse112966&page=44)  No activist or formal strategic review signals were identified in current research; however, the company faces potential risks from AI-driven disruption and competition from global OTAs. [[11 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-ff7b27912b45347a89088b842651aece&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25722&hl=fse25722&page=11)
