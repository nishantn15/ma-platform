Timken India Ltd (TIMKEN) is currently a net-cash entity with a clear mandate from its global parent to pursue both organic expansion and opportunistic acquisitions to deepen its industrial and rail presence in the Indian market. The company recently integrated its first significant domestic acquisition in years, Timken GGB, and continues to signal a high readiness for further inorganic growth given its debt-free balance sheet.

## M&A and Strategic Profile

### Recent M&A Activity

Timken India (TIL) completed the acquisition of  **Timken GGB Technology Private Limited**  in December 2025 for INR 128.80 crore. [[1 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0bff5a6f8497e69abf5be3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse27730&hl=fse27730,fse28009&page=9)  Following the acquisition, the Board approved a scheme of amalgamation on May 18, 2026, to merge the entity into TIL to simplify corporate structure and realize operational synergies. [[2 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0c61c5fcdb145e15fd251d&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2243&hl=fse2243&page=2)  In March 2026, the company also made a minor strategic investment of INR 0.70 crore to acquire a 26.10% stake in  **Sunstream Green Energy**  under a group captive scheme for renewable power. [[3 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0bff5a6f8497e69abf5be3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse5984&hl=fse5984,fse6061&page=1)

### Parent and Subsidiary Structure

The company is a 51.05% subsidiary of  **The Timken Company**  (USA), which holds its stake primarily through  **Timken Singapore Pte. Limited** . [[4 • Nuvama Institutional Equities] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-2febb72e87a35bde3c7f861fdfdab86f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse423&hl=fse423&page=1)

The parent company reduced its stake from 57.70% to 51.05% in May 2024 via a block trade valued at approximately $233 million (INR ~1,940 crore). [[5 • Jefferies Research] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-JFR-b538aa73a434b72c1b40ca5e57434799&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse7374&hl=fse7374&page=3)  TIL currently operates one direct wholly owned subsidiary, Timken GGB, pending its legal amalgamation. [[6 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0bff5a6f8497e69abf5be3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse36221&hl=fse36221&page=12)

> We are raising TP to INR4,000 (from INR3,700), based on 45x FY28E earnings, led by higher earnings assumption and inclusion of the recent acquisition of Timken GGB. [[7 • Nuvama Institutional Equities • 19 May 26 • "BANYAN SERIES Timken India TMKN IN INR 3 490 BUY - Bharuch plant ramp-up to drive growth Result Update"] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ASR-NVMA-2febb72e87a35bde3c7f861fdfdab86f&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1069&hl=fse1069&page=1)

### Capital Allocation and Growth Signals

TIL remains a debt-free company with cash and equivalents of INR 319.20 crore as of March 31, 2026. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0bff5a6f8497e69abf5be3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse34622&hl=fse34622&page=11)  Management has explicitly signaled a readiness for M&A, stating they are "always on the lookout" for opportunities to expand their size range and technological capabilities. [[9 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-8350234-1999594378&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse28036&hl=fse28036&page=2)  The company intends to leverage its cash for investment in growth rather than higher dividend payouts, maintaining a disciplined approach to capital expenditure, which reached ~12% of revenue in FY25. [[10 • Transcript - Earnings Calls] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=ET-8350234-1999594378&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse31211&hl=fse31211&page=2) [[11 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-687f246fc67d8507e0be022c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1714&hl=fse1714&page=4)

### Strategic Review and Activist Signals

Research did not surface any formal strategic review or activist investor activity targeting the company.  The primary strategic focus remains on the ramp-up of the  **Bharuch**  facility and localizing product lines from the global parent's portfolio.
