## M&A-Readiness Brief

### Recent M&A Activity and Capital Allocation

**Welspun Enterprises (WEL)**  has undergone a structural transformation through high-value divestments and niche acquisitions. [[1 • CAPITAL] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-1daf45195657fd8a405180576c0c151b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2190&hl=fse2190,fse2263,fse2323,fse2372&page=7)  A defining milestone was the June 2022 agreement to divest a portfolio of six road assets to  **Actis**  for an enterprise value of approximately INR 9,000 crore. [[2 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-691593781a2c5f000771bea9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse2051&hl=fse2051,fse2086&page=4)  In August 2024, the company completed the exit of its 50% stake in  **Dewas Bhopal Road project (DBCL)**  for INR 184.1 crore, achieving a 2.15x investment multiple. [[3 • IRPRESS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-cb9a57292537eeb44b6b45ddd47e6c40&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse1256&hl=fse1256,fse1358,fse1407&page=1)  On the buy-side, WEL acquired a 50.1% stake in  **Michigan Engineers Private Limited**  (now  **WMEL** ) in early 2023, subsequently increasing its holding to 60.09% in 2024 to bolster its tunneling and water capabilities. [[4 • CAPITAL] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-1daf45195657fd8a405180576c0c151b&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse3183&hl=fse3183,fse3213&page=8)  Most recently, in October 2024, WEL acquired a further 9.99% stake in  **WMEL**  from  **Patel Engineering**  for approximately USD 11.89 million (INR 99.58 crore). [[5 • MarketLine] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=MTN-d2ebb2687eee5d06b855c499ed7748bc3b844b41&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse856&hl=fse856&page=1)

### Corporate Structure and Promoter Holding

Part of the  **Welspun World**  conglomerate, WEL is led by Chairman  **Balkrishan Goenka** . [[6 • Investor Presentation] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-691593781a2c5f000771bea9&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse4391&hl=fse4391&page=8)  As of March 31, 2025, the promoter group holds a 55.31% stake. [[7 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-689db66dc67d8507cf2979bd&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse370135&hl=fse370135&page=72)  The company operates through a network of 13 subsidiaries and several joint ventures, including a 35% stake in  **Adani Welspun Exploration Limited**  for oil and gas interests. [[8 • ARS] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-66dd398d581171638fd6203c&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse322595&hl=fse322595&page=66)

### Inorganic Growth Signals

WEL maintains an "asset-light" strategy, holding a standalone cash warchest of INR 1,728 crore as of March 2026. [[9 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0610e5e8778115d04b57c3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse25194&hl=fse25194&page=15)  Management explicitly stated an intent to continue exploring inorganic opportunities that align with its focus on water technology and complex urban infrastructure. [[10 • ESG] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=WEB-EX-NSE-838172460c6d9ab2cbb8fe45f71293dd&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse9506&hl=fse9506&page=5)  Recent capital raises, including a warrant allotment of INR 1,000 crore in late 2025, provide significant flexibility for future acquisitions. [[11 • INTRM] ](https://research.alpha-sense.com?docid=GDS-6a0610e5e8778115d04b57c3&scope=ANYWHERE&stmt=fse83147&hl=fse83147&page=37)  No major activist pressures or formal strategic reviews were identified, as the current management is proactively executing a well-defined value-unlocking roadmap.
