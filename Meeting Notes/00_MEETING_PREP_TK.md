## Quick Context
TK shared a 6-page concept document for a **Mid-Market M&A Leadership Platform** -- a national ecosystem targeting Indian companies with INR 250-2,500 crore revenue. He wants your evaluation and brainstorming input.

---

## What the Platform Proposes (in 60 seconds)

A tiered membership platform (INR 85L to 2.25 Cr/year) that covers the **entire M&A lifecycle**:
1. **Strategy forums** -- CXO/Corp Dev roundtables, cross-industry cohorts
2. **Capability building** -- diagnostics, training by IIT/IIM faculty, playbooks
3. **Deal sourcing** -- daily monitoring, weekly target refreshes, curated reports
4. **Post-merger integration** -- Day 1 to Year 1 support, dashboards, lessons library
5. **Policy influence** -- NITI Aayog/CCI advisory engagement

**Key differentiator:** One Platform Partner per industry cohort (exclusivity = safe space + competitive moat)

---

## Why This Idea Has Legs (Data-Backed)

### The Market is Massive
- **115,000+ mid-market enterprises** in India (INR 200-2,000 Cr revenue)
- Contribute **>42% of India's GDP**, employ **>51 million people**
- Mid-market acquirers account for **~50% of India's M&A activity** (Bain)
- India M&A hit **$113 billion in 2025** (+42% YoY)

### The Problem is Real
- **70-90% of M&A deals** globally fail to deliver projected value
- **83% fail to boost shareholder returns**
- **47% employee turnover** in Year 1 post-acquisition; 75% by Year 3
- In India: **38% of executives** report talent retention challenges post-deal (vs. 30% global avg)
- Only **30% of Indian executives** begin culture fit assessment before diligence (vs. 44% globally)
- **40% of Indian mergers face business disruption** -- poor integration capability
- Most mid-market companies **lack dedicated corporate development teams**

### The Whitespace is Real
- **No platform globally** combines strategy forums + capability building + deal sourcing + PMI support
- Closest comparisons (Axial, Vistage, IMAA, DealRoom) each do only 1-2 of these
- IIM/ISB M&A programs are short-duration, one-off courses (3-5 days) -- no ongoing ecosystem
- **Post-merger integration training is virtually nonexistent** in Indian executive education

### Tailwinds Are Strong
- KKR, Carlyle, BlackRock all **expanding India operations massively** (India = new Asia buyout HQ)
- CCI streamlined merger approvals (210 to 150 days), 97% approval rate
- PLI schemes (INR 1.97 lakh crore across 13 sectors) creating M&A opportunities
- 2 lakh+ startups, 120+ unicorns creating M&A pipeline

## Strengths of the Proposal

1. **Full-lifecycle approach** -- no competitor covers strategy through PMI
2. **Exclusivity model** -- one partner per industry cohort creates trust + premium pricing justification
3. **Institutional anchors** -- IIT Roorkee (host), IIT Delhi, IIM Bangalore add credibility
4. **Policy access** -- NITI Aayog/CCI engagement is unique and high-value
5. **Strong ROI narrative** -- INR 2.25 Cr replaces 5-7 Cr in consulting; one deal = 20-50x ROI
6. **Tiered pricing** -- growth ramp from 85L to 2.25 Cr captures different segments

## Questions & Concerns to Raise with TK

### On the Concept
1. **Who created this?** Is TK the author or evaluating for someone? What's his role?
2. **What stage is this at?** Concept on paper? Pilot launched? Any traction?
3. **What's the ask for you/PwC?** Advisory? Co-creation? Investment? Partnership?

### On Viability
4. **How many industry cohorts are planned?** If only 10 cohorts at INR 2.25 Cr = INR 22.5 Cr. Is that enough to fund the platform's infrastructure?
5. **Who is the team?** Building deal sourcing intelligence, PMI dashboards, and running IIT/IIM workshops requires significant talent
6. **What's the go-to-market?** Which industry cohorts first? How are you reaching CXOs?
7. **What's the unit economics?** Platform Partner pricing at 2.25 Cr seems high for a startup platform -- what do early partners get in return for the risk?

### On Differentiation
8. **Deal sourcing vs. existing players** -- How does this compete with Avendus, Equirus, boutique IBs on target sourcing?
9. **NITI Aayog/CCI claim** -- Is there an actual MOU or just aspiration? This is a key credibility marker
10. **IIT/IIM partnership status** -- Confirmed or proposed? IIT Roorkee as "host" is specific -- what does that mean operationally?

### On Market Fit
11. **Pricing sensitivity** -- For INR 250 Cr companies, 2.25 Cr is ~1% of revenue. Is that palatable for a platform membership?
12. **PE firms as partners** -- KKR, BlackRock, Carlyle are named as potential anchors but they have in-house teams for everything this platform offers. What's their incentive?
13. **Competing priorities** -- Mid-market firms are fighting for survival and growth. Is M&A capability a priority they'd pay for?
