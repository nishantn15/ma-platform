## Key Strategy Decisions

### Go-to-Market Approach
1. **Talk to relevant CXOs** -- discovery conversations first
2. **Don't pitch directly** -- position it as wanting to understand from their lens about the need for the platform
3. **Get them interested in the platform concept** organically through conversation
4. **Then pitch** to get them onto the platform as partners

### Phased Evolution Model
5. **Phase 1: Begin as a Leadership Training Institute** -- this is the entry point
6. **Phase 2: Then evolve into a full platform** -- dashboards, tracking, monitoring ability, plus consulting -- the platform kicks in at this stage
7. **Phase 3: Crawl and build target list data** in the platform -- another tangible deliverable
8. **Important: Platform must NOT distract from intangibles** -- the core is learning modules and leadership development

### The Core Product: Executive Course
9. **5-day executive course** for corporate business development teams
10. **Core promise: "How to think about acquisitions successfully"** -- that's the headline
11. **Mock out the Leadership Institute section** first -- this is priority
12. **Credentialing using institute-driven model** -- supply side, certification/credential from premier institutions

### Curriculum Design
13. **Curriculum TBD** -- need to define what the 3-to-5 day curriculum looks like, including:
    - Aha moments
    - Tangible takeaways
    - Frameworks and toolkits

### Vision / Ideal Scenario
14. **Example: Aditya Birla as a partner/sponsor**
    - They send a 10-member Corp Dev team
    - 7-8 members get coached
    - 3 times a year (recurring engagement)
    - Depth increases over time
    - Real integration cases
    - Real consulting alongside the training

### USP vs. Existing College Exec Programs
15. **Key differentiators from standard IIM/IIT exec programs:**
    - **Duration:** With us for an entire year (not a 3-5 day one-off)
    - **Not just training:** Give toolkits, frameworks, and tangible platform access
    - **Platform integration:** The tangible tech platform (dashboards, tracking, deal sourcing) kicks in alongside the training
    - **Real advisory:** Advising them during actual integrations
    - **PMI prep:** Preparing corporate M&A teams for real integrations
    - **On top of:** Everything a normal exec training course at a premier institute offers

### Naming Consideration
- Need a name that captures: **Consulting + Training + Actual Real-time Tracking/Tracing Platform**
- It's a hybrid model -- not purely any one of these
