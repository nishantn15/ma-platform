## 1. Course Architecture — Pivot to Account-Based Onboarding

### Core message
> **We are not selling 3–5 day course blocks. We are onboarding companies. Companies are the account.**

Once a company is onboarded, **all 3 layers** of leadership training unlock for that account — to develop the firm holistically and strategically on acquisition + integration capability.

### The three layers

| Layer | Audience | Duration | Core promise |
|-------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **L1 — Enterprise Strategy** | CXOs, promoters, board, group heads, corp dev heads, CFO/CHRO/CSO, PE operating partners | **2.5 days + capstone** | Strategic judgment, governance discipline, failure recognition, stakeholder conviction |
| **L2 — Execution Leaders** | BU heads, VPs, functional leaders (strategy/finance/HR/IT/ops/sales/transformation), integration leaders | **5 days (Day 5 half-day)** | Convert deal thesis → Day 1 readiness → 100-day execution, workstreams, synergy tracking, governance |
| **L3 — Implementation Champions / Budding M&A Leaders** | Senior managers, managers, analysts, workstream leads, high-potential talent, integration champions, sales/marketing integrators | **5 days (Day 5 half-day)** | M&A fluency, role clarity, escalation discipline, communication confidence, ground-level integration leadership |

### Critical points from TK
- 3 layers in the company are critical for holistic & strategic development
- Companies need all 3 once onboarded — not pick-and-choose
- Overall capability development for the firm: "to know acquisition and integration of companies"

## 3. L1 — Enterprise M&A Leaders (2.5-day Closed-Door Forum)

**Positioning:** Premium discussion-led leadership forum for people who approve, sponsor, govern, and communicate acquisitions. Should not feel like a compressed M&A course. Should feel like a **boardroom-quality peer forum.**

### Design principles
- Fewer sessions, more dialogue
- Curated failure cases, boardroom dilemmas, peer debate
- Practical leadership artifacts

### Social architecture
- Networking lunch Day 1 & Day 2
- Curated dinner Day 1
- Informal peer time Day 2 evening
- Departure-friendly lunch Day 3

### Signature feature
**M&A Failure Case Discussion** — inspired by *Billion Dollar Lessons*. Tailored to acquisition failures, value leakage, cultural mismatch, synergy overclaiming, integration governance breakdowns.

### Day-by-day

**Day 1 — Strategic Judgment & Failure Recognition**
- Opening provocation: *Why good companies make bad acquisitions*
- Session 1: The M&A leadership problem
- Session 2: Failure case studies in M&A
- Session 3: Deal thesis & boardroom judgment
- Curated networking dinner
- **Outputs:** M&A Failure Pattern Map; Board-Level Deal Approval Checklist

**Day 2 — Governance, Integration Oversight, Stakeholder Conviction**
- Session 4: Executive integration governance
- Session 5: Stakeholder storytelling in M&A
- Session 6: Building repeatable M&A capability
- Late afternoon capstone briefing + team prep
- **Outputs:** Executive Integration Governance Model; M&A Leadership Narrative Template; Enterprise M&A Capability Roadmap

**Day 3 (Half) — Morning Capstone & Closure**
- Capstone: M&A Leadership War Room
- Plenary: takeaways + leadership commitments
- Photos, certificates, closing lunch, departures
- **Outputs:** Capstone recommendations (approve/reject/approve-with-conditions); top risks; integration governance; stakeholder narrative; first 100-day leadership priorities
