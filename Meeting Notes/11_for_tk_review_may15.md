## 2. Architecture v5 — incorporates your Apr 28 feedback

### The pivot we made
- From: selling 3-5 day course blocks to individuals
- To: **onboarding companies as accounts**, all 3 layers unlock together

### Three layers
| | L1 Enterprise | L2 Execution | L3 Champions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | 2.5 days + capstone | 5 days (Day 5 half) | 5 days (Day 5 half) |
| **Audience** | CXOs, board, group heads, promoters, corp dev, CFO/CHRO/CSO, PE op partners | VPs, BU heads, integration leaders, functional heads | Sr managers, analysts, sales/marketing integrators, high-potential talent |
| **Promise** | Strategic judgment, boardroom governance | Deal thesis → 100-day execution | Ground-level integration fluency |
| **Status** | ✓ Thought through by TK (incorporated verbatim) | Refined (your relook needed) | Refined (your relook needed) |
| **Signature feature** | Billion-Dollar Lessons (curated failure cases) | Sim-heavy: Deal Thesis Defense → Announcement Day Tabletop → IMO Sprint → Synergy Pressure Test → CXO Panel Capstone | Rumour-Mill Sim · 4-scenario carousel · Personal Day-1 Monday Action Plan |

### Account & commercial model (now coherent in v5)
- All 3 tiers (Platform Partner / Elite Member / Growth Member) onboard accounts and unlock all 3 layers
- **What varies = depth and capacity**, not layer access:
  - Platform Partner (Rs 2.25 Cr/yr): Exclusive cohort, full capacity, co-creator role, policy access
  - Elite Member (Rs 1.35 Cr/yr): Shared cohort, full capacity, leadership forums
  - Growth Member (Rs 85L/yr): Smaller capacity, L3 priority + observer slots in L1/L2
- **Launch sequence** (per your commercial logic): L2 Execution flagship Q2 → L1 Enterprise + L3 Champions Q3 → Stack bundle as "M&A Capability Building Stack" thereafter
