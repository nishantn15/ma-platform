## The Concept

A tiered membership platform combining five integrated modules:

1. **Corporate Strategy Leadership** — CXO roundtables, cross-industry cohorts
2. **Capability Building** — diagnostics, workshops, playbooks
3. **Target Sourcing Intelligence** — curated deal pipeline
4. **Post-Merger Integration** — Day 1 to Year 1 support
5. **Policy & Governance Influence** — NITI Aayog, MCA, CCI engagement

## Phasing

- **Phase 1** — Leadership Institute (5-day exec course + year-long engagement)
- **Phase 2** — Full platform (dashboards, deal sourcing, integration tracking)

## Status

Concept stage. Founding cohort in formation. Prototypes & research are working drafts.
