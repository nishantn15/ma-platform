# Mid-Market M&A Leadership Platform - Detailed Summary

## Document: Platform Narrative - 6 Pager

---

## Page 1: Vision & Rationale

### The Problem
- India's growth story is at an **inflection point** - mid-market firms (INR 250-2,500 crore revenue) are becoming the true engine of consolidation, innovation, and global expansion
- **Persistent gaps hinder value creation:**
  - Weak strategy-to-M&A linkage
  - Limited corporate development maturity
  - Weak target sourcing
  - High post-merger failure rates
  - Fragmented regulatory alignment

### The Solution
Launch the **Mid-Market M&A Leadership Platform** - a national ecosystem that integrates:
1. **Corporate strategy leadership forums** (CXO & Corp Dev peer engagement)
2. **Capability building** for corporate development teams (diagnostics + training + playbooks)
3. **Platform intelligence & curated target sourcing** (daily monitoring, weekly refreshes, target reports)
4. **Post-merger integration support** (monitoring, trackers & lessons)
5. **Policy visibility & alignment** with NITI Aayog and CCI

### Positioning Statement
> India's first ecosystem that spans M&A strategy, capability building, curated target sourcing, and post-merger integration support - ensuring acquisitions create sustainable value.

## Page 4: Partnership Model & Pricing

| Tier | Annual Fee | Key Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform Partner** | INR 2.25 Cr/year | Exclusive (1 per industry cohort). Co-creator role. Full access across all modules. Direct participation in government policy forums |
| **Elite Member** | INR 1.35 Cr/year | Premium access. Leadership forums, diagnostics, sourcing, PMI (1 deal annually). Branding & media presence in select outputs |
| **Growth Member** | INR 85L/year | Entry-level access. Insights, newsletters, 1 integration feature annually. Limited forum access. On-ramp to higher tiers |

## Page 5: ROI Justification

| Tier | Fee | ROI Argument |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform Partner** (INR 2.25 Cr) | Replaces INR 5-7 Cr annual spend on consultants. Exclusivity + policy-shaping. **One successful deal = 20-50x ROI** |
| **Elite Member** (INR 1.35 Cr) | Equivalent to 3-4 month consulting retainer, but delivers across 12 months. **One PMI success alone protects INR 20-50 Cr value** |
| **Growth Member** (INR 85L) | Insights + integration feature worth INR 1-1.5 Cr in standalone consulting. Affordable entry into the ecosystem |
