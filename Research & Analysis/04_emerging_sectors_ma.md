# India Mid-Market M&A: Emerging Sector Consolidation Trends (2023-2026)

*Research for Mid-Market M&A Leadership Platform | Target: INR 250-2,500 Crore Revenue Companies*

---

## Executive Summary

India's M&A deal value surged 42% to reach **$113 billion in 2025**, with mid-market acquirers (up to $1 billion revenue) accounting for nearly **50% of all M&A activity**. The Power/Renewable sector led with **US$8.5 billion in H1 2025 alone** (80% from renewables), followed by healthcare/life sciences at **$2.5 billion across 46 deals**, and technology/TMT with **420+ deals by volume**.

---

## 1. Hydrogen Sector M&A in India

### Policy Framework: National Green Hydrogen Mission
- Approved January 2023 with an initial outlay of **INR 19,744 crore** till FY 2029-30
- Target: **5 million metric tonnes per annum (MMTPA)** green hydrogen production by 2030
- **19 companies** allocated cumulative annual production capacity of **862,000 tonnes**
- **15 firms** awarded **3,000 MW** annual electrolyzer manufacturing capacity

### Key Investment Deals

| Company | Investment | Details |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Adani New Industries Ltd | US$50 billion (decade) | 1 MMTPA green hydrogen by 2030; partnered with TotalEnergies |
| Reliance Industries | US$10 billion (3 years) | Transitioning grey to green; pilot electrolyzer plant targeting <$1/kg |
| ReNew Energy | INR 26,400 crore | Green hydrogen project in Karnataka, 0.22 MMTPA |
| ACME Cleantech | INR 27,000 crore | Green hydrogen facility, 1.10 MMTPA |
| NTPC Green Energy | US$21.6 billion | First green hydrogen hub in Andhra Pradesh, 20 GW renewable + 1,500 TPD |

### Electrolyzer Manufacturing
- **L&T** (partnership with France's McPhy): First factory, 150-200 MW capacity
- **Reliance**: Exclusive rights to produce Nel ASA's alkaline technology in India
- India's green hydrogen market projected to reach **USD 12.98 billion by 2032** (CAGR 11.11%)

### Mid-Market M&A Opportunity
- Consolidation of electrolyzer component manufacturers
- Acquisition of green hydrogen project developers by larger industrial groups
- Cross-border technology transfer deals
- Supply chain companies (membranes, balance-of-plant) in INR 250-2,500 crore range

## 2. Solar Energy M&A in India

### Market Context
Renewable energy contributed ~**80% of the Power sector's US$8.5 billion** M&A value in H1 2025 -- the single largest M&A vertical in India.

### Major Deals (2024-2025)

| Deal | Value | Details |
|------|-------|---------|
| **JSW Neo Energy acquires O2 Power** | **INR 12,468 crore** (~$1.5B) | 4.7 GW platform; backed by EQT and Temasek |
| ONGC NTPC Green acquires Ayana Renewable Power | Undisclosed | 100% equity; ~4,112 MW portfolio |
| Brookfield sells 1.6 GW portfolio to Gentari Renewables | Undisclosed | Solar and wind assets |
| BluPine Energy acquires from Acme Group | Undisclosed | 369 MW solar capacity |
| Havells invests in Goldi Solar | INR 600 crore | Surat-based PV module manufacturer |
| Premier Energies JV with SAS (Taiwan) | Undisclosed | 74:26 JV for 2 GW solar wafer manufacturing |

### JSW-O2 Power Deal Deep Dive
- JSW's largest-ever acquisition, bringing pro forma capacity to **12,212 MW**
- Additional **INR 13,500 crore** investment planned to scale to full 4,696 MW by June 2027
- Target annual EBITDA of **INR 3,750 crore**

### Market Leaders
- **Adani Green Energy**: 17.6% of 2024 utility-scale capacity additions (2.6 GW)
- **ReNew**: INR 31,500 crore investment plan for 40 GW projects + 6 GW manufacturing
- **Tata Power Solar**: Expanding manufacturing and project development

### Mid-Market M&A Opportunity
- **Platform aggregation**: Solar IPPs with 200-500 MW portfolios (INR 500-2,000 crore EV range)
- **Manufacturing consolidation**: Module and cell manufacturers in PLI ecosystem
- **EPC companies**: Smaller solar EPC firms being acquired for execution capability

## 3. Electric Vehicle (EV) M&A in India

### Market Scale
- India EV market: **USD 5.22 billion (2024)**, projected **USD 23.52 billion by 2030** (CAGR 28.52%)
- EV battery market: **USD 2,715 million (2025)** growing to **USD 15,898 million by 2034** (CAGR 21.70%)

### Key Deals and Strategic Moves

| Deal/Event | Value | Details |
|------------|-------|---------|
| Ather Energy IPO | INR 4,500 crore (~$536M) | Listed on NSE/BSE; valuation ~$2.5B |
| Ola Electric battery gigafactory | $100 million (Phase 1) | Tamil Nadu; indigenous lithium-ion cells |
| Reliance acquires Lithium Werks assets | Undisclosed | Lithium battery technology for EV supply chain |
| Yuma Energy acquires Grinntechn Motors | Undisclosed | Battery-as-a-Service ecosystem |
| JBM Auto + Macquarie's Vertelo | Undisclosed | EV financing + fleet management |

### Mid-Market M&A Opportunity
- **Component suppliers**: Battery management systems, power electronics, motor manufacturers (INR 250-1,000 crore)
- **Charging infrastructure**: Statiq, Charge Zone, Ather Grid as acquisition targets
- **Two/three-wheeler OEMs**: Sub-scale players ripe for consolidation
- **Battery recycling**: Emerging sub-sector ready for rollup
- **EV financing platforms**: Growing niche with M&A potential

## 4. Healthcare M&A in India

### Deal Volume & Value
- **Q3 2025**: 72 deals, up 28% in volume; values surged **166% to USD 3.5 billion** (highest quarterly level)
- **FY 2025-26**: USD 3.5 billion across 46 M&A deals in healthcare/life sciences

### Major Hospital Chain Deals

| Deal | Value | Details |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Manipal Hospitals acquires Sahyadri Hospitals** | **USD 700M (~INR 6,000 crore)** | Temasek-backed Manipal buys from OTPP |
| **KKR acquires 54% of Healthcare Global (HCG)** | **USD 400M** | Controlling stake in oncology chain |
| **Aster DM + Quality Care India** | ~USD 94M | Combined: 38 hospitals, 10,150+ beds |
| **BPEA EQT takes majority in Indira IVF** | **>USD 1 billion valuation** | IVF chain |
| Apollo Hospitals acquires 31% of Apollo Health & Lifestyle | INR 1,254 crore | Consolidating subsidiary |
| CK Birla Fertility acquires BabyScience IVF chain | INR 500 crore | Target: 100 clinics by FY28 |

### Diagnostics Chain Consolidation
- Metropolis acquires Core Diagnostics: INR 246.8 crore
- Metropolis acquires Dr Ahujas' Pathology: INR 35 crore
- Thyrocare acquires Polo Labs pathology unit
- Organized players aggressively acquiring smaller labs

### Mid-Market M&A Opportunity
- **Regional hospital chains** (100-500 beds, INR 300-1,500 crore revenue) -- primary targets
- **Single-specialty chains** (IVF, oncology, ophthalmology, dialysis) -- premium valuations
- **Diagnostics rollup**: Organized players acquiring smaller labs
- **Digital health**: Telemedicine startups being acquired
- **CDMO/CMO**: Pharma outsourcing in mid-market range

## 6. Government Policy Driving M&A

### PLI Schemes

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total PLI outlay | INR 1.97 lakh crore across 13 sectors |
| Realized investments (by 2025) | INR 1.76 lakh crore |
| Approved applications | 806 |
| Employment generated | 12 lakh+ |

### How PLI Creates Mid-Market M&A
- **Scale requirements** push smaller firms to merge or be acquired
- **Technology gaps** drive acqui-hires and technology acquisitions
- PLI beneficiaries become **attractive acquisition targets**
- Ecosystem development across **Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities** creates local targets

### Other Policy Drivers
- **Insurance FDI** hike to 100% could trigger major consolidation
- **Satellite/Space**: New FDI rules creating M&A zone
- **Defense manufacturing**: FDI liberalization driving JVs to full acquisitions
- **Startup India**: 71 startup M&As in 2024; projected **58% surge in 2025**
- CCI Deal Value Threshold at INR 2,000 crore makes regulatory advisory essential

## Key Implications for the Platform

1. **INR 250-2,500 Crore is the sweet spot** -- ~50% of India's M&A activity (Bain)
2. **Healthcare is the most active** mid-market M&A sector -- highest deal frequency, most fragmented
3. **Renewable Energy** has the largest deal values -- platform aggregation accelerating
4. **EV/E-Mobility** has the fastest growth trajectory -- early consolidation phase
5. **Semiconductors/Electronics** -- massive government-backed investment creating future M&A
6. **Green Hydrogen** -- pre-M&A investment phase; advisory opportunity from 2027+
