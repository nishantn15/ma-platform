## Slide 2: The Gap in India's Mid-Market

**70% of M&A deals fail to create expected value**

Five key gaps identified:
1. **Strategy Gap** -- M&A treated as opportunistic, not a strategic capability
2. **Capability Gap** -- Limited corporate development team maturity across firms
3. **Sourcing Gap** -- Weak target identification and proprietary pipeline building
4. **Execution Gap** -- High post-merger failure driven by poor integration readiness
5. **Regulatory Gap** -- Fragmented alignment across CCI, MCA, and FEMA requirements
