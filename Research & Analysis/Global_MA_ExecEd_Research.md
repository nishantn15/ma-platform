## Program Deep Dives

### Harvard HBS
- Faculty: Guhan Subramanian (HBS star), David Fubini, Stuart Gilson
- Also offers: **M&A Negotiation Practicum** -- 4-day role-play with 5 competing companies ($3,440)
- Cohort: 47% N. America, 19% Europe, 9% Asia Pacific

### Stanford GSB
- **Standout:** Week-long proprietary deal simulation spanning entire program
- Teams apply daily concepts to simulated merger; present at week's end
- Pre-work: Online "Financial Valuation and Strategy" course

### Chicago Booth
- **Standout:** Participants build customizable M&A playbook for their organization
- Pre-work: 8-12 hours + 6 online Financial Accounting modules

## Key Design Recommendations for Our Program

### Must-Have Experiential Elements
- Stanford-style week-long deal simulation (adapted for Indian mid-market)
- Harvard-style multi-party negotiation role-play
- Deloitte-style integration war room simulation
- Chicago Booth-style customizable M&A playbook

### India-Specific Differentiation (No Global Program Covers)
- Indian regulatory: SEBI/CCI/NCLT/FEMA modules
- Family business / promoter-led deal dynamics
- Cross-border India inbound/outbound case studies
- Indian PE/VC deal structuring patterns

### Credentialing Model
- Tiered: Certificate of Completion (3-5 day) → "Certified Corporate Development Professional" (additional modules + documented deal experience)
- Modeled on CMAS/CM&AA approach
