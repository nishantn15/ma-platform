# IIM Ahmedabad + Other Indian Institutes - Executive Education Research
**Date:** April 2026 | **Purpose:** Benchmarking for M&A Leadership Institute

## IIM Ahmedabad M&A Programme

### Mergers, Acquisitions & Restructuring Programme
- **Duration:** 6 days residential at IIMA campus
- **Next Offering:** February 15-20, 2027
- **Fee:** INR 1,91,250 + 18% GST
- **Faculty Chair:** Prof. Sobhesh Agarwalla (+ 3 additional IIMA faculty)
- **Target:** Top management in growth strategy, senior execs in strategy/biz dev/finance
- **Curriculum (4 Key Questions):**
  - **WHAT?** Transaction types (mergers, acquisitions, demergers, spin-offs, divestitures)
  - **WHY?** Drivers (value creation, synergies, market expansion)
  - **WHEN?** Industry/org lifecycle timing for M&A vs. organic growth
  - **HOW?** Pre-acquisition planning, deal execution, post-acquisition integration, regulatory/tax
  - **HOW NOT?** Industry-specific pitfall mitigation
- **Pedagogy:** Faculty-led discussions, peer learning, team projects, case studies, end-of-course evaluation
- **Online version:** 27-day e-learning at INR 1,12,000

## IIM Calcutta LEAD Programme (Best CXO Benchmark)
- 12 months, 4 campus modules of 5-6 days each + online between
- **LEAD Framework:** LEARN → ENHANCE → ASPIRE → DELIVER
- Includes "CEO Looks Back" sessions with current/past CEOs
- Personalized executive coaching + Learning Diary

## Key Gaps Identified (Our Opportunity)
1. **No Indian institute offers a dedicated year-long M&A programme**
2. IIMA's M&A program is 6 days at ~INR 2.25L (with GST) -- short-duration only
3. No peer-learning community model for M&A practitioners
4. No post-program support or ongoing advisory
5. Multi-module blended format is the trend but nobody applies it to M&A specifically
