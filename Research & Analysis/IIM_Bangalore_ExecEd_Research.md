# IIM Bangalore Executive Education - Research
**Date:** April 2026 | **Purpose:** Benchmarking for M&A Leadership Institute

## Key M&A Program

### Corporate Restructuring, Mergers & Acquisitions, Buyouts and Divestitures
- **Duration:** 5 days residential at IIMB campus (typically August)
- **Fee:** INR 1,52,500 (residential) / INR 1,25,000 (non-residential) + GST
- **Faculty:** Dr. Ashok Thampy (Finance Professor) + Mr. Pratap Giri S (practitioner/adjunct with PE, M&A deal experience)
- **Curriculum:** Full deal lifecycle -- restructuring strategy, M&A motivations, hostile bids, cross-border M&A, merger process/scheme of arrangement, SEBI/company law, takeover code, valuation, LBOs, term sheets, due diligence, reps & warranties, investment banking roles
- **Pedagogy:** Case studies + role plays + group assignments
- **Source:** https://eep.iimb.ac.in/course/corporate-restructuring-mergers-acquisitions-buyouts-and-divestitures/
