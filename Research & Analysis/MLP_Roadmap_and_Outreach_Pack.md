## Slide 1: 30-Day Roadmap to First Founding Partner

**Framework: Validate -> Engage -> Convert**

### WEEK 1-2: VALIDATE (Discovery & Outreach)
- Finalise target list across 4 buckets
- Send 20 outreach messages (LinkedIn + Email)
- Run 8-12 discovery conversations (30 min each)
- Use meeting guide -- diagnose, don't pitch
- Capture: which pain point resonates most?
- Log all replies and signals in tracker

### WEEK 3-4: ENGAGE (Deepen & Convert)
- Identify top 3-4 warmest prospects
- Send sector-specific 1-page founding note
- Run second meetings -- bring the deck
- Ask: "Would you join as a Founding Partner?"
- Handle objections (pilot / references / terms)
- Aim for 1 verbal commitment by Week 4

### MONTH 2: BUILD (Anchor & Activate)
- Design Founding Roundtable (10-15 CXOs)
- Invite as founding participants -- not paying
- Confirm IIT Roorkee MoU in writing
- Get NITI Aayog engagement documented
- Continue outreach -- target 3 more conversations
- Close first Platform Partner formally

### MONTH 3: CLOSE (Close & Launch)
- Deliver Founding Roundtable event
- Use event to convert 1-2 more partners
- Formalise contracts -- 1-year minimum
- Announce founding cohort (with consent)
- Design Year 1 programming calendar
- Begin Module 1 delivery with founding cohort

### Milestones
- **Day 7:** 10 outreach messages sent
- **Day 14:** 8 conversations done
- **Day 21:** 3 prospects in serious discussion
- **Day 30:** First verbal commitment secured
- **Month 3:** First partner signed

## Slide 2: Outreach Templates

**Key principle:** You are inviting a perspective, not pitching a product. Lead with curiosity, not credentials.

### LinkedIn InMail / Connection Note
*300 chars max for connection note; 1,900 for InMail*

**Subject:** M&A Capability Platform -- Brief Conversation?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm developing India's first M&A Capability & Execution Platform, built for corporate development leaders and acquisitive firms in the mid-market space.
>
> Before we build anything, I'm speaking with a small number of senior M&A leaders to understand where the real capability gaps are.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation? No pitch -- just a genuine diagnostic.
>
> -- TK

**Tips:** Personalise line 1: reference their recent deal, sector, or a shared connection. Do not attach anything in the first message.

### WhatsApp (Warm Contact Only)
*Use only if you have the number through a mutual contact or prior meeting*

> Hi [Name], TK here.
>
> I'm building India's first M&A capability and execution platform -- for corporate development teams and acquisitive leaders who want to make M&A a repeatable strength, not a one-off event.
>
> Speaking with a small group of senior leaders before we formalise anything.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes for a quick call this week?

**Tips:** Keep it short. No attachments in the first message. If they reply positively, follow up with the one-pager PDF.

*Aim for under 150 words. Subject line is everything -- test 2 variants.*

**Tips:** BCC yourself to track replies. Follow up once after 5 working days if no reply. Do not attach the deck to the first email -- offer to share materials if they express interest.

## Slide 3: First Meeting Discussion Guide

**30 minutes | Diagnose, don't pitch**

### OPEN (0-5 min) -- Set the Context
- "Thanks for making time. I'll keep this to 30 minutes."
- "I'm building a platform -- but before I build, I want to understand the real gaps from people doing this work."
- "So today I'd like to ask you some questions, not give you a pitch."
- "Would that work for you?"

### EXPLORE (5-22 min) -- 10 Diagnostic Questions
1. How active is your firm on acquisitions in the next 2-3 years?
2. Where is the biggest gap -- sourcing, evaluation, integration, or governance?
3. What's typically underbuilt internally when a deal is running?
4. Are lessons from past deals being captured and reused?
5. Do you have a confidential peer forum for M&A decision-making?
6. What would be most useful -- roundtables, diagnostics, playbooks, or deal flow?
7. Who in your firm would sponsor participation in something like this?
8. Would you see this as a membership, program, or advisory relationship?
9. What would make this clearly worth your time?
10. Would you be open to helping shape the founding group?

### CLOSE (22-30 min) -- Confirm Next Step
- Summarise what you heard: "Based on what you've shared, [X] seems to be the sharpest pain point."
- "I'll send you a one-page note -- specifically written for [sector] -- within 24 hours."
- "I'd like a second conversation with whoever else needs to be in the room."
- "Is there anyone else you'd recommend I speak with?"
- **Never leave without a named next action and a date.**

> **Remember:** Your goal in this meeting is NOT to close a deal. It is to earn the right to a second, more specific conversation.
