Signature opener · M1 Foundation

"The engine we couldn't build, and the rail we did"

ISRO's cryogenic engine flew after 22 years of failing forward; the Kaveri jet engine, started earlier, still hasn't - because an easy "buy" option quietly killed the will to build. Then UPI: a rail India built and gave the world. The lesson for the room: capability cannot be imported - the artifact is not the capability. Absorbing it is a three-to-five-year act of leadership. That is integration.

This case runs at all three depths - board dilemma (L1), deep-end simulation (L2/L3).
